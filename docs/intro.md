## Introduction

The *Śrī Vaiṣṇava Matābja Bhāskara* is a significant theological and
philosophical text within the Śrī Vaiṣṇava and broader Rāmānandī
traditions. Structured as a series of responses to doctrinal questions,
the work addresses fundamental issues concerning reality (*tattva*),
means (*upāya*), and attainment (*puruṣārtha*), with a sustained focus
on devotion to Lord Rāma.

This digital edition is intended as a **research-oriented presentation**
of the text. It aims to preserve the integrity of the Sanskrit source
while providing careful translations and, where appropriate, structured
commentary. The site is organized according to the text’s internal
division into replies (*uttara*) and their constituent subsections.

### Method and Scope

The materials presented here are produced through a semi-automated
pipeline involving:

- OCR extraction from scanned sources  
- normalization and correction of Sanskrit text  
- verse-aware translation workflows  
- structured output suitable for both reading and citation  

All intermediate artifacts are reproducible from source materials.
Only curated textual and structural outputs are presented on this site.

### Organization of the Site

- The **Table of Contents** follows the traditional division of the work.
- Each subsection corresponds to a conceptual unit of the text.
- Individual verses will appear as discrete pages, allowing precise
  reference and future expansion with commentary.

### Status

This site is under active development. Translations and annotations will
be refined iteratively, and additional materials may be added as the
project progresses.
