# Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
SOURCE_PAGES: page_0081.txt

=== मूलपाठः ===


देहादेरप्यपूर्वी विविदितविविधस्तप्मरियस्तत्सहायः । नित्यो जीवस्तृतीयेन तु खलु पदतः प्रोच्यते  
स्वप्रकाशो जिज्ञासूनां सदेव्थ॑ णुभनतिसुमते शास्तवित्सज्जनानाम् ।।१९।।

dehāderapypūrvī vividitavividhastapmariyastatsahāyaḥ । nityo jīvastṛtīyena tu khalu padataḥ procyate  
svaprakāśo jijñāsūnāṃ sadevtha ṇubhanatisumate śāstavitsajjanānām ।।19।।

देहाद् (dehād) deha (noun), ablative singular masculine  
एर् (er) er (pronoun), genitive plural masculine  
अपि (api) api (indeclinable, particle)  
पूर्वी (pūrvī) pūrvī (adjective), nominative singular feminine  
विविदित (vividita) vivid (past passive participle, adjective), nominative singular masculine  
विविध (vividh) vividh (adjective), accusative plural masculine  
स्तप्मरिय (stapmariya) stapmariya (noun, compound tatpuruṣa?), nominative plural masculine  
तत् (tat) tat (pronoun), genitive singular neuter  
सहायः (sahāyaḥ) sahāya (noun), nominative singular masculine  
नित्यः (nityaḥ) nitya (adjective), nominative singular masculine  
जीवः (jīvaḥ) jīva (noun), nominative singular masculine  
तृतीयेन (tṛtīyena) tṛtīya (ordinal adjective), instrumental singular masculine  
तु (tu) tu (indeclinable, particle)  
खलु (khalu) khalu (indeclinable, particle)  
पदतः (padataḥ) pada (noun), ablative singular masculine  
प्रोच्यते (procyate) √uc (verb root), passive present indicative 3rd singular  
स्वप्रकाशः (svaprakāśaḥ) svaprakāśa (noun), nominative singular masculine  
जिज्ञासूनाम् (jijñāsūnām) jijñāsū (noun, derived verbal noun), genitive plural masculine  
सदेव्थ॑ (sad evtha) saḥ + eva + atha (indeclinables/particles; evtha likely a scribal variant of eva)  
णुभनतिसुमते (ṇubhanatisumate) णुभनतिसुमते (appears to be a compound or a corrupted word; could be पुनःभनतिसुमते "punar-bhanati sumate"? Possibly a scribal or printing error)  
शास्तवित्सज्जनानाम् (śāstavitsajjanānām) śāstva-vit-sajjanānām (compound noun, genitive plural masculine)  

Literal translation (with notes):  
"From the body indeed predecessors (or elders) also, well-known and various tapasvī who are their helpers. Eternally the soul by the third (instrumental) indeed from the foot is spoken (procyate - passive).  
Self-luminous to the inquisitive indeed, of good-minded, learned sages (śāstva-vit-sajjanānām gen. pl.) ... (remaining part unclear due to corrupted word)."

Smooth English translation:  
"Even the predecessors from the body, known and various ascetics who are their helpers. The eternal soul is, indeed, by the third limb spoken from the foot.  
The self-luminous one is surely for the inquisitive, truly the learned companions of good intellect..."  

(Note: The second half contains unclear or corrupted words making exact translation difficult.)
```
