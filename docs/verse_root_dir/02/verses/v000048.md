## Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
=== मूलपाठः ===
```

---

## Transliteration (IAST)

```text
अस्मीत्यनेन चोपायस्वीकारः प्रतिपाद्यते ।
समाप्त्यर्थतिशब्देन तूपायानन्यतोच्यते ।।४८।।
```

---

## Literal Translation

अस्मीत्यनेन (asmītyanena) — अस्मीत्यन् (asmīt yan), asmī (pronoun "I") + ति (conjunct suffix) + अन (enclitic particle) + एन (ablative singular case of अयन् - here suffix indicating 'by this') [compound of pronoun + particle + instrumental singular]
चोपायस्वीकारः (copāyasvīkāraḥ) — च (ca, indeclinable conjunction "and") + उपायस्वीकारः (upāyasvīkāraḥ), compound tatpuruṣa: उपाय (upāya, noun instrumental singular "means") + स्वीकारः (svīkāraḥ, noun nominative singular masc. "acceptance") [compound]
प्रतिपाद्यते (pratipādyate) — verb root पद् (pād), verbal participle (present passive indicative 3rd person singular middle voice) "is established, is expounded"
समाप्त्यर्थतिशब्देन (samāptyarthatiśabden) — compound bahuvrīhi: समाप्ति (samāpti, noun instrumental singular "completion") + अर्थ (artha, noun genitive singular "meaning") + ति (ti, suffix) + शब्देन (śabden, noun instrumental singular "word") — "by a word exceeding the meaning of completion"
तूपायानन्यतोच्यते (tūpāyān anyatocyate) — तूपायान् (tūpāyān, accusative plural from उपाय (upāya, "means")) + अन्यत (anyat, accusative singular masc. "another") + उच्यते (ucyate, verb root उच्, passive indicative 3rd singular present middle voice "is said") — "is said another (different) from the means"

---

## Smooth Translation

"अस्मीत्यनेन चोपायस्वीकारः प्रतिपाद्यते"  
"By this (asmītyanena) and means acceptance (upāyasvīkāraḥ), it is established (pratipādyate)."
