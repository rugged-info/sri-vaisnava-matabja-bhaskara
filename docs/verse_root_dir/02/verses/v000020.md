## Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
=== मूलपाठः ===
```

---

## Transliteration (IAST)

```text
मवाच्योऽहं रवाच्याय शेषभूतोऽस्मि सर्वदा ।
इतीत्थमेव बोध्यो ज्ञेवक्यार्थस्तद्विवित्सया ।।२०।।
```

---

## Literal Translation

मवाच्यः (mavācyaḥ), म्-आ-इ-च्-य-ः, (tatpuruṣa compound ‘mama + ācyā’?), nominative singular masculine, ‘to be spoken by me’
अहम् (aham), अहम्, nominative singular first person pronoun, ‘I’
रवाच्याय (ravācyāya), रवाच्याय, dative singular masculine, tatpuruṣa compound ‘rava + ācyāya’, ‘to be spoken by Rava’
शेषभूता (śeṣabhūtā), शेषभूत-आ, nominative singular feminine, bahuvrīhi/karma tatpuruṣa?, ‘remaining being’
अस्मि (asmi), अस्मि, first person singular present indicative active verb ‘to be’, ‘I am’
सर्वदा (sarvadā), सर्वदा, indeclinable adverb, ‘always’

---

## Smooth Translation

इति (iti), इति, particle, ‘thus’
इदम् (idam), इदम् (implied), demonstrative pronoun neuter singular, ‘this’
इदम् converted to ‘इति’ as quotation marker, treating as indeclinable particle here
अमेव (ameva), (actually इव) parsed as इव (iva) indeclinable particle ‘like, indeed’
बोध्यो (bodhyo), बोध्य-उ, nominative singular masculine, gerundive, ‘to be known/understood’
ज्ञेवक्यार्थ (jñevakyārtha), ज्ञ + इव + अर्थ, compound indicating ‘meaning to be known’
तत् (tat), तत्, nominative singular neuter, demonstrative pronoun ‘that’
विवित्सया (tadvivitsayā), तत् + वित्सा (vivitsā), instrumental singular feminine, tatpuruṣa or bahuvrīhi, ‘by the one who understands/distinguishes that’
