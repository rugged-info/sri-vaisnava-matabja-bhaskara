## Smooth Translation

योगः प्रकीर्तितः इति। (Yogaḥ prakīrtitaḥ iti.)  
"Yoga is declared." (Quotation)  
उत्क्तत्वेत्थं (Uktatvetthaṃ) — "Because it has been stated here" (locative of abstract noun 'uktatva' + demonstrative accusative ettham)  
तारकार्थ (tārakārtha) — "for the purpose of the stars," (compounded noun)  
तु (tu) — "but"  
द्रयार्थः प्रतिपाद्यते (drayārthaḥ pratipādyate) — "a notion/fact to be established"  
विमत्सराः (vimatsarāḥ) — "those who do not envy" (plural nominative)  
प्रपश्यन्तु (prapaśyantu) — "may see" (imperative plural)  
प्रगृह्णन्तु (pragṛhṇantu) — "may understand" (imperative plural)  
अवयन्तु (avayantu) — "may comprehend/remove" (imperative plural)  
च (ca) — "and"
