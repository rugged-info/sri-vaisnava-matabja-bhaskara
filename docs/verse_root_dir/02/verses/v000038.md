## Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
=== मूलपाठः ===
```

---

## Transliteration (IAST)

```text
चरणावित्यनेनैव वात्सल्यादिकसीतयोः ।
विलक्षणस्य दिव्यस्य विग्रहस्याश्रयस्य च ।। ३८ ।।
```

---

## Literal Translation

चरणाविति (caraṇāviti), caraṇā + iti (indeclinable, quotation particle)
नेन (nena), naḥ, 5th case instrumental singular pronoun
ऐव (aiva), aiva (indeclinable emphatic particle)
वात्सल्यादि (vātsalyādi), vātsalya + ādi, noun compound (bahuvrīhi), genitive singular masculine
सीतयः (sītayaḥ), sīta, noun plural nominative masculine
विलक्षणस्य (vilakṣanasya), vilakṣaṇa, adjective/noun genitive singular masculine
दिव्यस्य (divyasya), divya, adjective genitive singular masculine
विग्रहस्य (vigrahasya), vigraha, noun genitive singular masculine
आश्रयस्य (āśrayasya), āśraya, noun genitive singular masculine
च (ca), ca (indeclinable conjunction)

---

## Smooth Translation

"caraṇāviti" (literally "thus called 'caraṇa'") + "nena eva" (by this only) + "vātsalyādi sītayaḥ" (the coldness of kindness, etc.) +
"vilakṣanasya divyasya vigrahasya āśrayasya ca" (and the shelter of the distinctive divine form).
