## Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
=== मूलपाठः ===
```

---

## Transliteration (IAST)

```text
प्रोक्ता वत्सक! मन्तरत्रविवृतिः सन्मानसाभीष्टदं  
सद्वेद्यं सकृदित्यवेहि चरमं निर्णीतवाक्यार्थकम् ।  
रामीयं हि तदीयमन्तरनिरतैरुद्वोधनीयं परं  
द्ात्रिंशत्प्रमिताक्षरं मनुपदं द्रयरद्धं जगद्विश्रुतम् ।।४५।।
```

## Smooth Translation

Literal translation:  
"O dear one, the explanation here of the mantra spoken, (which is) a gift desired with respectful mind,  
(is) venerable, once thus bring the supreme decided meaning of sentence.  
Indeed, delightful is that internal inspiring one engaged in it, the supreme  
thirty-measured syllable expression, valuable, world-famous."
