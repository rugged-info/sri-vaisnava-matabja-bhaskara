# Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
SOURCE_PAGES: page_0092.txt

=== मूलपाठः ===


वाक्यार्थः प्राप्यसम्बन्धिस्वरूपाभिनिरूपणम् ।।३१।।  

vākyārthaḥ prāpyasambandhisvarūpābhinirūpaṇam ।।31।।  

वाक्यार्थः (vākārthaḥ), वाक् (vāka, m.), अर्थ (artha, m.); द्विवचनः, प्रथमतः पुनरुत्तमः, तत्पुरुषसमासः  
प्राप्यसंबन्धिस्वरूपाभिनिरूपणम् (prāpyasambandhisvarūpābhinirūpaṇam), प्राप्य (prāpya, avy.), सम्बन्धि (sambandhi, m.), स्वरूप (svarūpa, n.), अभिनिरूपणम् (abhinirūpaṇam, n.); एकवचनम्, कृत्वा कृदन्तसमासः (बहुव्रीहि + तत्पुरुष)

Literal translation:  
vākārthaḥ (the meaning of speech), prāpyasambandhi (related to attainment), svarūpa (nature/essence), abhinirūpaṇam (delineation/definition); (the) delineation of the nature connected with the attainment of the meaning of speech.

Smooth English translation:  
The delineation of the nature connected with attaining the meaning of speech.
```
