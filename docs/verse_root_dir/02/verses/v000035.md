## Literal Translation

sanmanuṣyamano (san-manuṣya-mano), san (indeclinable, prefix, intensive) + manuṣya (noun, masc. sg. nom., "human") + mano (noun, masc. sg. nom., "mind") - tatpuruṣa compound meaning "good/honest human mind"  
yathā (indeclinable, particle, "as", "like")  
iti (indeclinable, particle, "thus", "so")  
(1.2.5) (reference note, not parsed)  
śrīrāmāyaṇokteḥ (śrīrāmāyaṇa + ukteḥ), śrīrāmāyaṇa (noun, masc. sg. loc. "in the Rāmāyaṇa") + ukteḥ (participle, masc. sg. loc. of √vad "said", literally "said in") - tatpuruṣa compound "in the Rāmāyaṇa said"  
śrīrāmadrayam-antrā-mad-ubhutatamam (compound: śrīrāma + adrayam + antram + ad + ubhutatamam)  
- śrīrāma (noun, masc., name of Rāma)  
- adrayam (noun, neut. sg. acc., "jewel")  
- antram (noun, neut. sg. acc., "mantra")  
- ad (prefix intensifier)  
- ubhutatamam (superlative adjective, neut. sg. acc., "most wonderful")  
Combined as tatpuruṣa and bahuvrīhi compound, meaning "the most wonderful mantra jewel of Śrī Rāma"  
vākyadvayaṃ (vākyadvaya) vākyam (noun, neut. sg. acc. "sentence") + dvayaṃ (noun, neut. sg. acc., "pair") - bahuvrīhi compound, "a pair of sentences"  
ṣaṭpadaṃ (ṣaṭ + padaṃ), ṣaṭ (numeral "six") + padaṃ (noun, neut. sg. acc., "foot", "line")  
bāṇākṣipramitākṣarantu  
- bāṇa (noun, masc. nom. pl. "arrows")  
- akṣipra (adjective "swift")  
- mitākṣara (noun, masc. nom. pl., "letters")  
- anta (suffix, verbal or nominal end)  
- tu (indeclinable, conjunction "but")  
Possibly compound or phrase meaning "Let the swift arrows, the measured letters"  
khalu (indeclinable, particle "indeed", "really")  
viddhi (verb imperative 2nd sg. of √vid "know")  
tvaṃ (pronoun 2nd sg. nom. "you")  
daśārthāvvitam (daśa + artha + avvitam)  
- daśa (numeral "ten")  
- artha (noun, masc. sg. acc. "meaning")  
- avvitam (adjective "full", "completely endowed")  
Meaning "full of ten meanings"  
yuktaṃ (adjective, neut. sg. acc., "proper", "appropriate")  
tattipadaistu (tat + tipadai + tu)  
- tat (pronoun, neut. sg. nom. "that")  
- tipadaiḥ (noun, masc. pl. instr., "three feet", i.e., tripāda)  
- tu (indeclinable "but")  
Meaning "but that with three feet"  
tatra (indeclinable, adv. "there")  
sumate (su + mate)  
- su (prefix "good")  
- mate (noun, masc. sg. loc. "mind")  
Meaning "in the good mind", "according to the wise"  
pūrva (adjective, neut. sg. acc., "former", "previous", "east")  
śubhasyāspadaṃ  
- śubha (adjective, masc. sg. gen. "auspicious")  
- aspadam (noun, neut. sg. acc. "foot", "step")  
Meaning "auspicious foot/step"  
* saṃvitkaraḥ * (saṃvit + karaḥ)  
- saṃvit (noun, fem./masc. nom. sg. "consciousness", "understanding")  
- karaḥ (noun masc., "doer")  
Compound meaning "consciousness-doer", possibly author/editor signature  
vākyam (noun, neut. sg. acc. "sentence")  
pañcadaśākṣaraṃ (pañcadaśa + akṣaraṃ)  
- pañcadaśa (numeral, "fifteen")  
- akṣaraṃ (noun neut. sg. acc. "letter")  
Meaning "fifteen-lettered"  
tadanv (tat + anu) misspelled as tadan + anu  
tadanu - tat + anu (compound particle "thereafter")  
digvarṇāv-makaṃ  
- digvarṇa (noun, masc. pl. gen. "directional letters")  
- āmakaṃ (unclear) possibly a suffix or miswritten  
tṛttaram (numeral "third")
