## Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
=== मूलपाठः ===
```

---

## Transliteration (IAST)

```text
वाक्यार्था मन्तरलस्य त्वथ निर्णीयते बुधः ।।४२।।
```

---

## Literal Translation

वाक्यार्था (vākārthā), vākyārtha, nominative/vocative singular masculine, tatpuruṣa compound  
मन्तरलस्य (mantaralasya), mantarala, genitive singular masculine, tatpuruṣa compound  
त्वथ (tvatha), indeclinable (particle)  
निर्णीयते (nirṇīyate), nirṇī (root nirṇī-), present passive/mediopassive indicative 3rd person singular  
बुधः (budhaḥ), budha, nominative singular masculine, subject

---

## Smooth Translation

"The meaning of the speech of the mantra, thus, is determined by the wise (man)."
