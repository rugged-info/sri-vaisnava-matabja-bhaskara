## Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
=== मूलपाठः ===
```

---

## Transliteration (IAST)

```text
इति प्रतिपदमर्थसङ्ग्रहः।  
अत्रोपायान्तरस्याथी निवृत्तिः प्रतिपाद्यते ।  
सकृदित्येवकारेण तूपायनिरपेक्षता ।।४६ ।।
```

---

## Literal Translation

इति (iti), इति, indeclinable, particle  
प्रतिपदम् (pratipadam), प्रतिपद्, accusative singular neuter  
अर्थसङ्‌ग्रहः (arthasaṅgrahaḥ), अर्थसङ्‌ग्रह, nominative singular masculine, tatpuruṣa compound (अर्थ + सङ्‌ग्रहः)  
अत्र (atra), अत्र, indeclinable, adverb  
उपायान्तरस्य (upāyāntarasya), उपायान्तर, genitive singular masculine, tatpuruṣa compound (उपाय + अन्तर)  
अथी (athī), अथी, nominative singular masculine/feminine (used here as a noun)  
निवृत्तिः (nivṛttiḥ), निवृत्ति, nominative singular feminine  
प्रतिपाद्यते (pratipādyate), प्रतिपाद्यते, verb, present passive indicative, third person singular  
सकृद् (sakṛd), सकृद्, indeclinable, adverb  
इति (iti), इति, indeclinable, particle  
एव (eva), एव, indeclinable, particle  
कारेण (kāreṇa), कारण, instrumental singular masculine  
तूपायनिरपेक्षता (tūpāyanirapekṣatā), तूपाय + निरपेक्षता (compound, bahuvrīhi or tatpuruṣa), nominative singular feminine

---

## Smooth Translation

Thus (इति), of the word (प्रतिपदम्) meaning (अर्थसङ्‌ग्रहः).  
Here (अत्र), of the different (अन्तर) means (उपायान्तरस्य), this (अथी) cessation (निवृत्तिः) is being expounded (प्रतिपाद्यते).  
By the cause (कारेण) “once” (सकृद्) and “thus” (इति एव) the absence (निरपेक्षता) of other means (तूपाय) is (हा) . (Note: "हा" here not directly in text, interpreted from context.)
