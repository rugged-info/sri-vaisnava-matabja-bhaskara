# Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
SOURCE_PAGES: page_0100.txt

=== मूलपाठः ===


विभक्त्त्यायेतिपदतः शेषवृत्तिर्महात्मभिः ।
विरोधिनो निरासस्तु नमः पदेन वर्ण्यते ।।४१।।

vibhaktthyāyetipadataḥ śeṣavṛttirmahātmbhiḥ ।
virodhino nirāsastu namaḥ padena varṇyate ।।41।।

विभक्त्त्यायेतिपदतः (vibhaktthyāyetipadataḥ)  
विभक्त्यु, विभक्तिः (case/number/gender: instr. sing. fem.), आयेतिः (root आय, verb suffix, indeclinable), पदतः (पद, noun, gen. or abl. sing. neuter)  
शेषवृत्तिर्महात्मभिः (śeṣavṛttirmahātmbhiḥ)  
शेषवृत्तिः (noun, nom. sing. masc., tatpuruṣa compound), महात्मभिः (महात्मन्, noun, instr. pl. masc.)  
विरोधिनो (virodhino)  
विरोधिन् (noun, gen. sing. masc.)  
निरासस्तु (nirāsastu)  
निरासः (noun, nom. sing. masc.), तु (indeclinable - particle)  
नमः (namaḥ)  
नमः (noun, nom. sing. masc.)  
पदेन (padena)  
पद (noun, instr. sing. neuter)  
वर्ण्यते (varṇyate)  
वर् (verb root; passive voice, present tense, 3rd pers. sing.)

"By case, by what is applied (to) the word; the residual form by the great souls (is marked). The opposing one is rejected indeed; homage (is) by the word, indicated."

"By the case endings applied to words, the leftover forms are marked by great souls. The opposing form is indeed rejected; homage is indicated by the word."
```
