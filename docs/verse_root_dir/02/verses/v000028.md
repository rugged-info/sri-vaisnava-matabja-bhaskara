# Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
SOURCE_PAGES: page_0090.txt

=== मूलपाठः ===


उपायार्थपरेणासावखण्डनमसोच्यते  
उपायो हि मवाच्यस्य रवाच्यो राम एव सः ।। २८।।  

upāyārthapareṇāsāvakhaṇḍanamasocyate  
upāyo hi mavācyasya ravācyo rāma eva saḥ ।। 28।।  

उपायार्थपरेण (upāyārthapareṇa) — उपाय (upāya, m, nominative singular), अर्थ (artha, m, genitive singular), परेण (para, m, instrumental singular); compound tatpuruṣa (purpose by means of the higher/another)  
आसाव् (āsāv) — अस् (as, root verb) + आव् (suffix forming present participle), masc, nominative singular; participle present active  
अखण्डनम् (akhaṇḍanam) — अखण्डन (akhaṇḍana, n, nominative singular); verbal noun  
सोच्यते (socyate) — root शुच् (śuc), passive present 3rd person singular  

उपायः (upāyaḥ) — noun, masculine, nominative singular  
हि (hi) — particle (indeclinable)  
मवाच्यस्य (mavācyasya) — म + अवाच्य (avācy, adjective) + णस् (suffix), genitive singular masculine (of that which ought to be spoken)  
रवाच्यः (ravācal) — adjective, masculine nominative singular (to be spoken)  
रामः (rāmaḥ) — noun, masculine, nominative singular (proper name)  
एव (eva) — particle (indeclinable)  
सः (saḥ) — pronoun, masculine, nominative singular (he)  

By means of the higher purpose of methods, that present participle "āsāv" (existing) is lamented (literally: is lamented as akhaṇḍanam: unbroken/undivided treatment).  
The method indeed of the one to be spoken about (mavācyasya), to be spoken (ravācyah) Rama only he is.  

By means of the supreme purpose of methods, that constant one (āsāv) is to be lamented as unbroken treatment (i.e., the continuous nature is lamented).  
Indeed, the method to be spoken of, the one to be told, is Rama alone.
```
