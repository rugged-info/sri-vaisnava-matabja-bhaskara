=== मूलपाठः ===


द्वितीयप्रश्रीत्तरे जप्यनिर्धरणम् ७३  
* संवित्करः *  
'ददामि' पद से उपायरूप भगवान् की सर्वशक्तिमत्ता कही जाती है। जो सर्वशक्ति से सम्पन्न  
होगे, वहीं 'सर्वभूतोँ से अभय देता है एेसा कह सकते हे ओर वे सर्वसमर्थ श्रीभगवान् ही हो  
सकते हं। असन्दिग्ध तथा सन्तिकृष्टितर वस्तु को बताने के लिए 'एतत्' शब्द का प्रयोग होता  
हे। यहँ भी 'एतत्' पद से प्रपन्नजनरक्षाविधान में अपने व्रत में असंशयत्व (संशयरहितत्व) का  
बोध कराया गया हे।  
* पदपरामर्शः *  
ददामीतिपदेनोपायस्य भगवतः सर्वशक्तिमत्ता प्रकाश्यते, अभयदानसामर्ध्यप्रकाशनेन  
सर्जनधारणनियमनप्रवेशनव्यापनपोषणरक्षणाद्यनेककार्यसमग्पादनसामर्थ्यं कथ्यते। जीवस्तु  
स्वापाद्यवस्थायां स्वसामर्थ्यं न वेत्ति, जानन्तपि किमपि न धारयति। यदि धारयति तर्हिं तस्य  
सर्वथा नियमनं कर्तु नाहति। कदाचित्तथा शक्तोऽपि नूनं ग्लानो भवतीत्यतो भगवत एव  
सर्वविधसामर्ध्यं नान्यस्येति ददामीत्येतेन प्रतिपाद्यते। स्वायत्तचराचरं स्वेच्छयात्पपर्यन्तं  
दातुमुद्यतपिह मां को निगृहीयादिति भावः। यश्च सकलभयहेतुवारणशक्तो भवेत्स एवाभयं  
ददामीति वक्तुं शक्नुयात्। भयार्तस्याभयं कुर्विति सुग्रीवेण प्रार्थितो भगवांस्तद्धयहेतुं तथाविधं  
वालिनं व्यापाद्य स्वाचिन्त्यशक्तिमत्तां प्राकाशयद्यां निपुणं बभाण सुग्रीवः "सेन्द्रानपि  
सुरान्सर्वस्त्वं बाणैः पुरुषर्षभ। समर्थः समरे हन्तुं किं पुनर्वालिनं प्रभो" (वा.रा.४.१२.८) इति।  
स्वयञ्चोक्तं भगवता विभीषणाप्रपत्ति-प्रसङ्ग - 'पिशाचान् दानवान् यक्षान् पृथिव्याञ्चैव  
राक्षसान्। अङ्गुल्यग्रेण तान्हन्यामिच्छन् हरिगणेश्वर (६.१८.१०) इति। इत्थं ददामीत्यनेन  
प्रकृत्यर्थमुपेक्ष प्रत्ययार्थस्य व्यापाराश्रयत्वस्य विवरक्षणात्सर्वक्रियासम्पादनसामर्थ्य प्रकाश्यते।  
एतदिति पदेनासंशयत्वं प्रतिपाद्यते। तस्यायं भावः समीपतरवर्ति चेतदो  
रूपमिन्युक्तौस्तथाविध-समर्थस्य वक्तुस्तत्सामर्थ्यस्य च समीपतरवर्तित्वात्तदर्थे न संशयस्य  
प्रवृत्तिरिति बोधनायैतदिति पदम्। एतदः  
सर्वनामत्वाल्यधानपरामर्शित्विनाव्यवहितपूर्वोक्ताकारेण गृह्यमाणपिण्डीभूतार्थ प्रतिज्ञातं  
बुद्धावधिरूढं परामृश्यते। आपामरप्रतिपत्यनुगुणं सकलं स्फुटमाकर्ण्यमानमिति लभ्यते,  
स्फुटत्वाद्यावदीवधारणेऽसंशयत्वमुच्यते; स्वसामर्थ्यं सन्दिहानः स्फुटं प्रतिज्ञातुं नार्हतीति  
भावः।  
व्रतमेतत्पदेनाथी तद्ायमभिधीयते ।  
निर्भरत्वानुसन्धानं ममेति प्रतिपाद्यते ।।५२।।

* saṃvitkaraḥ *  
'dadāmi' pada se upayārūpa Bhagavān kī sarvaśaktimattā kahī jātī hai. jo sarvaśakti se sampanna  
hoge, vahīṁ 'sarvabhūtoṁ se abhaya detā hai esā kah sakte he or ve sarvasamartha śrībhagavān hī ho  
sakte haṁ. asandigdha tathā santikṛṣṭitara vastu ko batāne ke liye 'etat' śabda kā prayoga hotā  
he. yahaṁ bhī 'etat' pada se prapannajanarakṣāvīdhān meṁ apane vrata meṁ asaṁśayatva (saṁśayarahitva) kā  
bodh karāyā gayā he.  
* padaparāmarśaḥ *  
dadāmītipadenopāyasya Bhagavataḥ sarvaśaktimattā prakāśyate, abhayadānasāmardhyaprakāśanena  
sarjanadhāraṇaniyamanapraveśanavyāpanapōṣaṇarakṣaṇādyānekakāryasamgapasādanasāmarthyaṁ kathyate. jīvasti  
svāpādyavasthāyāṁ svasāmarthyaṁ na veti, jānampi kimapi na dhārayati. yadi dhārayati tarhiṁ tasya  
sarvathā niyamanam kartu nāhati. kadācittathā śakto'pi nūnaṁ glāno bhavatītyato Bhagavata eva  
sarvavidhāsāmarthyaṁ nānyasyeti dadāmītyetena pratipādyate. svāyattacarācaraṁ svecchayātpaparyantaṁ  
dātumudyatapiha māṁ ko nigṛhīyāditi bhāvaḥ. yaśca sakalabhayahētuvāraṇaśakto bhavets evābhayaṁ  
dadāmīti vaktuṁ śaknuyāt. bhayārthasyābhayaṁ kurviti sugrīveṇa prārthito Bhagavānstaddhayahētuṁ tathāvidhaṁ  
vālinaṁ vyāpādya svācin̄tyaśaktimattāṁ prākāśayadyāṁ nipuṇaṁ babhāṇa sugrīvaḥ "sendrānapi  
surānsarvastvaṁ bāṇaiḥ puruṣarṣabha. samarthaḥ samare hantuṁ kiṁ punarvālinaṁ prabho" (vā.rā.4.12.8) iti.  
svayañcoktaṁ Bhagavatā vibhīṣaṇāprapatti-prasaṅga - 'piśācān dānavān yakṣān pṛthivyāṁcaiva  
rākṣasān. aṅgulyagreṇ tānhanyāmicchan harigaṇeśvara (6.18.10) iti. itthaṁ dadāmītyanena  
prakṛtyartham upekṣa pratyayārthasya vyāpārāśrayatvasya vivarakṣaṇātsarvakriyāsampādanasāmarthya prakāśyate.  
etaditi padenasaṁśayatvaṁ pratipādyate. tasyāyaṁ bhāvaḥ samīpataravarti cetado  
rūpaminyuktaustathāvidha-samarthasya vaktustatsāmarthyasya ca samīpataravartitvāttadarthe na saṁśayasya  
pravṛttiriti bodhanāyaetaditi padam. etadaḥ  
sarvanāmatvālyadhānaparāmarśitvināvyavahitapūrvoktākāreṇa gṛhyamāṇapiṇḍībhūtārtha pratijñātaṁ  
buddhāvadhirūḍhaṁ parāmṛśyate. āpāmarapatipatyanuguṇaṁ sakalaṁ sphuṭamākarṇyamānamiti labhyate,  
sphuṭatvādyāvadīvadhāraṇe'saṁśayatvamuccyate; svasāmarthyaṁ sandihānaḥ sphuṭaṁ pratijñātuṁ nārhatīti  
bhāvaḥ.  
vratametatpadenāthī tadtāyamabhidhīyate ।

।।५२।। (52)  

(I) Second, the decision of the sacred chant 73  
* Conscious creator *  
The word 'dadāmi' (I give) is used as a hypostasis of the Lord's omnipotence. He who is endowed with all power  
(you) are the one who gives fearlessness to all beings, so it can be said, and only that omnipotent revered Lord can  
be so. To indicate an undoubted and proximate object, the word 'etat' ('this') is used. Here also, by the word 'etat',  
in the regulation for protection of supplicants, the awareness of certainty (freedom from doubt) in one's vow is conveyed.  
* Word commentary *  
By the word 'dadāmi', the omnipotence of the Lord is manifested; by manifesting the capacity to grant fearlessness,  
the capacity is narrated to accomplish numerous functions such as creation, sustenance, control, regulation, insertion,  
pervasion, nourishment, protection and more. But the living being, in a state of subjection to weakness, does not know  
his own power, even if he knows, he does not wield it. If he wields it, then his full control is not established.  
Sometimes, though powerful, he surely becomes weakened. Therefore, it is established by this saying that only the Lord  
has omnipotence of all kinds, not another. The attitude is: I am ready here to give the autonomous moving and unmoving  
(by) my own will; who can restrain me? And he who is capable of removing all fears and causes can say, "I give fearlessness."  
Fearlessness is granted; thus Sugrīva requested the Lord as a cause of mercy; attacking Vālin and manifesting his own  
mastery Sugrīva said skillfully, "O best of men, you who can kill Indras and all gods by arrows in battle, O Lord,  
why not Vālin?" The incident of Vibhīṣaṇa's surrender is self-referentially stated by the Lord: "I wished to kill 
the demons, Dānavas, Yakṣas, Rākṣasas on the earth with my fingertip." Thus, by this 'dadāmi', the nature's meaning  
and confidence-based dependence and explanation manifest the capability to perform all actions. This word establishes  
undoubted nature. Its meaning is: It occurs near the mind and form in the combined manner of such capable speaker and of  
such speaking capacity; therefore for that matter there arises no doubt, this is the teaching, thus the word. This,  
owing to pronominality and attentive implication, accepted in the previously declared form, with accepted solid  
meaning and mental conception, is thoroughly considered. Everything is found to be manifestly audible and clear, thus  
non-doubtfulness is called. The one who doubts his own efficacy does not deserve clear recognition, such is the meaning.  
This vow is designated by this word. The investigation of dependence is regarded as mine. (Verse 52)  

The omnipotence expressed by the word ‘dadāmi’ is established here as an expression of the Lord’s all-powerful capacity to grant fearless protection and manage all creation and control. The certainty and lack of doubt inherent in the vow are signified by the demonstrative word ‘etat’. The passage emphasizes that only the Lord alone has this supreme, all-encompassing power and no other.  

In close harmony with the verse, one who is capable of eliminating all fears can confidently say “I give fearlessness,” as exemplified by Sugrīva’s plea to the Lord to overcome Vālin, and the Lord’s own assertion of divine power. Thus, the translation reflects both the literal and interpretative sense of divine capacity and unfaltering assurance conveyed by these sacred words.
