## Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
=== मूलपाठः ===
```

---

## Transliteration (IAST)

```text
नमः पदेनाखण्डेन त्वात्मात्मीयत्वमुच्यते ।
षष्ठ्यन्तेन मकारेण भीग्यभीक्तृत्वमप्युत ।।१८।।
```

---

## Literal Translation

नमः (namaḥ) – नमः, indeclinable, nominal indeclinable (particle, salutation)
पदेनाखण्डेन (padenākhaṇḍena) – पदेन (pada, padena) instrumental singular masculine + अखण्डेन (akhaṇḍena) instrumental singular masculine; "with an unbroken foot" (compound tatpuruṣa: पदेन अखण्डेन)
त्व (tva) – त्व (tvad), pronoun, nominative singular masculine/neuter; "you"
आत्मात्मीयत्वम् (āt-māt-mīyatvam) – आत्म (ātma) noun stem + आत्मीय (ātmiya) adjective stem + तत्त्व (tattva) abstract noun ending – neuter nominative singular; "the quality of being related to self"
उच्यते (ucyate) – verb, 3rd person singular present passive or middle indicative; root उप + इ (uc), "is said/called"
षष्ठ्यन्तेन (ṣaṣṭhyantena) – षष्ठ्यन्त (ṣaṣṭhyanta) compound of षष्ठि (ṣaṣṭhi, sixth) + अन्त (anta, end), instrumental singular masculine; "by the end of the sixth"
मकारेण (makāreṇa) – मकार (makāra) noun masculine, instrumental singular; "by the letter 'ma'"
भीग्यभीक्तृत्वम् (bhīgyabhīktrtvam) – भीग्य (bhīgya, past passive participle neuter singular) + भीक्तृत्वम् (bhīktrtvam), abstract noun, neuter nominative singular; "the state of being soaked and steeped"
अपि (api) – indeclinable particle; "also"
उत् (ut) – indeclinable particle; "and"
।
।

---

## Smooth Translation

"नमः पदेनाखण्डेन" – "Salutation by the uninterrupted foot (meter)" (instrumental case)
