## Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
=== मूलपाठः ===
```

---

## Transliteration (IAST)

```text
तच्छेषी पदतोऽप्यतो भगवतोऽनत्रूत्याहशोषत्वकं  
व्यावृत्तिस्तु सुरान्तरादिगतसत्तच्छेषताया मुहुः ।।१३।।
```

---

## Literal Translation

तत् (tat), तत्, pronoun;  
अष्टषी (chēṣī), चेṣ्, present participle, masculine nominative singular, verb base √चेष् (to desire, to pursue) (compound: तत् + चेṣī = तत् + चेषी 'that remaining/pursuing' [tatpuruṣa]);  
पदतः (padataḥ), पद, noun masculine dual genitive or ablative singular (here genitive singular "from the foot");  
अपि (api), अपि, indeclinable (particle, "also, even");  
अतः (ataḥ), तत्, pronoun, masculine ablative singular ("from that");  
भगवतः (bhagavataḥ), भगवन्, noun masculine genitive singular ("of the Blessed One");  
अनत्रूत्य (anatrūtya), अ+नत्रूत्य, present participle, masculine nominative singular, prefix अ- (negation) + त्रू (root); here a compound meaning "not trembling, unmoved";   
आह (āha), आह, verb root past tense 3rd singular ("said");  
शोषत्वकं (śōṣatvakaṃ), शोषत्वक, noun neuter accusative singular ("dryness, withering");

---

## Smooth Translation

व्यावृत्ति (vyāvṛtti), व्य + आवृति, noun feminine nominative singular ("withdrawal, departure");  
तु (tu), तु, indeclinable ("but, indeed");  
सुरान्तरात् (surāntarāt), सुर (god) + अन्तर (between) + आदि (from), noun masculine ablative singular ("from among the gods");  
आगत (āgata), adjective masculine nominative singular ("having come");  
सत्व (sattva), सत्व, noun neuter nominative singular ("being, existence");  
तच्चेषताया (tacchēṣatāyā), तत् + चेषत (verbal noun or participle noun), instrumental singular ("by that remaining/continuance");  
म hüः (muhuḥ), muhuḥ, indeclinable ("again and again, repeatedly").
