# Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
SOURCE_PAGES: page_0115.txt

=== मूलपाठः ===


विकचपदमदलायतवीक्षणं विधिभवादिमनोहरसुस्मितम् ।
जनकलजाटगपाङ्गसमीक्षितं प्रणतसत्समनुग्रहकारिणम् ।। ५६।।

vikacapadamadalayatavīkṣaṇaṃ vidhibhavādimanoharasusmitam ।
janakalajāṭagapāṅgasamīkṣitaṃ praṇatasatsam-anugrahakāriṇam ।। 56।।

vikaca (vikaca), adj. m. nom. sg. (flower bud)
padam (padam), n. acc. sg. (foot)
adala (adala), n. gen. sg. (cluster)
ayatā (ayatā), adj. f. gen. sg. (extended)
tvīkṣaṇa (tvīkṣaṇa), n. nom. sg. (looking or viewing)
vidhi (vidhi), m. inst. sg. (by the rule)
bhavādi (bhava+ādi), m. inst. sg. (beginning with existence and so on)
manohara (manohara), adj. m. nom. sg. (charming)
susmita (susmita), adj. m. nom. sg. (smiling)

jana (jana), m. nom. sg. (people)
kalaja (kalaja), adj. m. nom. sg. (born of the bud)
aṭa (aṭa), m. nom. sg. (tiger)
gapaṅga (gapaṅga), m. nom. sg. (body parts)
samīkṣita (samīkṣita), adj. n. acc. sg. (looked upon)
praṇata (praṇata), adj. m. nom. sg. (bowed down)
sat (sat), adj. m. nom. sg. (true, good)
sa-manugraha (sa + anugraha) (tatpuruṣa compound) (sa = with, anugraha = favor)
kāriṇam (kāriṇam), m. acc. sg. (doer, maker)

Literal translation:  
The flower bud’s cluster-extended looking (tvīkṣaṇa) (acting) by rules and so on (vidhi-bhavādi), charming and sweetly smiling,  
the people’s bud-born tiger’s body parts having been looked upon, bowed and good with favor-doer.

Smooth English translation:  
With eyes as if looking over clusters of flower buds, charming and sweetly smiling by laws and such,  
he is the benefactor who graciously regards with folded devotion the people’s tiger-bodied forms born of the bud.
```
