# Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
SOURCE_PAGES: page_0108.txt

=== मूलपाठः ===


चकारतोऽनुक्तसमुच्ययार्थतो निगद्यते त्वन्य उपाय आत्मवित्।
उपायसंसेव्यधिकारिलक्षणं पदेन वै याचत इत्यनेन तु ।।४९।।

cakārato'nuktasamucyayārthato nigadyate tvanya upāya ātmavit।
upāyasaṁsevyadhikārilakṣaṇaṁ padena vai yācata ityanena tu ।।49।।

चकारतः (cakārataḥ), चकारत् + अः, अशुद्धप्रत्यय (ablative singular masculine); 
अनुक्त (anukta), अनुक्त (past passive participle); 
समुच्यय (samucyaya), समुच्यय (nom. sg. m. or indeclinable part. ?), compound element; 
अर्थतः (arthataḥ), अर्थ + अः, ablative singular masculine; 
निगद्यते (nigadyate), निगद् + यते (present passive indic. 3 sg.); 
त्व (tva), त्व (emphatic particle); 
अन्य (anya), अन्य (adj. nom. sg. m.); 
उपाय (upāya), उपाय (nom. sg. m.); 
आत्मवित् (ātmavit), आत्मवित् (nom. sg. m.); 

उपायसंसेव्यधिकारिलक्षणं (upāyasaṁsevyadhikārilakṣaṇaṁ), उपाय + सं + सेव् (root) + अधिकार + लक्षणं (compound, bahuvrīhi/tatpuruṣa - describing characteristic of one authorized to use the upāya); acc. sg. n.; 
पदेन (padena), पद + एन, instr. sg. m./n.; 
वै (vai), particle (emphatic); 
याचत (yācata), याच् + त (imperative 3 sg.); 
इत्यनेन (ityanena), इति + एन, instr. sg. m.; 
तु (tu), तु (indeclinable particle "but").

(chakārataḥ anukta samucyaya arthataḥ nigadyate tva anya upāya ātmavit|
upāya saṁsevyadhikārilakṣaṇaṁ padena vai yācata ityanena tu||49||)

"chakārataḥ" - ablative singular masculine of "chakārata" (from 'cakārata'?), ablative (from the doer);
"anukta" - past passive participle, 'not said' or 'not told';
"samucyaya" - "pronouncement, assertion" (nom. sg. m.);
"arthataḥ" - ablative singular masculine of "artha" (meaning, purpose);
"nigadyate" - (present passive indic. 3 sg.) "is said, is spoken";
"tva" - emphatic particle "indeed";
"anya upāya" - "another method/tool" (nom. sg. m.);
"ātma-vit" - "one who knows the self" (nom. sg. m.);
"upāya-saṁsevyadhikara-lakṣaṇam" - compound: "upāya" (means), "saṁsevi" (to serve), "adhikāri" (one who is authorized), "lakṣaṇa" (mark, characteristic), i.e. "the characteristic of one authorized to use the means" (acc. sg. n.);
"padena" - by the word (instr. sg. n.);
"vai" - indeed (particle);
"yācata" - imperative 3 sg. "he/she/it should request";
"ityanena" - by this (instr. sg. m.) "thus";
"tu" - but (particle);

Literal translation (with grammatical notes):

"From (abl.) the doer (implied by 'chakārataḥ'), (something) not said (anukta) about the assertion (samucyaya), indeed in the sense (arthataḥ), is said (passive present) another method (upāya) [by] the self-knower (ātma-vit).
The mark (lakṣaṇa) of one authorized to use the method (upāya-saṁsevyadhikara-) is requested (imperative yācata) by the word (padena) indeed (vai) by this (ityanena) but (tu)."

Smooth English translation:

"The self-knower refers to another method, not explicitly mentioned in the assertion, in this sense.  
But by this word, one is indeed asked for the characteristic of the person authorized to use the method."
```
