# Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
SOURCE_PAGES: page_0109.txt

=== मूलपाठः ===


७२ श्रीवेष्णवमतान्नभास्करः  
अथाभयमिति प्राप्तिप्रतिबन्धकवारणम् ।  
सर्वभूतेभ्य इत्येव प्राप्यस्य प्रतिबन्धकम् ।।५०।।

72 śrīveṣṇavamatānnabhāskaraḥ  
athābhayamiti prāptipratibandhakavāraṇam |  
sarvabhūtebhya ityeva prāpyasya pratibandhakam ||50||

७२ (72), base numeral, indeclinable  
श्रीवेष्णवमतान्नभास्करः (śrīveṣṇavamatānnabhāskaraḥ), compound (tatpuruṣa): śrīveṣṇa + amatā + annabhāskara; masculine, nominative singular  
अथाभयमिति (athābhayamiti), compound (bahuvrīhi): atha + abhaya + iti; indeclinable  
प्राप्तिप्रतिबन्धकवारणम् (prāptipratibandhakavāraṇam), compound (tatpuruṣa): prāpti + pratibandhaka + vāraṇam; neuter, nominative singular  
सर्वभूतेभ्य (sarvabhūtebhya), from सर्व (sarva), भूता (bhūta), suffix एभ्य (ebhya); masculine/neuter plural, ablative/dative/instrumental plural  
इत्येव (ityeva), indeclinable: iti + eva (emphatic)  
प्राप्यस्य (prāpyasya), verbal adjective (gerundive) nominalized, genitive singular  
प्रतिबन्धकम् (pratibandhakam), neuter singular accusative or nominative singular

७२ श्रीवेष्णवमतान्नभास्करः  
अथाभयमिति प्राप्तिप्रतिबन्धकवारणम् ।  
सर्वभूतेभ्य इत्येव प्राप्यस्य प्रतिबन्धकम् ।।

72 Śrīveṣṇavamatān Nabhāskara  
Then "fear not," says: the obstruction to obtaining (success).  
To all beings indeed, the obstruction of that which is to be obtained.

Nabhāskara the Śrīveṣṇavamatānn (name/title) says: “Now be without fear,” (this is) the obstruction preventing attainment. This obstruction to attainment is indeed directed towards all beings.

Nabhāskara of Śrīveṣṇavamatānn says:  
Now, “Be without fear”—this is the obstruction preventing success.  
This obstruction to attainment is indeed for all beings.
```
