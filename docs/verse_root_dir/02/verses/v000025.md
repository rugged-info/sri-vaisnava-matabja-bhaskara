# Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
SOURCE_PAGES: page_0088.txt

=== मूलपाठः ===


पिता च मम राघवः" इति।
तच्चतुर्थ्या स्वानुरूपकैङ्कर्यप्रार्थनोच्यते ।
विषयान्तरसेवाऽपि प्राप्ता सा विनिवल्यते ।। २५।।

pitā ca mama rāghavaḥ" iti।
taccaturthyā svānurūpakaiṅkaryaprārthanocyate ।
viṣayāntarasevā'pi prāptā sā vinivalyate ।। 25।।

पिता (pitā), पिता, masculine, singular, nominative  
च (ca), indeclinable, conjunction (and)  
मम (mama), pronoun, 1st person, singular, genitive (my)  
राघवः (rāghavaḥ), rāghava, masculine, singular, nominative (Rāghava)  
इति (iti), indeclinable, particle (thus says/quote marker)

तत् (tat), pronoun, neuter, singular, nominative (that)  
चतुर्थ्या (caturthyā), caturtha (fourth), feminine, singular, instrumental (by the fourth)  
स्व (sva), pronoun/adjective, neuter, plural, accusative (own)  
अनुरूप (anurūpa), adjective, neuter, plural, accusative (corresponding)  
कैङ्कर्य (kaiṅkarya), neuter, plural, accusative (service)  
प्रार्थना (prārthanā), feminine, singular, nominative (request/prayer)  
उच्यते (ucyate), verb, middle/passive, present tense, 3rd person, singular (is said)

विषय (viṣaya), masculine, singular, nominative (object, topic)  
अन्तर (antara), masculine, singular, nominative (internal, inner)  
सेवा (sevā), feminine, singular, nominative (service)  
अपि (api), indeclinable, particle (also)  
प्राप्ता (prāptā), past participle, feminine, singular, nominative (obtained)  
सा (sā), pronoun, feminine, singular, nominative (she/that)  
विनिवल्यते (vinivalyate), verb, middle/passive, present, 3rd person, singular (is lost/dissolves)

Literal translation:  
"Father and my Rāghava," thus (is said).  
That (tat), by the fourth (chaturthyā) corresponding own services request is said.  
The internal service to the object also obtained, she is dissolved.

Smooth English translation:  
"He is called 'Father and my Rāghava.'  
This is spoken as a request for service appropriate to one’s own position by the fourth (class).  
Even the service within the subject having been attained, she becomes absorbed (or dissolved)."
```
