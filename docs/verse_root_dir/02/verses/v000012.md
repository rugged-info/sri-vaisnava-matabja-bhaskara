## Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
=== मूलपाठः ===
```

## Transliteration (IAST)

```text
वसिष्टलोमशपराशरप्रभतिभिः समुपात्तो लोकहिताय समुपदिष्टः। नित्यानां श्रीहनुमदादीना-
मपि जीवनाश्रयः परमाधारः। परित उरु अधिकं शुभं येन तादृशः सर्वतो मङ्गलाकारः।
आग्रितानामेश्वर्याद्युपलम्भकत्वमपि स्वत एवानुष्ठातृणां ज्ञानशक्तत्यादिभिः सहानेकगुणलाभी
भवतीति। ऽपिक्षितज्ञानशक्तत्यादिलाभीऽनायासेनैव भवति। अत आचार्यादयं लब्धुं योग्यः
स्वश्रयस इति सङ्कपः।
यावद्रेदार्थगर्भं प्रणवि जगदुदाधारभूतं सविन्दु
प्रव्यक्त रामबीजं श्रुतिमुनिगदितो्कृष्टषद्याप्तिभदम् ।
रेफारूढत्रिमूरति प्रचुरतरमहाशक्ति विश्वौत्निदानं
णश्रत्संराजते सद्विविधसकलसग्भासमानप्रपञ्चम् ।।१२।।
```

वसिष्ट (vasiṣṭa): Proper noun, masculine, nominative singular  
लोम (loma): noun, masculine, nominative singular, tatpuruṣa compound member  
शपराशरप्रभति (śaparāśaraprabhati): compound (tatpuruṣa), masculine, nominative plural (prabhati = children/descendants), base words: śapara, āśara, prabhati  
-भिः (-bhiḥ): instrumental plural ending (prabhati-bhiḥ)  
समुपात्तः (samupāttaḥ): past passive participle, masculine nominative singular  
लोकहिताय (lokahitāya): noun, masculine/neuter, dative singular (lokahita + āya, purpose)  
समुपदिष्टः (samupadiṣṭaḥ): past passive participle, masculine nominative singular  
नित्यानाम् (nityānām): adjective, masculine, genitive plural (nitya, eternal)  
श्रीहनुमदादीनाम् (śrīhanumadādīnām): compound of śrī, hanumad + ādīnām (etcetera), genitive plural  
अपि (api): indeclinable, particle meaning ‘also’  
जीवनाश्रयः (jīvanāśrayaḥ): noun masculine nominative singular  
परमाधारः (paramādhāraḥ): noun masculine nominative singular  
परित (parita): adjective, means ‘around’  
उरु (uru): adjective, comparative or positive, nominative singular, ‘broad’  
अधिकं (adhikaṃ): adjective or adverb, accusative singular neuter ‘more, greater’  
शुभं (śubhaṃ): adjective, accusative singular neuter ‘auspicious, good’  
येन (yena): relative pronoun, instrumental singular masculine or neuter, 'by whom'  
तादृशः (tādṛśaḥ): pronoun/adjective ‘such’, masculine nominative singular  
सर्वतः (sarvataḥ): adverb ‘from all sides’  
मङ्गलाकारः (maṅgalākāraḥ): noun masculine nominative singular ‘maker of auspiciousness’  
आग्रितानाम् (āgritānām): noun masculine genitive plural (?) — possibly ‘the foremost’ (from āgṛta)  
ईश्वर्यादि (īśvaryādi): compound 'īśvaryādi' meaning ‘divine power etc.’, indeclinable or noun base in genitive plural ‘and so on’  
उपलम्भकत् (upalambhakat) with suffix ‘tvam’ = noun ‘quality of grasping/perceiving’  
अपि (api): particle ‘also’  
स्वत (svata): indeclinable ‘by itself’  
एव (eva): particle ‘only, indeed’  
अनुष्ठातृणाम् (anuṣṭhātṛṇām): noun masculine genitive plural ‘of the performers’  
ज्ञानशक्तत्यादिभिः (jñāna-śaktattyādibhiḥ): instrumental plural, 'with knowledge-power and others'  
सह (saha): indeclinable ‘together with’ (here: sahā) + इनेकगुणलाभी (anekaguṇalābhī): adjective or noun ‘gainer of many qualities’  
भवतीति (bhavatīti): verb 3rd person singular present indicative ‘becomes, is’ + iti (quote)  
अपिक्षित (apikṣita): participle or adjective ‘unexpected’  
ज्ञानशक्तत्यादिलाभी (jñānaśaktatyādilābhī): noun ‘gainer of knowledge power etc.’  
अनाय़ासेनैव (anāyāsenaiva): indeclinable ‘effortlessly indeed’  
भवति (bhavati): verb 3rd person singular present indicative ‘becomes’  
अतः (ataḥ): particle ‘therefore’  
आचार्यादयः (ācāryādayaḥ): noun masculine nominative plural ‘teachers and others’  
एयम् (ayam): pronoun masculine nominative singular ‘this’  
लॄब्धुम् (labdhuṃ): infinitive form of ‘to obtain’  
योग्यः (yogyaḥ): adjective masculine nominative singular ‘worthy, fit’  
स्वश्रयः (svaśrayaḥ): noun masculine nominative singular ‘own support’  
इति (iti): particle ‘thus’  
सङ्कपः (saṅkapaḥ): noun masculine nominative singular ‘resolution, intention’

सद्विविधसकलसग्भासमानप्रपञ्चम् (sadvidhvasakalasagbhāsamānaprapañcam): compound noun accusative singular ‘manifestation having all types of good and beautiful splendor’

## Smooth Translation

Vasiṣṭa, Loma, Śapara, Āśara, and descendants came together and were instructed for the welfare of the world.  
Among eternals like Śrī Hanumad and others, (he) is also the refuge of life and the supreme support.  
Around (him) broad, greater, auspicious (qualities) by which such a one is an all-sided maker of auspiciousness.  
Among the foremost, the qualities of divine sovereignty and the like, and the quality of perception, are indeed by themselves the gains of the performers by means of knowledge-power and others possessing many good qualities.  
It is said that those gaining knowledge-power etc. unexpectedly also easily become so. Therefore, this is worthy to be obtained from the teacher as one’s own support — thus is the resolve.  
As long as the womb of Redārtha exists, O Praṇava, manifest seed of Rāma, described by the sage Śruti on the best auspicious day,  
Mounted on the Repha and of three forms, great very abundant power, giver of the world’s vital breath,  
(He) shines splendidly, the manifestor of all kinds of good and beautiful splendor. (verse 12)
