## Verse — मूलपाठः

=== मूलपाठः ===

---

## Transliteration (IAST)

```text
इति प्रतिपदमर्थसङ्ग्रहः।  
अत्रोपायान्तरस्याथी निवृत्तिः प्रतिपाद्यते ।  
सकृदित्येवकारेण तूपायनिरपेक्षता ।।४६ ।।  
प्रपत्रायेतिपदतस्तूपायस्थानमुच्यते ।  
उपायत्वं भगवतस्तवेति पदतस्तथा ।।४७।।
```

---

## Literal Translation

इति (iti) – indeclinable, particle (thus, so)  
प्रतिपदम् (pratipadam) – प्रतिपद्, accusative singular neuter (object: "word/term")  
अर्थसङ्‌ग्रहः (arthasaṅgrahaḥ) – अर्थ-सङ्ग्रहः, nominative singular masculine (compound: tatpuruṣa; "collection of meanings")  
अत्र (atra) – indeclinable, adverb ("here")  
उपायान्तरस्य (upāyāntarasya) – उपाय-अन्तरस्, genitive singular masculine (compound: tatpuruṣa; "of another method/means")  
अथी (athī) – अथ, indeclinable, particle ("then")  
निवृत्तिः (nivṛttiḥ) – निवृत्ति, nominative singular feminine ("cessation, conclusion")  
प्रतिपाद्यते (pratipādyate) – verb root प्रदा, present passive/mediopassive, 3rd singular ("is expounded")  
सकृद् (sakṛd) – indeclinable, adverb ("once")  
इति (iti) – indeclinable, particle ("thus")  
एव (eva) – indeclinable, particle ("indeed")  
कारेण (kāreṇa) – कार, instrumental singular masculine ("by the form")  
तूपायनिरपेक्षता (tūpāyanirapekṣatā) – तूपाय-निरपेक्षता, nominative singular feminine (compound: tatpuruṣa; "absence of dependence on the perfect means")  
प्रपत्रायेतिपदतस् (prapatrāyetipadatas) – compound, likely tatpuruṣa (unclear segmentation)  
तूपायस्थानम् (tūpāyasthānam) – तूपाय-स्थानम्, accusative singular neuter (compound: tatpuruṣa; "place of perfect means")  
उच्यते (ucyate) – verb root उच्, present passive 3rd singular ("is called")  
उपायत्वं (upāyatvaṃ) – उपायत्वं, accusative singular neuter ("the state of being a means")  
भगवतः (bhagavataḥ) – भगवत्, genitive singular masculine ("of the blessed Lord")  
त्वे (tve) – unclear; likely तव (tava), possessive pronoun, genitive or locative singular ("your")  
इति (iti) – indeclinable particle ("thus")  
पदतः (padataḥ) – पदतस्, genitive singular masculine (compound tatpuruṣa)  
तथा (tathā) – indeclinable, particle ("so")
