२.११२.१८) इत्यादिवचनास््फुटमवसेयम्, एतेन अनन्याधीनत्वाद्

भावः।

वचनबद्ध हो रहे हैं। — Are becoming verbally bound (present participle 'are bound by word')

Smooth English translation:  

74. Bhāskara of the Śrīvaiṣṇava doctrine is becoming verbally committed. Therefore, the use of the word ‘mama’ (‘my’) confirms that one should rely on them thinking that they certainly protect the surrendered devotees. The vow-expressive word ‘vratam’ conveys a strong sense of their steadfast commitment.  

Commentary:  

The vow is a truthful resolve concerning the Lord, always accepted unconditionally. It must not be abandoned in any circumstance. If, by mistake, the vow-keeper faces the loss of all duties and risk of life, firm protection is still indicated. As stated in Vālmīki’s vow: ‘When destroyed, this dharma should perish.’ By the rule of restraint of possession, the vow is regarded as something not to be violated, giving rise to strict self-control.  

The saying ‘Rāma does not speak to two’ demonstrates the unwavering nature and the undefeated power of true resolve, independent and equipped with all support, the Lord and embodiment of dharma, son of Daśaratha, thus making the vow sacred and effective. The words such as ‘Lakṣmī, Chandra, Himavat or the snow should be abandoned, cross beyond ocean and shore’ make it absolutely clear that the vow binds exclusively, implying strict observance and the protection of all kinds of commitments.  

Even with the final word ‘mame’ (‘mine’), it is expressed that the entire burden is placed upon the Lord, father, and son; thus, one becomes free from all burden there. Hence, the concern for maintaining the vow rests entirely with the one who made it, not with me. With intellect, I cannot accomplish it; only the Lord will do so. Recognizing this dependence brings complete freedom from anxiety.  

The meaning and essence of this passage are to be understood as sheltering and based on preference. This is the explanation of the nature of attaining that vow and the meaning of this statement. Verse 53.
