# Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
SOURCE_PAGES: page_0157.txt

=== मूलपाठः ===


स्पृशत्येकादशीं स्पृष्टा द्वादशी श्रवणेन चेत् ।
विष्णुश्शृङ्खलयोगोऽयं भाद्रे शुक्ले प्रकीर्तितः ।।९९।।
श्रवणद्वादशी होषा पुनरावृत्तिनाशिनी ।
तस्मादुपवसेदत्र यततो मतिमान्नरः ।।१००।।

spṛśatyekādaśīṃ spṛṣṭā dvādaśī śravaṇena cet ।
viṣṇuśśṛṅkhalayogo’yaṃ bhādre śukle prakīrtitaḥ ॥99॥
śravaṇadvādaśī hoṣā punarāvṛttināśinī ।
tasmādupavedatra yatato matimānnaraḥ ॥100॥

स्पृशति (spṛśati), √स्पृश् (to touch), verb, 3rd person singular, present indicative active  
एकादशीं (ekādaśīṃ), ekādaśī, feminine, accusative singular  
स्पृष्टा (spṛṣṭā), √स्पृश् (past passive participle), feminine nominative singular  
द्वादशी (dvādaśī), dvādaśī, feminine nominative singular  
श्रवणेन (śravaṇena), śravaṇa, masculine/neuter, instrumental singular  
चेत् (cet), indeclinable, conjunction  

विष्णुश् (viṣṇuṣ), विष्णु (Viṣṇu), masculine, nominative singular, tatpuruṣa compound (viṣṇu + śṛṅkhalā)  
शृङ्खलयोगः (śṛṅkhalayogaḥ), śṛṅkhalā (chain) + yogaḥ (union), masculine, nominative singular, tatpuruṣa compound  
अयम् (ayam), demonstrative pronoun, masculine, nominative singular  
भाद्रे (bhādre), bhādra, adjective (good/auspicious), locative singular  
शुक्ले (śukle), śukla, adjective (bright/white [half of lunar month], adjective), locative singular  
प्रकीर्तितः (prakīrtitaḥ), √कीर्त् (to proclaim), past passive participle, masculine nominative singular  

श्रवणद्वादशी (śravaṇadvādaśī), śravaṇa + dvādaśī, feminine nominative singular, bahuvrīhi compound  
होषा (hoṣā), hoṣa (name of lunar day), feminine nominative singular  
पुनरावृत्तिनाशिनी (punarāvṛttināśinī), punar-āvṛtti + nāśinī, feminine nominative singular, bahuvrīhi compound  
तस्मात् (tasmāt), indeclinable, ablative singular  
उपवसेत् (upavaset), √उपवस् (to fast), verb, 3rd person singular, optative mood, active voice  
अत्र (atra), indeclinable, locative  
यतः (yataḥ), yat (restrained/dedicated), masculine nominative singular, adjective  
मतिमान् (matimān), matiman (intelligent), masculine nominative singular, adjective  
नरः (naraḥ), nara (man), masculine nominative singular  

"स्पृशत्येकादशीं स्पृष्टा द्वादशी श्रवणेन चेत्"  
(One who touches) (the ekādaśī) (touched) (the dvādaśī) (by hearing) (if) — ("if dvādaśī is touched by the hearing of ekādaśī")  

"विष्णुश्शृङ्खलयोगोऽयं भाद्रे शुक्ले प्रकीर्तितः"  
(This union of Viṣṇu’s chain) (in the auspicious bright half) (is proclaimed)  

"श्रवणद्वादशी होषा पुनरावृत्तिनाशिनी"  
(Śravaṇa dvādaśī) (Hoṣā) (destroyer of repetition)  

"तस्मादुपवसेदत्र यततो मतिमान्नरः"  
(Therefore should fast here) (one dedicated) (intelligent man)  

If the dvādaśī (the twelfth lunar day) is touched (made holy) by hearing ( śravaṇa ) of the ekādaśī (the eleventh lunar day),  
Then this Viṣṇu chain-yoga in the auspicious bright half is declared (prakiirtitaḥ).  
Śravaṇa-dvādaśī, Hoṣā, is the destroyer of repetition (punarāvṛtti-nāśinī).  
Therefore, an intelligent man, dedicated (to restraint), should fast here.  

If the Dvādaśī is sanctified by hearing the Ekādaśī,  
Then this Viṣṇu chain yoga is proclaimed on the bright auspicious half.  
The Śravaṇa Dvādaśī, called Hoṣā, destroys the cycle of repetition.  
Therefore, the wise and devoted man should fast on this day.
```
