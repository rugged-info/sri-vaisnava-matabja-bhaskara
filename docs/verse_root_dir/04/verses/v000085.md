```text
=== मूलपाठः ===
```

## Transliteration (IAST)

```text
जया तथाष्टौ विजया जयन्ती द्रादश्य एता इति पापनाशनी ।।८५।।
```

## Literal Translation

जया (jayā), जय (jaya), fem. nom./instr. sg.;  
तथ (tatha), तत्र (tatra), indeclinable;  
अष्टौ (aṣṭau), अष्ट (aṣṭa), nom. du.;  
विजया (vijayā), विजय (vijaya), fem. nom./instr. sg.;  
जयन्ती (jayantī), जयन्त (jayanta), fem. nom. sg., part.;  
द्रादश्य (drādaśya), द्रादश्य, indeclinable (possible verbal root or phrase, unclear, treated as indeclinable);  
एता (etā), एता (etā), fem. nom. du.;  
इति (iti), इति, indeclinable;  
पापनाशनी (pāpanāśanī), पापनाशनी (pāpanāśanī), fem. nom. sg., tatpuruṣa compound (pāpa + nāśanī).

---

## Smooth Translation

Jayas (victories) and thus eight (in number), victories, victorious, friend-of-the-wealth, these, thus, destroyers-of-sins.
