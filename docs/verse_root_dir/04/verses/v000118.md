## Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
=== मूलपाठः ===
```

---

## Transliteration (IAST)

```text
आत्मारामैस्तथोपायस्वरूपन्ञानिभिश्च तैः ।
मतज्ञर्विरजापारं कैवल्यमिति मन्यते ।।११८।।
```

---

## Literal Translation

आत्मारामैः (ātamāramaiḥ), आत्माराम (ātmarāma), लोकवादी (lokavādī, compound type: bahuvrīhi) कृत्वा बहुवचन-इष्टकरण-करण।
तथा (tathā), तदैव (indecinable, adverb) 
उपाय (upāya), उपाय (upāya), masculine singular nominative
स्वरूपन् (svarūpan), स्वरूप (svarūpa), masculine singular nominative present active participle
ञानि (ñāni), ज्ञ (jña), masculine plural nominative
भी (bhī), भी (indeclinable particle), emphatic particle
च (ca), च (indeclinable conjunction), "and"
तैः (taiḥ), त (tat), pronoun, masculine/neuter plural instrumental
मतज्ञः (matajñaḥ), मतज्ञ (matajña), masculine singular nominative
विरजा (virajā), विरज (viraja), adjective, feminine singular nominative
आपारं (āpāraṁ), आपार (āpāra), neuter singular accusative
कैवल्यम् (kaivalyam), कैवल्य (kaivalya), neuter singular accusative
इति (iti), इति (indeclinable particle), quotative
मन्यते (manyate), मन् (man), verb root, present middle/passive indicative 3rd singular

---

## Smooth Translation

Self-delighting thus, possessing form, and knowledgeable, and also by them (instrumental plural),
The knower of the opinion, free from impurities, and limitless,
Is considered to be kaivalya (is regarded as kaivalya).
