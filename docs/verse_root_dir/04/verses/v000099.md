# Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
SOURCE_PAGES: page_0157.txt

=== मूलपाठः ===


स्पृशत्येकादशीं स्पृष्टा द्वादशी श्रवणेन चेत् ।
विष्णुश्शृङ्खलयोगोऽयं भाद्रे शुक्ले प्रकीर्तितः ।।९९।।  

spṛśatyekādaśīṃ spṛṣṭā dvādaśī śravaṇena cet  
viṣṇuśśṛṅkhalayogo'yaṃ bhādre śukle prakīrtitaḥ ॥99॥  

स्पृशति (spṛśati), √spṛś (verb root), 3rd person singular present indicative active  
एकादशीं (ekādashīṃ), एकादशी (ekādaśī), accusative singular feminine (feminine noun, compound noun)  
स्पृष्टा (spṛṣṭā), √spṛś +kta (participle), nominative singular feminine (perfect passive participle)  
द्वादशी (dvādaśī), द्वादशी (dvādaśī), nominative singular feminine  
श्रवणेन (śravaṇena), श्रवण (śravaṇa), instrumental singular masculine (noun, ātmane)  
चेत् (cet), च (ca) + एत (et), indeclinable (particle)  
विष्णुश् (viṣṇuḥ), विष्णु (viṣṇu), nominative singular masculine (noun, tatpuruṣa compound part)  
शृङ्खलयोगः (śṛṅkhalayogaḥ), शृङ्खला (śṛṅkhalā) + योग (yoga), nominative singular masculine (bahuvrīhi compound)  
अयम् (ayam), अयम् (ayam), nominative singular masculine (demonstrative pronoun)  
भाद्रे (bhādre), भाद्र (bhādra), locative singular masculine (adjective)  
शुक्ले (śukle), शुक्ल (śukla), locative singular masculine (adjective)  
प्रकीर्तितः (prakīrtitaḥ), प्र + कीर्त (root) + ta (participle), nominative singular masculine (perfect passive participle)  

Touching the Ekādaśī, having been touched (referring to the Ekādaśī), (then) on the Dvādaśī by hearing (श्रवणेन, instrumental of means), if (चेत्)  
this Viṣṇu chain-yoga (शृङ्खलयोगः, compound of chain and yoga) (which is) celebrated/known (प्रकीर्तितः) in Bhādra and Śukla (both auspicious tithis).  

If one touches the Ekādaśī, and the Dvādaśī is touched by hearing, this chain-yoga of Viṣṇu is said to be celebrated in Bhādra and Śukla (lunar fortnights).  

If one touches Ekādaśī, and the Dvādaśī is experienced by hearing, this Viṣṇu chain-yoga is celebrated on the auspicious bright fortnights.
```
