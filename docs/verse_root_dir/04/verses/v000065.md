# Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
SOURCE_PAGES: page_0130.txt

=== मूलपाठः ===


महदतिक्रमश्चात्र हेयताबीजम्।
हीनाङ्घो ह्यधिकाङ्घश्च कपटी कृष्णदन्तवान् ।
नेत्ररोगी च कुष्ठी च वावद्रूकश्च वामनः ।।६५।।

mahadatikramaścātra heyatābījam।
hīnāṅgho hyadhikāṅghaśca kapaṭī kṛṣṇadantavān ।
netrarogī ca kuṣṭhī ca vāvadrūkśca vāmanaḥ ।।65।।

महदतिक्रमश्चात्र (mahad-atikrama+śca+atra): 
महद- (mahad), adjective, nominative singular neuter, "great"
अतिक्रम (atikrama), noun, nominative singular neuter, "transgression, overstepping"
श्च (śca), indeclinable conjunction, "and"
अत्र (atra), indeclinable adverb, "here"

हेयताबीजम् (heyatābījam): 
हेय (heya), adjective, nominative singular neuter, "to be avoided"
बीज (bīja), noun, nominative singular neuter, "seed; cause"

हीनाङ्घः (hīnāṅghaḥ): 
हीन (hīna), adjective, nominative singular masculine, "defective, lacking"
अङ्घ (aṅgha), noun, nominative singular masculine, "limb"

हि (hi), indeclinable particle, "indeed, surely"
अधिकाङ्घः (adhikāṅghaḥ): 
अधिक (adhika), adjective, nominative singular masculine, "excessive"
अङ्घ (aṅgha), noun, nominative singular masculine, "limb"

श्च (śca), indeclinable conjunction, "and"

कपटी (kapaṭī): 
कपटी (kapaṭī), noun/adjective, nominative singular masculine, "deceptive person"

कृष्णदन्तवान् (kṛṣṇadantavān): 
कृष्ण (kṛṣṇa), adjective, nominative singular masculine, "black"
दन्त (danta), noun, combining stem, "tooth"
वान् (vān), suffix indicating possession, "having"

नेत्ररोगी (netrarogī): 
नेत्र (netra), noun, nominative singular masculine, "eye"
रोगी (rogī), noun/adjective, nominative singular masculine, "diseased, sick"

च (ca), indeclinable conjunction, "and"

कुष्ठी (kuṣṭhī): 
कुष्ठ (kuṣṭha), noun/adjective, nominative singular feminine, "leper, leprous"

च (ca), indeclinable conjunction, "and"

वावद्रूकः (vāvadrūkaḥ): 
वावद्रूक (vāvadrūka), noun, nominative singular masculine, "a kind of disease or affliction"

श्च (śca), indeclinable conjunction, "and"

वामनः (vāmanaḥ): 
वामन (vāmana), noun, nominative singular masculine, "dwarf"

Literal translation:
Great transgression and here the seed to be avoided.
Defective limb indeed and excessive limb and the deceitful black-toothed one.
Eye sufferer and leper and Vāvadrūka and dwarf.

Smooth English translation:
Great transgressions are here the seeds to be avoided: defective and excessive limbs, the deceitful black-toothed man, the sufferer of eye diseases, the leper, the afflicted Vāvadrūka, and the dwarf.
```
