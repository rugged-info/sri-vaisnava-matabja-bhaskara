## Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
=== मूलपाठः ===
```

---

## Transliteration (IAST)

```text
महदतिक्रमश्चात्र हेयताबीजम्।
हीनाङ्घो ह्यधिकाङ्घश्च कपटी कृष्णदन्तवान् ।
नेत्ररोगी च कुष्ठी च वावद्रूकश्च वामनः ।।६५।।
```

---

## Literal Translation

महदतिक्रमश्चात्र (mahad-atikrama+śca+atra): 
महद- (mahad), adjective, nominative singular neuter, "great"
अतिक्रम (atikrama), noun, nominative singular neuter, "transgression, overstepping"
श्च (śca), indeclinable conjunction, "and"
अत्र (atra), indeclinable adverb, "here"

---

## Smooth Translation

हेयताबीजम् (heyatābījam): 
हेय (heya), adjective, nominative singular neuter, "to be avoided"
बीज (bīja), noun, nominative singular neuter, "seed; cause"
