# Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
SOURCE_PAGES: page_0182.txt

=== मूलपाठः ===


कृतप्रपत्तिस्मरणं प्रायश्चित्तमथीच्यते ।
परमाप्तैश्च तन्निष्ठः कोविदेस्तेर्मुमुक्षुभिः ।।११५।।

kṛtaprapattismarāṇaṁ prāyaścittamathīcyate ।
paramāptaiśca tannishṭhaḥ kovidestermumukṣubhiḥ ।।115।।

कृतप्रपत्तिस्मरणं (kṛtaprapattis-maraṇaṁ), कृत (kṛta, adjective, past passive participle), प्रपत्तिः (prapattiḥ, noun, nominative singular, feminine), स्मरणं (smaraṇaṁ, noun, nominative singular, neuter); tatpuruṣa compound  
प्रायश्चित्तम् (prāyaścittam), noun, nominative singular, neuter  
अथीच्यते (athīcyate), verb, present passive indicative, 3rd person singular  
परमाप्तैः (paramāptaiḥ), adjective plural instrumental masculine or neuter plural instrumental; here instrumental plural masculine/neuter, means "with completely ended (sins)"  
इच (ica), indeclinable particle, "also"  
तत् (tat), pronoun, neuter nominative singular  
निष्ठः (niṣṭhaḥ), adjective, nominative singular masculine  
कोविदेः (kovideḥ), noun, genitive singular, masculine, "expert, knower"  
ते (te), pronoun, nominative plural masculine, "they"  
मुमुक्षुभिः (mumukṣubhiḥ), noun, instrumental plural masculine, "liberation seekers"

Literal translation (with grammatical notes):  
"The remembering of committed wrongdoing (कृतप्रपत्तिस्मरणं - tatpuruṣa compound: remembrance of committed misdeed) is generally considered (अथीच्यते - passive verb) as expiation (प्रायश्चित्तम् - nominative) . Among those who have completely ended (sins) (परमाप्तैः - instrumental plural) and who are well-established (निष्ठः - nominative singular masculine) in that (तत्), you (कोविदेः - genitive singular masculine) are experts; for those (ते - nominative plural masculine) who desire liberation (मुमुक्षुभिः - instrumental plural masculine)."

Smooth English translation:  
"Remembering committed misdeeds is generally regarded as expiation. You are the experts in that discipline for those seeking liberation who have completely ended their sins."
```
