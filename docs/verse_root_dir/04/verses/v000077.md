## Verse — मूलपाठः

=== मूलपाठः ===

## Transliteration (IAST)

## Smooth Translation

He (sa) (is) measured five times five (pañcappañcapramitaḥ) in years (hayuṣaḥ) by (with) the wise ones (budhaiḥ); but the time (kālaḥ) (of) the dawn (aruṇodayaḥ) is measured six times five (ṣaṭpañcamitaḥ).  
But the morning (prātastu) is measured seven (in) the months (sapteṣu-mitaḥ); the sunrise (sūryodayaḥ) is said (nigadyate) (to be so); may it be so (syāt) then (tataḥ), indeed (tathā), beyond (param).
