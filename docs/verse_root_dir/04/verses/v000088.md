```text

=== मूलपाठः ===


कृतार्थयितुं जात इति भावः  
तामष्टमीवेधयुतां विहाय व्रतोत्सवं तत्र करोतु भक्तः ।  
असङ्ख्यसूर्यग्रहतोऽधिका वे या केवला सा नवमीत्युपोष्या ।।८८।।  

kṛtārthyituṃ jāta iti bhāvaḥ  
tāmaṣṭamīvedhayutāṃ vihāya vratotsavaṃ tatra karotu bhaktaḥ ।  
asaṅkhyasūryagrahato'dhikā ve yā kevalā sā navmītyupoṣyā ।।88।।

जात (jāta), जात (participle), प्रथमा एकवचन पुरुषम्  
इति (iti), इति (particle), अव्यय  
भावः (bhāvaḥ), भाव (masculine, nominative singular), सामान्य पुरुष (subject)  
तामष्टमीवेधयुतां (tāmaṣṭamīvedhayutāṃ), ताम् (accusative singular feminine) + अष्टमी (name of the eighth lunar day) + वेध (sacrificial observation) + युतां (past passive participle, accusative singular feminine), बहुव्रीहि  
विहाय (vihāya), विहाय (gerund), अव्यय

भक्तः (bhaktaḥ), भक्त (masculine nominative singular)

नवमीत्युपोष्या (navamītyupoṣyā), नवमी (ninth lunar day) + तपस्य (gerund or feminine adjective), अव्यय/बहुव्रीहि (compound of ordinal and verbal noun)  

(1) कृतार्थयितुं जात इति भावः  
(A feeling/perception that) (2) the one born to fulfill (a purpose) (is born) (3) so (is) the state/feeling.  

(4) तामष्टमीवेधयुतां विहाय व्रतोत्सवं तत्र करोतु भक्तः  
(The devotee) (giving up) (the sacrificial observation connected with the Ashtami) (should perform) (the vow-festival) there.  

(5) असङ्ख्यसूर्यग्रहतोऽधिका वे या केवला सा नवमीत्युपोष्या  
(Which) (unequalled by the countless sun and planets) (alone that) (female) (is) (the one) (to be fasted on the Navami day).  

The feeling is that the one born for fulfillment (of purpose) is born.  
The devotee, forsaking the sacrificial observances connected with the eighth lunar day, should perform the vow festival there.  
That which surpasses the countless sun and planets, she alone is to be observed by fasting on the ninth lunar day.
```
