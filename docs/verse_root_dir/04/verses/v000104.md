# Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
SOURCE_PAGES: page_0161.txt

=== मूलपाठः ===


ते. २.१.१) इति श्रुतेः।  
भवन्त्युपायान्तरमेव सर्वे स्वातन्तर्यतो मुक्तिपदप्रदास्ते ।  
सुकर्मसंवेदनभक्तियोगाः प्रपत्तिनिठैः समनुष्टितास्तु ।।१०४।।  

te. 2.1.1) iti śruteḥ।  
bhvantiupāyāntarameva sarve svātantryato muktipadapradāste ।  
sukarmasaṃvedanabhaktiyogāḥ prapattiniṭhaiḥ samanuṣṭitāstu ।।104।।  

(ते) (te), तैः (taḥ) pronoun, nominative plural masculine;  
(इति) (iti), इति indeclinable (quotation particle);  
(श्रुतेः) (śruteḥ), श्रुति (śruti), feminine singular locative;  
(भवन्ति) (bhvanti), भव (bhū), verb root, present tense, indicative, active, third person plural;  
(उपायान्तरम्) (upāyāntaram), उपायान्तर (upāyāntara), neuter singular nominative;  
(इव) (iva), इव indeclinable (simile/like);  
(सर्वे) (sarve), सर्व (sarva), adjective, nominative plural masculine;  
(स्वातन्त्र्यतः) (svātantryataḥ), स्वतंत्र (svātantrya), neuter singular ablative;  
(मुक्तिपद) (muktipada), मुक्तिपद (muktipada), neuter singular nominative;  
(प्रदाः) (pradāḥ), प्रदान (pradā), adjective, nominative plural masculine (acting as a verb participle or verbal adjective derived from root दा giving ‘givers’); [here functioning as a verbal root participle]  
(ते) (te), तैः (taḥ) pronoun, nominative plural masculine;  
(सुकर्म) (sukarma), सु (su) + कर्म (karma), compound tatpuruṣa, neuter plural nominative;  
(संवेदन) (saṃvedana), संवेदन (saṃvedana), neuter plural nominative;  
(भक्ति) (bhakti), भक्ति (bhakti), feminine plural nominative;  
(योगाः) (yogāḥ), योग (yoga), masculine plural nominative;  
(प्रपत्तिनिठैः) (prapattiniṭhaiḥ), प्रपत्तिनिठ (prapattiniṭha), masculine plural instrumental;  
(समनुष्टिताः) (samanuṣṭitāḥ), समनुष्ट (samanuṣṭa), adjective or past participle, nominative plural masculine;  
(तु) (tu), तु indeclinable (emphatic particle);  

"ते (they) इति (thus, according to) श्रुतेः (in the śruti / scripture)."  
"भवन्ति (are) उपायान्तरम् (alternative means) एव (indeed) सर्वे (all) स्वातन्त्र्यतः (from freedom) मुक्तिपदप्रदास्ते (givers/dispensers of the path to liberation)."  
"सुकर्म-संवेदन-भक्ति-योगाः (good-deeds sensing devotion paths) प्रपत्तिनिठैः (by the firmly devoted to surrender) समनुष्टितास्तु (indeed prescribed/established)."  

Thus:  
They (are) thus stated in the śruti.  
All (those) alternative means (to liberation), indeed, (are) dispensers of the path of liberation arising from independence.  
The yoga paths of good deeds, perception, and devotion are indeed practiced by those firmly devoted to surrender.  

They are thus declared in the śruti: all alternative means are indeed dispensers of the path of liberation born of freedom.  
The yogas of good actions, perception, and devotion are indeed practiced by those firmly dedicated to surrender.
```
