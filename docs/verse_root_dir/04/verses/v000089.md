## Transliteration (IAST)

```text
कृतार्थयितुं जात इति भावः  
तामष्टमीवेधयुतां विहाय व्रतोत्सवं तत्र करोतु भक्तः ।  
असङ्ख्यसूर्यग्रहतोऽधिका वे या केवला सा नवमीत्युपोष्या ।।८८।।
```

---

## Literal Translation

कृतार्थयितुं (kṛtārthayituṃ), कृतार्थयितुम् (infinitive form of the verb कृतार्थयति - causative of कृ meaning 'to make accomplished or satisfied'), कृतार्थ (verbal root), infinitive  
जात (jāta), जात (past participle of √जन् 'to be born' or 'to arise'), perfect passive participle, masculine singular nominative  
इति (iti), इति (indeclinable, particle meaning 'thus' or 'so')  
भावः (bhāvaḥ), भाव (masculine singular nominative), noun meaning 'feeling, sense, intention'

---

## Smooth Translation

तामष्टमीवेधयुतां (tāmaṣṭamīvedhayutāṃ), तम् (demonstrative pronoun, accusative singular feminine) + अष्टमी (aṣṭamī, feminine noun, nominative/accusative singular meaning 'eighth lunar day') + वेध (veda: 'penetrate, pierce' or 'marking' here probably part of compound) + युतां (yutāṃ, past participle, feminine singular accusative of युत - 'joined, united'), compound tatpuruṣa or bahuvrīhi (need context)  
विहाय (vihāya), verb from √वि-हृ (separating), gerund, ablative absolute participle meaning 'having abandoned'  
व्रतोत्सवं (vratotsavaṃ), व्रत (vrata), masculine noun, accusative singular + उत्सव (utsava), masculine noun, accusative singular; compound tatpuruṣa meaning 'vow festival'  
तत्र (tatra), indeclinable adverb meaning 'there'  
करोतु (karotu), कृ verb, imperative mood, 3rd person singular, active voice meaning 'let him do'  
भक्तः (bhaktaḥ), भक्त (masculine singular nominative), noun meaning 'devotee, servant'
