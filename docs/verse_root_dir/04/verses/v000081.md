# Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
SOURCE_PAGES: page_0146.txt

=== मूलपाठः ===


तत्र सूर्यप्रकाशदर्शनादपूर्व यद् घटीद्रयं स दण्डद्रयात्मककालोऽतिवेधः, रविप्र भासस्य  
दर्शनादनन्तरमर्थे सूर्य उदिते महावेध इति बुधैः कालवेर्तृभिरीर्यते।  
योगस्तुरीयस्तु दिवाकरोदये तेऽर्वाक्सुदोषातिशयार्थबोधकाः ।  
सर्वेऽपि वेधा मुनिभिर्विनिश्चिता निर्णेतृभिस्तस्य तु तत्त्वदर्शिभिः ।।८०।।

tatra sūryaprakāśadarśanādapūrva yad ghaṭīdrayaṁ sa daṇḍadrayātmakakālo'tivedhaḥ, ravipra bhāsasya  
darśanānanntaramarthe sūrya udite mahāvedha iti budhaiḥ kālavertṛbhirīryate।  
yogasturīyastu divākarodaye te'rvāksudoṣātiśayārthabodhakāḥ ।  
sarve'pi vedhā munibhirviniścitā nirṇetṛbhistasya tu tattvadarśibhiḥ ।।80।।

तत्र (tatra), तत्र, indecl. locative adv. (there)  
सूर्यप्रकाशदर्शनादपूर्व (sūryaprakāśadarśanādapūrva) - सूर्यप्रकाश (sūryaprakāśa, m. compound tatpuruṣa: सूर्य (sun) + प्रकाश (light)) + दर्शन (darśana, m. compound tatpuruṣa: दर्शन (seeing/appearance)) + आदि (ādi, ablative suffix) + पूर्व (pūrva, adj: before)  
यद् (yad), यत्, indecl. relative pronoun, nominative/accusative neuter singular (which/that)  
घटीद्रयं (ghaṭīdrayaṁ) - घटीद्रय (ghaṭī-draya, n. compound bahuvrīhi: घटी (hourglass, time-measurer) + द्वय (dwaya=two)) nominative singular neuter (the two hourglasses)  
स (sa), स, pronoun nominative singular masculine (he/that)  
दण्डद्रयात्मककालः (daṇḍadrayātmakakālaḥ) - दण्डद्रयात्मक (daṇḍa-drayātmaka, adj. compound tatpuruṣa: दण्ड (staff) + द्वय (two) + आत्मक (consisting of)) + कालः (kālaḥ, m: time) nominative singular masculine (the time consisting of the two staffs)  
अतिवेधः (ativedhaḥ), अतिवेधः, nominative singular masculine, compound bahuvrīhi (great piercer/penetrator)  
रविप्र (ravipra) - रवि (ravi, m: sun) + प्र (pra, postposition prefix) genetive singular masculine (of the sun)  
भासस्य (bhāsasya) भासस् (bhāsa) + suffix, genitive singular masculine (of brightness)  
दर्शनादनन्तरम् (darśanānanntaram) - दर्शन (darśana, n) + अनन्तरम् (anantara, adv) (after seeing)  
अर्थे (arthe) अर्थ (artha, m) locative singular (for the purpose)  
सूर्य (sūrya), सूर्य (sūrya, m) nominative singular masculine (sun)  
उदिते (udite) उदित (udita, adj part.) locative singular (at rising)  
महावेध (mahāvedha) महा (mahā, adj: great) + वेध (vedha, m: piercing) nominative singular masculine (great piercing)  
इति (iti), indecl. particle (thus)  
बुधैः (budhaiḥ), बुद्धि (buddhi, f.) instrumental plural (by the wise)  
कालवेर्तृभिः (kālavertṛbhiḥ), काल (kāla, m) + लेखक (vertṛ) + instrumental plural masculine (by the time-deciders)  
इर्यते (iryate), verb ir + yaṇa, present passive indicative 3rd singular (is said)  
योगः (yogaḥ), योग (yoga, m) nominative singular masculine (union)  
तुरीयः (turīyaḥ), adjective nominative singular masculine (fourth)  
तु (tu), indecl. conjunction (but)  
दिवाकरोदय (divākarodaya) - दिवाकर (divākara, m: sun) + उदय (udaya, m: rising) locative singular (at the sunrise)  
ते (te), pronoun nominative plural masculine (those)  
अर्वाक्सु (arvāksu) nominal form uncertain - possibly मृषाद्विषयं or variant, treated here as a noun, nominative plural masculine (unclear, likely "errors/flaws")  
दोṣातिशयार्थबोधकाः (doṣātiśayārthabodhakāḥ) - दोष (doṣa, m: fault) + अतिशय (atiśaya, m: excess) + अर्थ (artha, m: meaning) + बोधक (bodhaka, m: knower, revealer) nominative plural masculine (revealers of the meaning of excessive faults)  
सर्वेऽपि (sarve'pi), सर्व (sarva) pronoun, nominative plural masculine + भीन declension particle (all indeed)  
वेधा (vedhā), वेध (vedha, m.) nominative plural masculine (piercings/obstacles/queries)  
मुनिभिः (munibhiḥ), मुनि (muni, m.) instrumental plural (by sages)  
विनिश्चिता (viniścitā), verb ppp, nominative plural feminine (determined)  
निर्णेतृभि: (nirṇetṛbhiḥ), निर्णेतृ (nirṇetṛ, m: deciders) instrumental plural (by deciders)  
तस्य (tasya), pronoun genitive singular masculine (of it)  
तु (tu), indecl. conjunction (but)  
तत्त्वदर्शिभिः (tattvadarśibhiḥ), तत्त्वदर्शी (tattvadarśī, m) instrumental plural (by truth-seers)  
।।८०।। (80, verse number)

There (tatra), before the seeing of sunlight (sūryaprakāśadarśanāt pūrva), that which (yad) is the set of two hourglasses (ghaṭīdrayaṁ), that (sa) time characterized by the two staffs (daṇḍadrayātmakakālaḥ) is the super-piercer (ativedhaḥ) of the brightness of the sun (ravi pra bhāsasya). For the purpose of seeing (darśanāt anantara arthē), after the sun (sūrya) has risen (udite), the great piercing (mahāvedha) thus is said (iti budhaiḥ kālavertṛbhir īryate). The fourth union (yogasturīyaḥ) however (tu) at the sunrise (divākara udaye), these (te) are the revealers (bodhakāḥ) of the meaning (artha) of excessive faults (doṣātiśaya). All (sarve api) piercings (vedhā) are certainly (viniścitā) determined (viniścitā) by the sages (munibhiḥ) and deciders (nirṇetṛbhiḥ) but of that (tasya tu) by the truth-seers (tattvadarśibhiḥ).

There, the two hourglasses before the sight of the sunlight, that time characterized by those staffs is the great piercer of the sun's brightness. For the purpose of seeing, after the sun has risen, that great piercing is recognized by wise time-deciders. The fourth yoga, however, at sunrise, they are the revealers of the knowledge of excessive faults. All piercings are indeed firmly determined by sages and decision-makers, but that is by the truth-seers.

There, before the sight of the sunlight, the time marked by the two staffs is the great piercer of the sun's brightness. After the sun has risen, this great piercing is recognized by the wise time-measurers for the purpose of seeing. The fourth yoga, at sunrise, is the revealer of the meaning of excessive faults. All piercings are certainly determined by sages and decision-makers, but of it (the piercing) by the truth-seers.
```
