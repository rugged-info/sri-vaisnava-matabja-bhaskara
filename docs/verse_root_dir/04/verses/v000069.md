=== मूलपाठः ===


भीरुणेति भावनासंप्लुतशुश्रूषात्रपेश्षिता फलदेति भावः। दश्यते च भक्तिमतामपि स्वातन्व्येण  
स्वविवेकेन सेवनम्, तादशस्वातन्त्यदोषपरिहारपूर्वकाज्ञावर्तित्वमाह - ओज्ञाकारीति। एतेनोक्तं  
न व्यभिचरतीति यथीक्तकारित्वं श्रौ तलक्षणं परामृष्टम्। विशुद्धात्येत्यनेन प्रशान्तचित्तायेव्यर्थ  
उक्तः, शमाव्वितयेव्यर्थ प्रकाशयितुमुच्यते - जितेद्धिय इति। न विलासी विषयसेवी वा  
गुरुसेवया परमार्थ लप्स्यत इति भावः। एतेन नियमितमनोवृत्तितया वैराग्यं सूचितम्।  
विकारेहेतावुपस्थिते यो न विक्रियते तादशधीरजनगुणमाह अनुल्वण इति। अक्रोध इति भावः।  
प्रतिकूलतायां परैः पीड्यमानस्याप्यविकृतचित्तत्वरूप-मनुद्रेगकरत्वं प्रकाशितम्  
अतीक्षणचेष्टितत्वं हि सेवायाः प्रमुखो गुणः। शिष्यतेऽसाविति कर्मव्युत्पत्या  
शासर्नाहत्वप्रकाशनेनापि काङ्गित्तगुणाधान-पात्रत्वं तस्य लभ्यते। गुरूपदेशापूर्व  
भजनीयनिश्रयस्य तद्धक्तिस्वरूपस्य बोधाभावेऽपि भक्तिवान् स्यामिति  
देवसामान्यविषयाग्रहस्य दष्टत्वात्सर्वगुणसत्वेऽपि भक्तौ निष्ठाया अतितरामपेक्षितत्वादन्ते  
तदनिवार्यतामाह भक्तिनिष्ठशिष्यो भवेदिति। नियता स्थितिरेव निष्ठा,  
स्थिरपरिगृहीतमुपायानुष्ठानम्, भक्तावव्यहितोपाये निष्ठा यस्य, तादृशः कार्य इति।  
यद्रैवंगुणविशिष्टोऽपि शिष्यः फलरूपतया भक्तिनिष्ठः कार्य इति कश्चिदाचार्यधर्मोऽपि सूचितः,  
तत्परतासिद्धयेन्य-गुणसाफल्यात्तत्रैव विश्रान्तेः। इत्थं भगवत्समाश्रयणकामनया सम्प्राप्तजीवेषु  
शिष्यलक्षणमनुसन्धाय सम्परीक्षय च संस्कारमुखेन तत्त्वज्ञानाधानेन च ते भगवद्धक्तौ नेयाः,  
नान्यथीदेश्यसिद्धरिति लभ्यते। यस्य मनश्शान्तमिद्धियजातं विषयेभ्यो निवृत्त, यश्च  
स्वयमुपरतः प्रियाप्रियघटनाभिरविचलितो भवेत्, यस्य चित्तमात्पभ्ूतपरमात्मोपलब्धये  
तदनुरूपसाधनेषु संलग्रं तादशमुमुक्षीरनुरूपशिष्यत्वमिति फलितार्थः।  
परीक्ष्य शिष्यं समुपासकं गुरुर्वर्ष समभ्यर्च्य च वहिदेवताम् ।  
चापादिर्भिहतिवरेः सुतापितैर्दिने सुपुण्ये नियतः समङ्कयेत् ।।६९।।

bhīruṇeti bhāvanāsaṃplutaśuśrūṣātrapeśṣitā phalade ti bhāvaḥ। daśyate ca bhaktimatāmapi svātanvyeṇa  
svavivekena sevanaṃ, tādaśasvātantyadoṣaparihārapūrvakājñāvartitvamāha - o-jñākārīti। etenoktaṃ  
na vyabhicaratīti yathīkṭakāritvaṃ śrau talakṣaṇaṃ parāmrṣṭam। viśuddhātyetyanena praśāntacittāyevyaṛtha  
uktaḥ, shamāvvitayevyaṛtha prakāśayitumucyate - jiteddhiya iti। na vilāsī viṣayasēvī vā  
gurusevayā paramārtha lapseyata iti bhāvaḥ। etena niyamitamanovṛttitayā vairāgyaṃ sūcitam।  
vikārehētāvupasthite yo na vikriyate tādaśadhīrajānaguṇamāha anulvaṇa iti। akrodha iti bhāvaḥ।  
pratikūlatāyāṃ paraiḥ pīḍyamānasyāpyavikṛtacittatvarūpa-manudregakaratvaṃ prakāśitam  
atīkṣaṇaceṣṭitatvaṃ hi sevāyāḥ pramukho guṇaḥ। śiṣyate’sāviti karmavyutptyā  
śāsarnāhatvaprakāśanenaāpi kāṅgittaguṇādhāna-pātratvaṃ tasya labhyate। gurūpadeśāpūrva  
bhajanīyanishrayasya taddhaktisvarūpasya bodhābhāve’pi bhaktivān syāmīti  
devasāmānyaviṣayāgrahasya daṣṭatvātsarvaguṇasatve’pi bhaktau niṣṭhāyā atitarāmapekṣitatvādante  
tadanivāryatāmāha bhaktiniṣṭhaśiṣyo bhavediti। niyatā sthitireva niṣṭhā,  
sthiraparigṛhītamupāyānuṣṭhānam, bhaktāvavyahitopāye niṣṭhā yasya, tādṛśaḥ kārya iti।  
yadraivaṃguṇaviśiṣṭo’pi śiṣyaḥ phalarūpatayā bhaktiniṣṭhaḥ kārya iti kaścidācāryadharmo’pi sūcitaḥ,  
tatparatāsiddhayenyaguṇasāphalyāttatraiva viśrānteḥ। itthaṃ bhagavatsamāśrayaṇakāmanayā samprāptajīveṣu  
śiṣyalakṣaṇanusandhāya samparīkṣaya ca saṃskāramukhena tattvajñānādhānena ca te bhagavaddhaktau neyāḥ,  
nānyathīdeśyasiddhariti labhyate। yasya manaśśāntamid'dhiyajātaṃ viṣayebhyo nivṛtta, yaśca  
svayamuparataḥ priyāpriyaghāṭanābhiravicalito bhavet, yasya cittamātpabhūtparamātmopalabdhaye  
tadanurūpasādhanēṣu saṃlagraṃ tādaśamumukṣīranurūpaśiṣyatvamiti phalitārthaḥ।  
parīkṣya śiṣyaṃ samupāsakaṃ gururvarṣa samabhyarcya ca vahidevatām ।  
chāpādirbihi tivarēḥ sutāpitairdinē supuṇye niyataḥ samaṅkayēt ।।69।।

Literal translation (close, with grammatical notes):  
" Bhīruṇeti — 'Fearfulness' (quoted), the feeling immersed in devoted service, not expecting fruit (is called bhāva, feeling). It is also seen among those devoted, with self-reliance and self-discernment, the worship (sevanam) performed. He said that such was the continuance of obedience, accompanied by avoidance of faults in independence — called 'obedient-ness'. This statement is not deviated from; hear carefully, it is a sign fully considered.  
Said as intended for a peaceful mind, it is pointless to show it only to achieve calmness — called the conqueror of the intellect. It is not a luxury-lover or a sensual server, nor will he achieve highest purpose by serving guru (the spiritual teacher). Thus, by fixed mental disposition, dispassion is indicated.  
He who, in presence of cause of transformation, does not act, is termed a wise individual — called 'angri-less'. Even when oppressed by others in adversity, his unaltered mind nature and tranquil activity are manifested. Keen effort is indeed the chief quality of service.  
It is cultivated, and by prohibition and negation of commands, the capacity for possessing excellence is obtained. Prior to guru's instruction, even lacking understanding of the form of devotion, one may be devoted.  
Because seeing of the grasp of ordinary worldly objects, even in the presence of all virtues, devotion's steadfastness is expected greatly to the end, its indispensability is said: the devoted steadfast disciple should be.  
Fixed steadfastness alone, firm adoption of methods, and devotion without improper means, for such one the work is.  
Even if a disciple distinguished by qualities is steadfast in devotion by the form of fruit, it is said by some teacher duty. By the superior realization of qualities' fruits, the peaceful one rests there.  
Thus, by desire for refuge in the Lord, examining signs of disciples in attained beings, inspecting by impressions, and foundation in true knowledge, those in devotion to God should be taken; not otherwise is attainment by direction possible.  
Whose mind is peaceful, intellect born, withdrawn from objects, and who personally remains unmoved by pleasant or unpleasant events, and whose mind, for realization of the supreme self only as mind, firmly attached in corresponding means, is such a suitable disciple to the seeker — this is the intended meaning.  
After examining the disciple, the guru carefully worshipping annually and worshipping the presiding deities, on auspicious days like that of the bow and others, by sun and fire, should regularly assemble (them). "

Smooth English translation:  
“The term ‘bhīruṇeti’ refers to the feeling immersed in devoted service without expectation of reward. Such devotion is observed even among the devoted who worship with self-reliance and discernment. It is said that this is the continued obedience practiced while avoiding faults in independence — called obedience. This statement is firmly established and should be attentively heard as a clear sign.  
It is taught that this feeling is intended for one with a peaceful mind; merely showing it to gain calmness is pointless — this is called the conquest of the intellect. Such a devotee is neither indulgent nor sensual, nor will merely serving a guru lead to the highest spiritual goal. Rather, dispassion is indicated by a fixed mental disposition.  
One who remains inactive amid circumstances causing change is termed wise and free of anger. Even when oppressed by others in hardship, such a person’s mind remains unaltered and his subtle actions steadfast. Keen effort is the principal quality of service.  
Devotion is cultivated, and by prohibiting and negating improper commands, one attains suitability for possessing excellence. Even before explicit instruction from a guru, one may be a devotee.  
Because devotees require steadfastness greatly, from start to finish, especially given the lure of ordinary worldly objects, the necessity of steadfastness in devotion is affirmed: the disciple with steadfastness in devotion should be.  
Fixed steadiness, firm adherence to proper methods, and devotion without improper means — for such a one, the task is prescribed.  
Even a disciple distinguished by qualities should steadfastly maintain devotion by the result it bears, as is stated by some teacher’s duty. The peaceful person rests relying on the supreme attainment of excellence.  
Thus, motivated by desire for refuge in God, the guru must examine signs of disciples in those beings who have arrived, inspect their impressions, and based on true knowledge, accept them for devotion to God; attainment cannot be otherwise assured.  
One whose mind is peaceful, intellect enlightened, withdrawn from worldly objects, who remains unaffected by likes and dislikes, and whose mind is firmly attached to means suited to realization of the supreme self — such a disciple suits the seeker.  
Having examined the disciple, the guru should annually worship with care and offer worship to the presiding deities on auspicious days like the bow festival and others, by the sun and fire, regularly assembling them.”
