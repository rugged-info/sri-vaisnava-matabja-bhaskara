## Smooth Translation

There, before the sight of the sunlight (sūryaprakāśadarśanādapūrva), that pot-group (ghaṭīdrayaṃ) is (sa) a time (kālaḥ) characterized by a group of rods (daṇḍadrayātmakakālaḥ), an excessive penetration (ativedhaḥ) of the sun's intense shining (raviḥ pra bhāsasya). For the purpose following the sight (darśanādanantaramarthe), at sunrise (divākarodaye) the sun (sūryaḥ), the great penetration (mahāvedaḥ), thus (iti) is observed (iryate) by the wise boundary-controllers of time (budhaiḥ kālavertṛbhir). The fourth union (turīya yogaḥ) indeed (tu), at the rise of the sun (divākarodaye), these (te) are those who reveal meaning (arthabodhakāḥ) by excess (atiśaya) caused by atmospheric defects (arvāksudoṣāt). All (sarve api) these penetrations (vedhāḥ) have been determined (viniścitāḥ) by sages (munibhiḥ) and judges (nirṇetṛbhis) but of that (tasya) are by seekers of truth (tattvadarśibhiḥ).
