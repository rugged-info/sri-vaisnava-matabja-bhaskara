## Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
=== मूलपाठः ===
```

---

## Transliteration (IAST)

```text
यतेद्ियः शुचिः शुद्धवेशधृक्सुकुलोद्धवः।
सदाचारपरो नम्रः शास्तज्ञो देशनापटः ।। ६३।।
```

---

## Literal Translation

यतेदियः (yatediyaḥ), यतेदिय (vacat; tatpuruṣa compound) — nominative masculine singular  
शुचिः (śuciḥ), शुचि (adjective: pure, clean) — nominative masculine singular  
शुद्धवेशधृक्सुकुलोद्धवः (śuddhaveśadhṛksukuloddhavaḥ), शुद्ध (pure) + वेश (dress) + धृ (to hold) + सुखुल (clan) + उद्‌धव (rising/uplifted); compound (bahuvrīhi / complex tatpuruṣa); nominative masculine singular  
सदाचारपरो (sadācāraparo), सत् (good) + आचार (conduct) + पर (devoted to); tatpuruṣa compound, nominative masculine singular  
नम्रः (namraḥ), नम्र (humble) — nominative masculine singular  
शास्तज्ञः (śāstajñaḥ), शास्त्र (scripture) + ज्ञ (knower); tatpuruṣa compound, nominative masculine singular  
देशनापटः (deśanāpaṭaḥ), देशन (preacher) + अपट (expert) — tatpuruṣa compound, nominative masculine singular

---

## Smooth Translation

(yatediyaḥ śuciḥ śuddhaveśadhṛksukuloddhavaḥ) — Yatediya, pure, wearing pure garments from the Sukula clan (Yatediya is pure, pure-garment-wearing member of the Sukula clan);  
(sadācāraparo namraḥ śāstajñaḥ deśanāpaṭaḥ) — devoted to good conduct, humble, knower of scriptures, expert preacher.
