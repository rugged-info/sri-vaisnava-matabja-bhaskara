## Smooth Translation

Literal translation:  
She (sā) is like an oil-stream (tailadhārā) — (which is) not permanent (amanitya), of the memory (saṃsmṛteḥ), of the form of offspring (santānarūpeḥ), great devotion (parānuraktiḥ).  
Devotion (bhaktiḥ), beginning with (vivikādi) the seven-born (saptajanyā) and also (tathā) the eight easy-to-understand rules/methods (yamādyaṣṭa subodhakāṅghā).
