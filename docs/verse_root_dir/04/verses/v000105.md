## Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
=== मूलपाठः ===
```

---

## Transliteration (IAST)

```text
विभुत्वतो निर्भरतापरैस्तैः श्रीव्याप्तिरायैरभिधीयते हि ।
प्रपञ्चनिर्मातृविरिच्चिहेतुश्रीरामपादान्जनिविष्टचित्तैः ।।१०५।।
```

---

## Literal Translation

विभुत्वतः (vibhutvataḥ), vibhutva, abl. sg. (from vibhutvaḥ - "sovereignty, power")
निर्भरतापरैः (nirbharatāparaistaiḥ), nirbharatāpara, inst. pl. (compound tatpuruṣa: nir + bharatā + apara - "other than bearing/support")
श्रीव्याप्तिः (śrīvyāptiḥ), śrīvyāpti, nom. sg. (śrī + vyāpti - "glorious pervasion/omnipresence")
आयैः (āyaiḥ), āya, inst. pl. (from āy - "force, influence"; instrumental plural)
अभिधीयते (abhidhīyate), abhidhīyate, 3rd person singular, present indicative passive (verb root: bhid - "to speak", "to express")
हि (hi), hi, particle (emphatic)

---

## Smooth Translation

प्रपञ्चनिर्मातृविरि (prapañcanirmātṛviri), prapañcanirmātṛvirī, nom. sg. compound bahuvrīhi (prapañca + nirmātṛ + virī - "creator of the universe", lit. "woman/energy of the creator of the world")
च (ca), ca, indeclinable (conjunctive "and")
हेतु (hetu), hetu, nom. sg. (m.; "cause")
श्रीरामपादान् (śrīrāmapādān), śrīrāmapāda, acc. pl. (śrīrāma + pāda - "feet of Lord Śrī Rāma")
जनिविष्टचित्तैः (janiviṣṭhacittaiḥ), janiviṣṭhacitta, inst. pl. (compound tatpuruṣa: janiviṣṭha + citta - "with minds fixed on the birthplace")
