# Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
SOURCE_PAGES: page_0128.txt

=== मूलपाठः ===


यतेद्ियः शुचिः शुद्धवेशधृक्सुकुलोद्धवः।
सदाचारपरो नम्रः शास्तज्ञो देशनापटः ।। ६३।।

yatediyaḥ śuciḥ śuddhaveśadhṛksukuloddhavaḥ।
sadācāraparo namraḥ śāstajño deśanāpaṭaḥ ॥ 63॥

यत् (yat), pronoun, nominative plural neuter, “which” + इद् (id), demonstrative pronoun, nominative plural neuter, “these” → यतेद् (together yated), indeclinable compound (tatpuruṣa: relative pronoun + demonstrative)  
इयः (iyaḥ), pronoun, nominative plural masculine, “persons”  
शुचिः (śuciḥ), adjective, nominative plural masculine, “pure”  
शुद्धवेश (śuddhaveśa), compound noun (tatpuruṣa), neuter, nominative plural, “clean-clothed” (शुद्ध (śuddha, pure/clean) + वेश (veśa, dress))  
धृक् (dhṛk), noun, masculine, nominative plural, “head”  
सुकुलोद्धवः (sukuloddhavaḥ), compound noun (bahuvrīhi), masculine, nominative plural, “of good family/noble birth” (सु (su, good) + कुल (kula, family) + उद्धव (uddhava, descended))  
सदाचार (sadācāra), noun, masculine, nominative singular, “good conduct”  
पर (para), adjective, masculine, nominative singular, “devoted”  
नम्रः (namraḥ), adjective, masculine, nominative singular, “humble”  
शास्तज्ञः (śāstajñaḥ), compound noun (tatpuruṣa), masculine, nominative singular: शास्त्र (śāstra, scriptures) + ज्ञ (jña, knower) — “knower of scriptures”  
देशनापटः (deśanāpaṭaḥ), compound, masculine, nominative singular: देशना (deśanā, preaching/speaking) + पट (paṭa, expert) — “skilled orator/preacher”

Literal translation:  
“Those persons which are pure, clean-clothed, with noble head (of good family), devoted to good conduct, humble, knower of scriptures, skillful preacher.”

विरक्तधर्मनिरतो ध्यानाभ्यासी सुबुद्धिमान् ।
निग्रहेऽनुग्रहे चैव समर्थी देशिको मतः ।।६४।।

viraktadharmanirato dhyānābhyāsī subuddhimān ।
nigrahe’nugrahe caiva samarthī deśiko mataḥ ॥ 64॥

विरक्त (virakta), adjective, masculine, nominative singular, “detached”  
धर्म (dharma), noun, masculine, locative singular (धर्मे), here in compound, “duty, righteousness”  
निरतः (nirataḥ), adjective, masculine, nominative singular, “engaged in”  
ध्यानाभ्यासी (dhyānābhyāsī), compound (tatpuruṣa), masculine, nominative singular: ध्यान (dhyāna, meditation) + अभ्यास (abhyāsa, practice) + suffix, “practicing meditation”  
सुबुद्धिमान् (subuddhimān), adjective, masculine, nominative singular, “intelligent” (सु (su, good) + बुद्धि (buddhi, intellect))  
निग्रह (nigraha), noun, masculine, locative singular or nominative singular, “control, restraint”  
अनुग्रह (anugraha), noun, masculine, locative singular or nominative singular, “favor, help”  
च (ca), indeclinable conjunction, “and”  
एव (eva), indeclinable particle, “indeed”  
समर्थी (samarthī), adjective, masculine, nominative singular, “capable”  
देशिको (deśika), adjective, masculine, nominative singular, “preceptor, teacher”  
मतः (mataḥ), noun, masculine, nominative singular, “opinion, view”

Literal translation:  
“Detached from duty, engaged in meditation practice, intelligent; indeed, capable in restraint and favor, the teacher’s opinion.”

Smooth English translation:  
“Those who are pure, well-clothed, noble by birth, devoted to good conduct, humble, knowing the scriptures, and skilled in preaching; detached from worldly duties, committed to meditation practice, intelligent; capable in both self-restraint and kindness — such is the opinion of the teacher.”
```
