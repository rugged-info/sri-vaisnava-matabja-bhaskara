```text
SOURCE_PAGES: page_0183.txt

=== मूलपाठः ===


उत्कृष्टवर्णेरपि वैष्णवेर्जनैर्निकृष्टवर्णः स तदीयसेवने ।
तथानुसर्तव्य इतीष्यते बुधैः शास्तैर्विधेये विधिगोचरः परैः ।।११६।।


utkṛṣṭavarṇerapi vaiṣṇaverjanarnikṛṣṭavarṇaḥ sa tadīyaservane ।
tathānusartavya itīṣyate budhaiḥ śāstairvidheye vidhigocharaḥ paraiḥ ॥116॥

जनैः (janaiḥ), जन, पुं॰, बहुवचनः, तृतीयाभ्यां सन्धिं छेत्ताः सप्तमी
निकृष्टवर्णः (nikṛṣṭavarṇaḥ), निकृष्टर् (निकृष्ट) वर्णः, अकारान्तः पुं॰ एकवचनः, कर्तृ कारकः, तत्पुरुष

विधिगोचरः (vidhigocharaḥ), विधि + गोचर (समास), पुं॰ एकवचन, कर्ता, तत्पुरुष
परैः (paraiḥ), पर, बहुवचन सप्तमी


Even for the superior caste also by Vaishnava people, the inferior caste is (considered) for service to them.  
Thus to be followed this is said by wise men through scriptures; (it is) prescribed, to be observed by others.


Even the inferior caste is to be served by the Vaishnava people among the superior caste.  
Thus, it is stated by the wise through scriptures that this must be followed and observed by others.
```
