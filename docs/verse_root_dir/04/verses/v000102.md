## Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
=== मूलपाठः ===
```

---

## Transliteration (IAST)

```text
कर्मप्रवाहेण तु चेतनस्य सम्मग्रस्य संसारमहार्णवे चिरम् ।
उपर्य्यहो संसरतो वशस्य सत्कृपोद्धवत्येव हरेरहेतुका ।।१०२।।
```

---

## Literal Translation

कर्मप्रवाहेण (karma-pravāheṇa), कर्मप्रवाह (karma-pravāha, noun, masculine, instrumental singular) + (प्र-)suffix + (हेण) instrumental case ending; 
तु (tu), तु (indec. particle);
चेतनस्य (cetanāsya), चेतन (cेतन, noun, masculine/neuter, genitive singular);
सम्मग्रस्य (sammṛgasya), समग्र (स - समग्र, adjective, masculine/neuter, genitive singular);
संसारमहार्णवे (saṁsāramahārṇave), संसार (संसार, noun, masculine, accusative singular) + महा + अर्णव (noun, masculine, locative singular) compounded tatpuruṣa or bahuvrīhi;
चिरम् (ciram), चिर (adjective, masculine/neuter accusative singular);
उपर्य्यहो (uparyyaho), उपरि (prefix) + य्यहो (verb or verbal adverb, possibly causative or frequentative participle)—compound or complex form;
संसरतो (saṁsarato), संसार (verb root or participle, locative singular);
वशस्य (vaśasya), वश (noun, masculine, genitive singular);
सत्कृपोद्धवत्येव (satkṛpoddhavaty-eva), सत् (good), कृप (kindness), उद्दत्त (raised, active participle), एव (indeclinable particle);
हरेरहेतुका (harer-ahetukā), हरे (Hari, noun masculine genitive singular) + हेतुक (cause, adjective) + आ feminine singular ending;

---

## Smooth Translation

Literal translation:  
By the flow of actions (karma-pravāheṇa - instrumental), indeed (tu), of the conscious (cetanāsya - genitive) complete (sammṛgasya - genitive) world-ocean (saṁsāramahārṇave - locative), long time (ciram - accusative), having crossed above (uparyyaho), moving along (saṁsarato - locative) under control (vaśasya - genitive), the kind-reviving (satkṛpoddhavati-eva), cause-less of Hari (harer-ahetukā);
