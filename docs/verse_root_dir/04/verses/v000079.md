# Verse — मूलपाठः

```text

=== मूलपाठः ===


पदपरामर्शः  
अरुणोदयकालत्वेन घटीचतुष्टयात्मको यः कालो नियतस्तत्रैव चत्वारो वेधा भवन्तीति  
बोधयितुमाह - घटीचतुष्कमिति । चतसृणां घटीनां समुदायो घटीचतुष्कम्। कालविमर्शे  
प्रामाण्यं दर्शयितुं महर्षिभिरिव्युक्तम्, न हि तथाविधा मुधाऽचरन्ति। कालगतविशेषन्ते  
्रक्षितुर्महन्ति ऋ तम्भरप्रज्ञात्वात्। तादृशैः प्रातर्घदीचतुष्कमरुणोदयनामा समयो निर्धारितस्तत्र  
विपश्चितो वेधप्रभृतेर्विवेकतश्चतुरो विभागान् प्राहुरित्यन्वयः। विपश्चितः कालरहस्यविदः।  
विवेकतो विवेकेनेति तृतीयायां सार्वविभक्तिकस्तसिः।  
घटीत्रयं सार्दधमथारुणोदये वेधीऽतिवेधः कथितो घटीद्रयम् ।  
रविप्रभासस्य तथोदितेऽर्द्धके सूर्ये महावेध इतीर्यते बुधैः ।।७९।।  

padaparāmarśaḥ  
aruṇodayakālatvena ghaṭīcatuṣṭayātmako yaḥ kālo niyatastatraiva catvāro vedhā bhavantīti  
bodhayitumāha - ghaṭīcatuṣkamiti । catasṛṇāṃ ghaṭīnāṃ samudāyo ghaṭīcatuṣkam। kālavimarśe  
prāmāṇyaṃ darśayituṃ maharṣibhirivyuktam, na hi tathāvidhā mudhā'caranti। kālagataviśeṣante  
rakṣiturmahanti ṛ tambharaprajñātvāt। tādṛśaiḥ prātarghadīcatuṣkamaruṇodayanāmā samayo nirdhṛṣṭastastra  
vipaścito vedhaprabhṛtervivekataścaturō vibhāgān prāhurityanvayaḥ। vipaścitaḥ kālarahasyavidaḥ।  
vivekato vivekeneti tṛtīyāyāṃ sārvavibhaktikastasih।  
ghaṭītrayaṃ sārdadhamathāruṇodaye vedhī'tivedhaḥ kathito ghaṭīdaryam ।  
raviprabhasasya tathodite'rddhake sūrye mahāvedha itīryate budhaiḥ ।।79।।  

पदपरामर्शः (padaparāmarśaḥ): पद (padam) - base noun, masculine, singular, nominative; परामर्शः (parāmarśaḥ) - noun, masculine, singular, nominative; meaning "consultation on words" or "discussion of terms"

अरुणोदयकालत्वेन (aruṇodayakālatvena): अरुणोदय (aruṇodaya) - compound tatpuruṣa "sunrise", कालत्व (kālatva) - noun, neuter, singular, instrumental "the state of time", and तेन (tena) - indeclinable instrumental singular; together indicate "by the time of sunrise"; instrumental case, singular

घटीचतुष्टयात्मको (ghaṭīcatuṣṭayātmako): घट (ghaṭa) - "measure", इतचतुष्टय (catuṣṭaya) - "set of four", आत्मक (ātmak) - adjectival suffix, masculine singular nominative; compound tatpuruṣa meaning "consisting of four measures"

यः (yaḥ): relative pronoun, masculine singular nominative "who"

कालो (kālo): noun, masculine singular nominative, "time"

भवन्ति (bhavanti): verb, 3rd person, plural, present indicative active "are" or "exist"

इति (iti): particle indicating quotation

- (dash): punctuation mark here

घटीचतुष्कमिति (ghaṭīcatuṣkamiti): compound word; reference to name "ghaṭīcatuṣkam" = the fourfold measure system

चतसृणाम् (catasṛṇām): numeral adjective masculine/feminine plural genitive "of the four"

घटीनाम् (ghaṭīnām): noun feminine plural genitive "of measures"

समुदायः (samudāyaḥ): noun masculine singular nominative "community" or "collection"

कालविमर्शे (kālavimarśe): compound tatpuruṣa masculine singular locative; "in time consideration"

ऋतम्भरप्रज्ञात्वात् (ṛtambharaprajñātvāt): compound noun / ablative "due to possessing truthful wisdom"

घदीचतुष्कमरुणोदयनाम (ghadīcatuṣkamaruṇodayanāma): compound adjective "named the morning fourfold measure of sunrise"

समयः (samayaḥ): noun masculine singular nominative "time, period"

तत्र (tatra): "there"

विपश्चितः (vipaścitaḥ): adjective masculine singular nominative "wise"

वेधप्रभृतेः (vedhaprabhṛter): compound tatpuruṣa (compound form in genitive) "led by divisions or piercings"

चतुर्गः (caturaga): appears as चतुरो (caturah): adjective masculine singular nominative "four"

विभागान् (vibhāgān): noun masculine plural accusative "divisions"

Literal translation:  
"Consultation on terms: By the state of sunrise time, the measure consisting of four parts, which time is fixed there, exactly four divisions arise. To explain it, he said - it is called 'ghaṭīcatuṣka'. The community of the four measures is ghaṭīcatuṣkam. To show the validity in time discussion, it is stated by great sages; indeed, fools do not act thus. At the end of time-specific matters, great ones protect and preserve due to possessing truthful wisdom. By such, the morning fourfold measure named 'aruṇodaya' time is determined there. The wise, led by the divisions, by discrimination, said these four parts; thus is the logical connection. The wise are the knowers of the secret of time. By discrimination, it is called 'viveka' in the third (case) of the universal declension of the sun (shaft). The three measures altogether at sunrise, the piercer (solar entity) 'ativedha' is said to be the set of measures. Of the sun's light, in the half sun as stated above, the great piercing (eclipse) is called by the wise."  

Smooth English translation:  
"Consultation on terminology: By the time of sunrise, there is a fourfold measure called ‘ghaṭīcatuṣka,’ consisting of four parts fixed in time. This community of four measures, ghaṭīcatuṣkam, is authenticated in discussions of time by great sages, while fools do not act thus. At the end of specific time intervals, the wise preserve truthfulness. Such is the morning fourfold measure named after sunrise, the ‘aruṇodaya’ time, which is precisely determined. The wise, guided by these divisions and discrimination, assert these four parts, forming a logical connection. They are the knowers of the secrets of time. The term ‘viveka’ (discrimination) applies especially in the third case of the universal declension of the sun. The three measures combined at sunrise are called ‘ativedha’—the great piercer or solar eclipse. Regarding the sun’s light, in the half sun as previously stated, the wise call this the great eclipse."
```
