# Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
SOURCE_PAGES: page_0137.txt

=== मूलपाठः ===


उपाधिनिर्मुक्तमनेकभेदका भक्तिः समुक्ता परमात्मसेवनम् ।
अनन्यभावेन मुहुर्मुहुः सदा महर्षिभिस्तैः खलु तत्परत्वतः ।।७२॥

upādhinirmuktamanekabhedakā bhaktiḥ samuktā paramātmasēvanam ।
ananyabhāvena muhurmuhuḥ sadā maharṣibhistaik khalu tatparatvataḥ ॥72॥

उपाधिनिर्मुक्तम् (upādhinirmuktam), उपाधि (upādhi) + निर्मुक्त (nirmukta): adj. exempt from limiting adjuncts (compound: tatpuruṣa, क्तत्, singular, neuter, accusative)  
अनेकभेदका (anekabhedakā), अनेक (aneka, many) + भेद (bheda, difference) + का (kā, suffix forming adjectives): adj. causing many distinctions (bahuvrīhi compound, feminine nominative singular)  
भक्तिः (bhaktiḥ), भक्ति (bhakti): noun, feminine, nominative singular  
समुक्ता (samuktā), समुक्त (samukta): adj. freed, released (feminine nominative singular)  
परमात्मसेवनम् (paramātmasēvanam), परमात्मन् (paramātman, supreme self) + सेवनम् (sēvanam, service): noun, neuter singular accusative (compound: tatpuruṣa)  
अनन्यभावेन (ananyabhāvena), अनन्य (ananya, exclusive) + भावेन (bhāvena, with attitude/emotion): noun, instrumental singular  
मुहुःमुहुः (muhurmuhuḥ), adv.: again and again  
सदा (sadā), adv.: always  
महर्षिभिः (maharṣibhiḥ), महर्षि (maharṣi, great seer) noun, masculine, instrumental plural  
तैः (taiḥ), pronoun, instrumental plural (third person)  
खलु (khalu), particle indeed, certainly  
तत्परत्वतः (tatparatvataḥ), तत्परत्व (tatparatvatva, devotion, eagerness) + तः (tah, ablative singular): noun, masculine singular ablative

Exempt from limiting adjuncts, causing many distinctions, devotion is liberated for the service of the Supreme Self.  
With exclusive attitude, again and again always, by great seers indeed, because of eagerness/devotion.

Devotion that is free from limiting adjuncts and many distinctions is perfected for serving the Supreme Self.  
With exclusive attitude, always repeatedly, it is truly cherished by the great sages due to their eagerness.
```
