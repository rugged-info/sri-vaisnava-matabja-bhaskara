# Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
SOURCE_PAGES: page_0143.txt

=== मूलपाठः ===


एकादशीत्यादिमहात्रतानि वै कुयद्विवेधानि हरिप्रियाण्यथ ।
विद्धा दशम्या यदि साऽरुणोदये स द्वादशीं तूपवसेद्धिहाय ताम् ।। ७५।।

ekādaśītyādimahātratāni vai ku-yadvivedhāni haripriyāṇyatha ।
viddhā daśmyā yadi sā’ruṇodaye sa dvādaśīṁ tūpavase-ddhihāya tām ॥ 75 ॥

एकादशी (ekādaśī) - ekādaśī - fem. nom. sg.;  
इति (iti) - iti - indeclinable (quotative);  
आदि (ādi) - ādi - masc. nom. pl. (ordinal context: "beginning with, etc.");  
महात्रतानि (mahātratāni) - mahātratā - neut. nom. pl.; tatpuruṣa compound of महा (mahā, great) + त्रता (tratā, duration); here "great durations";  
वै (vai) - vai - particle (indeed);  
कुयद्विवेधानि (ku-yadvivedhāni) - ku + yadvivedhāni; ku = interrogative particle "how"; yadvivedhāni = neut. nom. pl. of vividha (vivēdha), "variations, distinctions"; compound - bahuvrīhi;  
हरिप्रियाणि (haripriyāṇi) - haripriya - neut. nom. pl.; tatpuruṣa compound हरि (Hari, Vishnu) + प्रिय (priya, dear); "dear to Hari (Vishnu)";  
अथ (atha) - ath - indeclinable, adverb "then";  

विद्धा (viddhā) - viddhā - fem. nom. sg. of पक्षवाचक participle from √विद् "to know";  
दशम्या (daśmyā) - daśmyā - fem. nom. sg. of ordinal "tenth (day)";  
यदि (yadi) - yadi - indeclinable (if);  
साऽरुणोदये (sā'ruṇodaye) - sā (fem. nom. sg.) + aruṇodaye (locative sg. masc.) "in red dawn";  
स (sa) - sa - masc. nom. sg. pronoun "he";  
द्वादशीं (dvādaśīṁ) - dvādaśīṁ - fem. acc. sg. ordinal "eleventh (day)";  
तूपवसेद्धिहाय (tūpavase-ddhihāya) - tūpavase-ddhihāya; tūpavase = verbal noun; ddhiāya - causative suffix / gerundive of धिह् "to meditate"? The morphemes indicate a verbal purpose or intent, "for the observance of fasting/tapas"; compound (perhaps tatpuruṣa or bahuvrīhi) meaning "for fasting/vow observance";  
ताम् (tām) - tām - fem. acc. sg. pronoun "her/that";  

Literal translation (close):  
The ekādaśī and so on great durations indeed how many variations dear to Hari (Vishnu) are there.  
Knowing the tenth day, if she (the tenth day) at the red dawn, he (the practitioner) for the purpose of observing the fasting on the eleventh day, that (eleventh day).  

Smooth English translation:  
Indeed, how many great periods beginning with ekādaśī and their variations dear to Hari exist?  
If one knows the tenth day, and at red dawn on that day, then for observing the fast on the following eleventh day, that (eleventh day) is suitable.
```
