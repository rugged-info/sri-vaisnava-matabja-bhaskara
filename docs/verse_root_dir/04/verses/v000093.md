## Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
=== मूलपाठः ===
```

---

## Transliteration (IAST)

```text
स्मरेण विद्धा तु चतुर्दशी यदा भवेद्धनापत्यविनाशिनी तदा ।
तत्रोपवासो न जनैर्विधीयतां महात्सभिर्विष्णुपरायणैरपि ।।९३।।
```

---

## Literal Translation

स्मरेण (smareṇa), स्मर, स्मरण (smaraṇa), अव्यय (indeclinable, instrumental singular of स्मृति, used adverbially "by remembrance/memory")  
विद्धा (viddhā), विद्ध (viddha), Participle, Perfect Passive Participle, feminine singular nominative ("known, remembered")  
तु (tu), तु, अव्यय (indeclinable, particle "but")  
चतुर्दशी (caturdaśī), चतुर्दशी, noun, feminine singular nominative ("the fourteenth (day of the lunar fortnight)")  
यदा (yadā), यदा, अव्यय (indeclinable, conjunction "when")  
भवेत् (bhavet), भव् (bhū), Verb, optative mood, singular, third person, active ("may be, would be")  
धनापत्यविनाशिनी (dhanāpatyavināśinī), धनापत्य-विनाशिनी, Compound (tatpuruṣa), feminine singular nominative ("destroyer of wealth and progeny")  
तदा (tadā), तदा, अव्यय (indeclinable, "then")  
तत्र (tatra), तत्र, अव्यय (indeclinable, "there")  
उपवासः (upavāsaḥ), उपवास, noun, masculine singular nominative ("fasting")  
न (na), न, अव्यय (indeclinable, negation particle "not")  
जनैः (janaiḥ), जन, noun, masculine plural instrumental ("by people")  
विधीयतां (vidhīyatāṃ), विध (vidh), Verb, imperative mood, middle voice, singular, third person ("let be ordained, let be observed")  
महात्सभिः (mahātsabhiḥ), महात्सभि, noun, masculine singular instrumental ("by a great assembly")  
विष्णुपरायणैः (viṣṇuparāyaṇaiḥ), विष्णु-परायण, Compound (tatpuruṣa), masculine plural instrumental ("devoted to Viṣṇu")  
अपि (api), अपि, अव्यय (indeclinable, particle "also, even")

---

## Smooth Translation

"By remembrance (smareṇa) known (viddhā), but (tu) the fourteenth day (caturdaśī), when (yadā) it may be (bhavet) the destroyer of wealth and progeny (dhanāpatyavināśinī), then (tadā) there (tatra) fasting (upavāsaḥ) not (na) by people (janaiḥ) let be observed (vidhīyatāṃ) by a great assembly (mahātsabhiḥ), devoted to Viṣṇu (viṣṇuparāyaṇaiḥ) also (api)."
