## Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
=== मूलपाठः ===
```

---

## Transliteration (IAST)

```text
विश्वं जातं यतोऽद्धा यदवितमखिलं लीयते यत्र चान्ते सूर्यो यत्तेजसेन्दुः सकलमविरतं  
भासयत्येतदेषः । यद्धीत्या वाति वातोऽवनिरपि सुतलं याति नैवेश्वरो ज्ञः साक्षी कूटस्थ एको  
बहुशुभगुणवानव्ययो विश्वभर्ता ।।८।।
```

---

## Literal Translation

viśvaṃ (viśva, neuter, accusative singular)  
jātaṃ (jāta, masculine/neuter, nominative/accusative singular, past participle of √jan)  
yatoʼddhā (yataḥ, relative pronoun, masculine/neuter, nominative singular + uddhā, possibly part of compound)  
yad (yat, relative pronoun, neuter, nominative singular)  
avitam (avita, adjective or noun, neuter, accusative singular)  
akhilaṃ (akhila, adjective, neuter, accusative singular)  
līyate (lī, verb root, 3rd person singular present middle/passive indicative)  
yatra (yat, relative pronoun, neuter, locative singular)  
cānte (ca + ante; anta, masculine/neuter, locative singular + indeclinable ca)  
sūryo (sūrya, masculine, nominative singular)  
yattējasainduḥ (yat + tejasa + induḥ; yat, neuter nominative singular relative pronoun; tejasa, masculine/neuter nominative singular; induḥ, masculine nominative singular; here a compound tatpuruṣa)  
sakalamavirataṃ (sakala + avirata + neuter accusative singular; sakala "all", avirata "unceasing")  
bhāsayaty (bhāsayati, verb root √bhās, 3rd person singular present indicative active)  
etaḥ (etad, neuter nominative singular demonstrative pronoun)  
eṣaḥ (eṣa, masculine nominative singular demonstrative pronoun)  
yaddhītyā (yat + dhītyā; yat, neuter nominative singular relative pronoun + dhītyā possibly instrumental singular of dhī, or related to dhī "intellect")  
vāti (vāta, masculine nominative singular or instrumental singular; also could be a verb form)  
vātaḥ (vātaḥ, masculine nominative singular)  
avanirapi (avanir + api; avanir possibly variant of avani "earth" or avani "wind", + indeclinable api)  
sutalaṃ (sutala, neuter accusative singular)  
yāti (√yā, verb root; 3rd person singular present indicative active)  
naiveśvaraḥ (na + īśvaraḥ; na negative particle + īśvaraḥ "lord", masculine nominative singular)  
jñaḥ (jña, masculine nominative singular, adjective "knowing")  
sākṣī (sākṣī, masculine nominative singular, noun "witness")  
kūṭasthaḥ (kūṭastha, masculine nominative singular adjective "steadfast", "unmoving")  
eko (eka, masculine nominative singular, numeral "one")  
bahuśubhaguṇavānavyayo (bahu + śubha + guṇa + vān + avyaya; compound of multiple words, bahu (many), śubha (auspicious), guṇa (qualities), vān (suffix indicating possession), avyaya (indeclinable, "imperishable"))  
viśvabhartā (viśvabhartā, masculine nominative singular noun "lord of the universe")

---

## Smooth Translation

(The compound yattējaseṃduḥ appears to be tatpuruṣa: "yat + tejasa + induḥ" = "that [which is] the luminous moon/sun").
