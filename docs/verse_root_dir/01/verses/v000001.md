# Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
SOURCE_PAGES: page_0040.txt

=== मूलपाठः ===


श्रीमन्तं श्रुतिवेद्यमद्श्ुतगुणग्रामाग्यरलाकरं
प्रयः स्वेक्षणसंसुलज्ितमहीजाताक्षिकोणेक्षितम् ।
भक्तारोषमनोभिवाज्छितचतुर्व्गप्रदं स्वर्द्धमं
रामं स्मेरमुखाम्बुजं शुचिमहानीलाश्मकान्तिं भजे ।।१।।

śrīmantaṃ śrutivedyamadśutaguṇagrāmāgyaralākaraṃ
prayaḥ svekṣaṇasaṃsulajitamahījātākṣikoṇekṣitam ।
bhaktāroṣamanobhibhājchitacaturvagapradaṃ svarddhaṃ
rāmaṃ smeramukhāmbujaṃ śucimahānīlāśmakāntiṃ bhaje ।।1।।

śrīmantaṃ (śrīmanta, adj., acc. sg. m.) — wealthy, splendid  
śrutivedya (śrutivedya, adj., acc. sg. m.) — known by scriptures and Vedas  
amadśutaguṇagrāmāgyaralākaraṃ (compound: tatpuruṣa)  
  amadśuta (amadśuta, adj., none in dictionary; likely a compound part: a- (neg.) + madśuta?), guessed as “free from intoxication”  
  guṇa (guṇa, m., nom. sg.) — quality  
  grāma (grāma, m., nom. sg.) — village  
  agya (ājñā, f., nom. sg.) — command/order  
  rala (rala, adj., rare) — sticky or binding  
  ākaraṃ (ākara, m., acc. sg.) — hand, arm, limb (also "symbol" or "mark")  
prayaḥ (prayaḥ, m., nom. sg.) — effort, advance  
svekṣaṇa (svekṣaṇa, n., nom. sg.) — own sight, outlook  
saṃsulajitamahījātākṣikoṇekṣitam (compound)  
   saṃsulajita (past ptc. from saṃ+laj, "to entangle", adj.) — entangled  
   mahi (mahī, f., nom. sg.) — earth  
   jāta (jāta, adj., nom. sg. m.) — born, originated  
   akṣikoṇa (akṣikoṇa, m., nom. sg.) — axis  
   ikṣitam (ikṣita, perf. ptc. n. sg.) — seen, observed

bhakta (bhakta, adj., nom. pl. m.) — devoted  
roṣa (roṣa, m., nom. sg.) — anger  
manobhibhāja (compound: tatpuruṣa)  
  mano (manas, n., nom./acc. sg.) — mind  
  bhibhāja (bhibhāj, verb root; here perhaps participle) — dividing, distributing  
chita (chita, adj., nom. sg. m.) — united, arranged  
caturvaga (caturvarga, m., nom. sg.) — four-fold group/class  
pradaṃ (prada, adj., acc. sg. m.) — giving  
svarddhaṃ (svarddha, n., acc. sg.) — wealth, prosperity  
rāmaṃ (rāma, m., acc. sg.) — Rama (personal name)  
smara (smera, m., nom. sg.) — smiling  
mukhāmbujaṃ (compound: bahuvrīhi)  
   mukha (mukha, n., nom. sg.) — face  
   ambuja (ambuja, m., acc. sg.) — lotus  
śuci (śuci, adj., acc. sg. m.) — pure  
mahānīlā (mahānīlā, adj., acc. sg. m.) — great blue  
aśmakāntiṃ (aśmakānti, f., acc. sg.) — light/shining of the eye  
bhaje (bhaj, verb root; 1st pers. sg. pres. ind.) — I worship

Literal translation (with grammatical notes in parentheses):

Wealthy (acc. sg. m.) and known from the scriptures and Vedas (acc. sg. m.), free from intoxication and the cluster of qualities like a village, command, and sticky hand (acc. sg. m.),  
effort (nom. sg.), with his own vision, entangled born earth axis observed (nom./acc. sg.),  
devotees' anger mind dividing united four-class giving wealth (acc. sg.),  
Rama (acc. sg.), smiling lotus-faced, pure great blue-eyed shining, I worship (1st pers. sg. pres.).

Smooth English translation:

I worship Rama, who is wealthy and revered in the scriptures and Vedas, free from intoxication and adorned with qualities like those of a village and command;  
who advances with a keen inner vision, perceiving deeply the entangled nature of the earthly realm and its axis;  
who dispels the anger of devotees, unites their minds, grants the fourfold blessings, and bestows prosperity;  
Rama, smiling with a face like a lotus, pure, with great blue eyes shining brightly.
```
