## Verse — मूलपाठः

=== मूलपाठः ===

---

## Transliteration (IAST)

```text
तत्त्वं किं किञ्च जप्यं परमिह विबुधेरवेष्णवेर्ध्यानमिषं मुक्तेः किं साधनं सत्सुमतिमतिमतो  
धर्म एकोऽस्ति कश्च । धर्माणां वैष्णवास्ते गुरुवर! कतिधा लक्षणं किञ्च तेषां कालक्षेपः  
किमाप्यं कथमुरुशुभदं कुत्र कार्यो निवासः ।।४।।
```

---

## Literal Translation

तत्त्वं (tattvaṃ), तत्त्व (tattva), neuter singular accusative;  
किं (kiṃ), किं (kiṃ), interrogative indeclinable;  
किञ्च (kiñca), किञ्च (kiñca), indeclinable conjunction;  
जप्यं (japyaṃ), जप्यम् (japyam), neuter singular accusative, verbal adjective from जप् (jap) root, participle passive gerundive;  
परमिह (paramiha), परम् (param) + इह (iha), compound adv. (tatpuruṣa), meaning "here beyond";  
विबुधेः (vibudheḥ), बुद् (bud) [but here बिध meaning wise], masculine singular genitive;  
अवेष्णवे (aveṣṇave), अवेष्णव (aveṣṇava), masculine singular dative or locative, "seeker/one who desires";  
अध्यानमिषं (adhyānamiṣaṃ), ध्यानमिष (adhyānamiṣa), neuter singular accusative, compound noun: meditation-food, "meditation as nourishment";  
मुक्तेः (mukteḥ), मुक्त (mukta) masculine/neuter singular genitive, "of liberation";  
किं (kiṃ), किं (kiṃ), interrogative indeclinable;  
साधनं (sādhanaṃ), सिधान (sādhana) neuter singular accusative, noun, "means, instrument";  
सत्सुमतिमतिमतो (satsumatimatimato), compound bahuvrīhi tatpuruṣa, from सत् (sat) + सुमति (sumati) +मतिमत (matimata), masculine singular nominative, descriptive adjective meaning "coming from/to the truly intelligent" or "by a truly wise mind";

---

## Smooth Translation

धर्म (dharma), धर्म (dharma), masculine singular nominative, noun;  
एकः (ekaḥ), एक (eka), masculine singular nominative numeral, "one";  
अस्ति (asti), अस् (as) verb root, 3rd person singular present indicative active, "is";  
कश्च (kaśca), कश्च (kaśca), masculine singular nominative, relative pronoun, "someone";  
धर्माणाम् (dharmāṇām), धर्म (dharma), masculine plural genitive;  
वैष्णवास्ते (vaiṣṇavāste), वैष्णव (vaiṣṇava) masculine plural dative/locative, "to Vaishnavas";  
गुरुवर (guruvara), गुरु (guru) + वर (vara), masculine singular vocative, "the best among gurus";  
कतिधा (katidhā), interrogative adv., "in what manner";  
लक्षणं (lakṣaṇaṃ), लक्षण (lakṣaṇa), neuter singular accusative, "characteristic, mark";  
किञ्च (kiñca), indeclinable conjunction, "some, any";  
तेषां (teṣāṃ), तेष (teṣa), masculine/neuter plural genitive pronoun, "their";  
कालक्षेपः (kālākṣepaḥ), कालक्षेप (kālākṣepa), masculine singular nominative, "time-spending, pastime";
