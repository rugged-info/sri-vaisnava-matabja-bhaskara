## Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
=== मूलपाठः ===
```

---

## Transliteration (IAST)

```text
निर्मत्युः सर्वशक्िर्विकलुषविजरो गीर्मनोभ्यामगम्यः ।।९।।
```

---

## Literal Translation

निर्मत्युः (nirmatyuḥ), निर्मत्युः, nom. sg. m. (वृत्ति — कर्ता)
सर्वशकिः (sarvaśakiḥ), सर्वशकिः, nom. sg. m. (समासः — बहुव्रीहि)
विकलुष (vikaluṣa), विकलुषः, nom. sg. m.
विजरः (vijaraḥ), विजरः, nom. sg. m.
गीर् (gīr), गीर्, instr. pl. m. (पदम्)
मनः (manaḥ), मनस्, instr. pl. n.
अभ्य (abhya), अभ्य, suffix of instr. pl. (पदम्)
अगम्यः (agamyaḥ), अगम्यः, nom. sg. m., adjective

---

## Smooth Translation

nirmatyuḥ: the creator (nominative singular masculine agent)
sarvaśakiḥ: all-capable, omnipotent (nominative singular masculine; bahuvrīhi compound)
vikaluṣa: impure, defiled (nominative singular masculine)
vijaraḥ: free from decay, unfading (nominative singular masculine)
gīr: with the speech (instrumental plural masculine)
manaḥ: with the mind (instrumental plural neuter)
abhya: suffix indicating companionship or agent with ablative/instrumental plural
agamyaḥ: incomprehensible, unattainable (nominative singular masculine adjective)
