## Verse — मूलपाठः

=== मूलपाठः ===

## Transliteration (IAST)

```text
भूता्षयुत्सङ्ख्यवर्णः सुकृतिभिरनिशं मोक्षकामौनिषिव्यः ।।१०。。
```

---

## Literal Translation

भूता (bhūtā), भूता (bhūta), nominative singular masculine;
अषयुत् (aṣayut), अषयुत् (aṣayut), indeclinable/compound component;
सङ्ख्य (saṅkhya), सङ्ख्या (saṅkhyā), tatpuruṣa compound component;
वर्णः (varṇaḥ), वर्ण (varṇa), nominative singular masculine;
सुकृतिभिः (sukṛtibhiḥ), सुकृत (sukṛta) + instrumental plural;
अनिशं (aniśaṃ), अनिश (aniśa), indeclinable (adverb);
मोक्षकाम (mokṣakāma), मोक्ष (mokṣa) + काम (kāma), bahuvrīhi compound;
औनि (auni), ऊन (ūna), adjectival compound;
षिव्यः (ṣivyaḥ), षिव्य (ṣivya), nominative plural masculine;
।१०। (10), verse number.

---

## Smooth Translation

The compound भूता्षयुत्सङ्ख्यवर्णः (bhūtāṣayutsaṅkhyavarṇaḥ) is a tatpuruṣa-type compound meaning "consisting of (varṇas) associated with beings and numbers"; सुकृतिभिर (sukṛtibhiḥ) is instrumental plural "by good deeds"; अनिशं (aniśaṃ) means "continuously"; मोक्षकामौनिषिव्यः (mokṣakāmouniṣivyaḥ) is a bahuvrīhi compound meaning "desirous of liberation and humble/devoted", with षिव्यः (ṣivyaḥ) as an adjective qualifying.
