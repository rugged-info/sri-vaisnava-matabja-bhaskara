# Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
SOURCE_PAGES: page_0057.txt

=== मूलपाठः ===


इद्थं पृष्टस्त्वया यः सकलहितकरः प्रश्रराशिर्गरिष्ठो वेद्यः सर्वश्रुतीनां जगति सुरसुरानन्द  
सद्यो मया सः । प्राचीनाचार्य वर्यान् यतिपतिसहितान् सादरं सम्प्रणम्य सम्यक् शास्वनुसारं  
गुरुनिरत! समाधीयते श्रूयतां तत् ।।५।।  

idthaṁ pṛṣṭastvayā yaḥ sakalahitakaraḥ praśrarāśir gariṣṭho vedyaḥ sarvaśrutīnāṁ jagati surasurānanda  
sadyo mayā saḥ । prācīnācārya varyān yatipatisahitān sādaraṁ sampraṇamya samyak śāsvanusāraṁ  
gurunirata! samādhīyate śrūyatāṁ tat ।।5।।  

इद्थं (idtham) - इदम् (idam), indeclinable demonstrative/adverb (thus, so)  
पृष्टः (pṛṣṭaḥ) - पृष्ट (pṛṣṭa), past passive participle of पृच्छ् (pṛcch), nominative singular masculine (asked)  
त्वया (tvayā) - त्वम् (tva), pronoun, instrumental singular (by you)  
यः (yaḥ) - relative pronoun, nominative singular masculine (who)  
सकलहितकरः (sakalahitakaraḥ) - compound tatpuruṣa of सकल (sakala: all/entire), हित (hita: welfare), कर (kara: doer/maker), nominative singular masculine (doing all welfare)  
प्रश्रराशिः (praśrarāśiḥ) - प्रश्र (praśra: a heap/pile) + आशी (āśī: suffix forming nouns) = heap or mass, nominative singular masculine  
गरिष्ठः (gariṣṭhaḥ) - adjective, nominative singular masculine (most eminent/highest)  
वेद्यः (vedyaḥ) - adjective, nominative singular masculine (to be known, knowable)  
सर्वश्रुतीनां (sarvaśrutīnāṁ) - सर्व (sarva: all) + श्रुती (śruti: heard scriptures), genitive plural feminine (of all scriptures)  
जगति (jagati) - जगत् (jagat: world), locative singular neuter (in the world)  
सुरसुरानन्द (surasurānanda) - compound bahuvrīhi or dvandva of सुर (sura: gods) + असुर (asura: demons) + आनन्द (ānanda: joy), nominative singular masculine (joy of gods and demons)  
सद्यो (sadyo) - adverb (immediately)  
मया (mayā) - अहम् (aham), pronoun instrumental singular (by me)  
सः (saḥ) - pronoun demonstrative, nominative singular masculine (he)  
प्राचीनाचार्य (prācīnācārya) - compound tatpuruṣa of प्राचीन (prācīna: ancient) + आचार्य (ācārya: teacher), nominative singular masculine (ancient teacher)  
वर्यान् (varyān) - adjective, accusative plural masculine (excellent, best)  
यतिपतिसहितान् (yatipatisahitān) - compound tatpuruṣa of यति-पति (yati-pati: lord of sages) + सहित (sahita: together with), accusative plural masculine (together with the heads of ascetics)  
सादरं (sādaraṁ) - noun/adjective, accusative singular neuter (with respect)  
सम्प्रणम्य (sampraṇamya) - verbal root सम्प्र+णम् (praṇam) with क्त्वा construction (having bowed)  
सम्यक् (samyak) - indeclinable adverb (properly, well)  
शास्वनुसारं (śāsvanusāraṁ) - compound tatpuruṣa of शास् (śās: govern) + अनु (anu: along with) + सारं (sāra: essence/accordance), accusative singular neuter (in accordance with instructions)  
गुरुनिरत (gurunirata) - compound tatpuruṣa of गुरु (guru: teacher) + निरत (nirata: engaged), vocative singular masculine (O devoted to the guru)  
समाधीयते (samādhīyate) - verb in present passive or middle indicative, 3rd person singular from समाधि (samādhi: concentration/meditation) root 1p. (is meditated upon)  
श्रूयताम् (śrūyatām) - verb, imperative or optative, 3rd person plural from श्रु (śru: to hear), (let it be heard)  
तत् (tat) - pronoun neuter singular nominative/accusative (that)  

Thus asked by you, who is the most eminent heap of all-welfare, knowable in all scriptures, the joy of gods and demons in the world,  
immediately by me he (is known). The ancient teacher, the best, with the lord of sages together, respectfully having bowed well in accordance with instructions,  
O devoted to the guru! That is meditated on, let that be heard. (Verse 5)  

Thus, immediately upon your questioning, he who is the greatest benefactor, knowable by all scriptures, the joy of gods and demons in the world,  
has been grasped by me. The ancient teacher, the best, together with the leaders of ascetics, having respectfully bowed properly according to instructions,  
O devoted to the guru! That is meditated upon; let it be heard.
```
