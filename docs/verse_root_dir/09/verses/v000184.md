=== मूलपाठः ===


मार्गे ततः सोऽर्चिरुपैति मुक्तकस्तथाऽर्चिषोऽहो दिनतः सुरार्चितः ।
आपूर्यमाणं विविधेस्तु वासरैः पक्षं प्रभूतोत्तमशर्मविज्वरः ।।१८२।।
पक्षादुदङ्मासमथी षडात्मकं तेभ्यश्च सम्वत्सरमब्दतो रविम् ।
चन्द्रं ततश्चन्द्रमसोऽथ विद्युतं स तत्र तत्राखिलदेवपूजितः ।।१८३।।
परं पदं सोऽयमुपेत्य नित्यममानवं ब्रह्मपथेन तेन ।
सायुज्यमेव प्रतिलभ्य तत्र प्राप्यस्य सन्रन्दति तेन साकम् ।।१८४।।

mārge tataḥ so'rcirupaiti muktakastathārciṣo'ho dinataḥ surārcitaḥ ।

(He, the liberated light, then approaches on the path; thus the light of the day adored by gods. Being filled with various days, the fortnight is dominantly guarded by the best protector "vijvara." From the fortnight, the moon of the full months, consisting of six parts, and from them also, the year from the month, the sun, the moon, and then the lightning; he is worshipped everywhere by all gods. Having reached the supreme position, always non-human, by the path of Brahman, he obtains the union; having attained it, he trembles together with him.)

On the path, then the liberated light approaches; thus the daylight adored by the gods. The fortnight is filled with many days and is dominantly guarded by the greatest protector, Vijvara. From the fortnight comes the moon of full months consisting of six parts; from them comes the year from the months, the sun, the moon, and then the lightning; he is worshipped throughout by all gods. Having attained the supreme position, always beyond the human, by the path of Brahman, he obtains union; having achieved this, he rejoices in it together with him.
