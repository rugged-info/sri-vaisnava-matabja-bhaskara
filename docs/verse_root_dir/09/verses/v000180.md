# Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
SOURCE_PAGES: page_0252.txt

=== मूलपाठः ===


कर्माखिलं सम्परिभुज्य चात्मवान् प्रारब्धमेवं प्रहतान्यकम्रमकः ।।१८०।।

karmākhilaṃ samparibhujya cātman prārabdham evaṃ prahatāny akramakaḥ ।।180।।

कर्माखिलं (karma-akhilaṃ) कर्म (karma), noun, neuter, accusative singular; अखिलम् (akhila) adjective, neuter, accusative singular; सम्परिभुज्य (samparibhujya) सम् (sam, prefix) + परिभुज्य (paribhujya, gerund from paribhuj) verbal root; च (ca) indeclinable, conjunction; आत्मवान् (ātman) आत्मन् (ātman), noun, masculine, nominative singular + वान् (vān) suffix indicating possession; प्रारब्धम् (prārabdhām) प्रारब्ध (prārabdha), noun, neuter, accusative singular; एवम् (evaṃ) indeclinable, adverb; प्रहतानि (prahatāni) प्रहत (prahat), adjective or noun, neuter, accusative plural; अक्रमकः (akramakaḥ) अक्रमक (akramaka), adjective or noun, masculine, nominative singular

All the actions of karma having been fully seized/embraced and being self-endowed, thus the commenced (karma or consequences) having been struck — not stepping aside.

All actions having been fully grasped by the self, thus those that have begun are struck without deviation.

(Note: The verse is complex and some word roles may be subject to interpretive nuance.)
```
