## Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
=== मूलपाठः ===
```

---

## Transliteration (IAST)

```text
तथा बदर्याभिध आश्रमे वरे सुरर्षिराजर्षिमहर्षिसेविते ।
नारायणं लोकनमस्कृताङ्प्रिकं मुनीशसेव्ये त्वथ नैमिषे हरिम् ।।१९०।।
```

---

## Literal Translation

तथा (tathā), indeclinable, adverb;  
बदर्याभिध (badaryābhidha), noun, masculine, singular, nominative, tatpuruṣa compound; base: बदर्याभिध (name of an ashram);  
आश्रमे (āśrame), noun, masculine, singular, locative; base: आश्रम (ashram);  
वरे (vare), noun, masculine, singular, locative; base: वर (best, excellent);  
सुरर्षिराजर्षिमहर्षिसेविते (surarṣirājaṛṣimaharṣisevite), compound word, locative singular feminine (participle-like), from सुरर्षि, राजर्षि, महर्षि, सेवित (served by); tatpuruṣa or bahuvrīhi compound of multiple elements (gods-rishis-kings-rishis-maharshis served);  
नारायणं (nārāyaṇaṃ), noun, masculine, singular, accusative; base: नारायण (Narayana);  
लोकनमस्कृताङ्प्रिकं (lokanamaskṛtāṅprikaṃ), compound adjective, accusative singular neuter, tatpuruṣa or bahuvrīhi; base: लोक (world), नमस्कृत (respected), आङ्प्रिक (probably a compound element or a sandhi compound from प्रिक or प्रिय - meaning 'beloved' or 'dear');  
मुनीशसेव्ये (munīśasevye), compound, locative singular feminine or masculine, present passive participle; meaning 'served by the Lord of the sages' (मुनि-ईश-सेव्य);  
त्वथ (tvatha), indeclinable, adverb;  
नैमिषे (naimiṣe), noun, locative singular neuter; base: नैमिष (Naimisha, a sacred forest or time);   
हरिम् (harim), noun, masculine, singular, accusative; base: हरि (Hari - Lord Vishnu).

---

## Smooth Translation

Thus, in literal order and case:  
"Thus in the excellent Badarya-named ashram served by gods, sages, kings of sages, and great sages,  
Narayana, adored by the people, beloved among them, served by the lord of sages, indeed in Naimisha, Hari."
