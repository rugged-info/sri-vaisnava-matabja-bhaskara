# Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
SOURCE_PAGES: page_0264.txt

=== मूलपाठः ===


विष्णुं तु लोके वरसत्यसञ्चके पदाक्षमिव्थं त्वथ सूर्यमण्डले ।
क्षीराब्धिमध्ये शुभशेषशायिनं शेते तथा द्वीपवरे च तारकम् ।।१८९॥।

viṣṇuṃ tu loke varasatyasañcake padākṣamivthaṃ tvatha sūryamaṇḍale ।
kṣīrābdhimadhye śubhaśeṣaśāyinaṃ śete tathā dvīpavare ca tārakam ।।189॥

(viṣṇuṃ, viṣṇu, accusative singular masculine)
(tu, tu, indeclinable/particle)
(loke, loka, locative singular masculine)
(varasatyasañcake, varasatyasañcaka, locative singular masculine; compound: tatpuruṣa)
(padākṣamivthaṃ, padākṣam + iva + thaṃ: padākṣam (accusative singular neuter), iva (indeclinable: like), thaṃ (indefinite pronoun accusative singular neuter))
(tvatha, tvatha, indeclinable/particle)
(sūryamaṇḍale, sūryamaṇḍala, locative singular neuter)
(kṣīrābdhimadhye, kṣīrābdhi + madhye: kṣīrābdhi (locative singular masculine), madhye (locative singular masculine))
(śubhaśeṣaśāyinaṃ, śubhaśeṣaśāyina, accusative singular masculine; compound: bahuvrīhi)
(śete, śet, present tense 3rd person singular active)
(tathā, tathā, indeclinable/particle)
(dvīpavare, dvīpavara, locative singular masculine; compound: tatpuruṣa)
(ca, ca, indeclinable/conjunction)
(tārakam, tāraka, accusative singular masculine)

Vishnu (acc.) indeed (particle) in the world (locative) on the excellent Satyasanchaka (locative; "Satyasanchaka" [name, a mountain]) like the stepping of a foot (acc. neut. + iva + indeclinable = "like the stepping of a foot") indeed (particle) on the sun's orb (locative).

In the ocean of milk (locative) lies (3rd person sing. present) the auspicious remainder-sleeper (accusative), so too (particle) on the excellent island (locative) and the star (accusative).

Vishnu indeed, in the world on the excellent Satyasanchaka mountain, like the stepping of a foot, is on the sun's orb.

He lies like the auspicious eternal sleeper in the ocean of milk, and also on the excellent island and the star.
```
