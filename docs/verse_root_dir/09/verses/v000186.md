## Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
=== मूलपाठः ===
```

---

## Transliteration (IAST)

```text
(उथ दशमप्रश्रीत्तरे वेष्णवनिवासयोग्यस्थाननिरूपणम्)
महामते प्रोच्य तदीयमाप्यं तेषां निवासस्तु निरूप्यतेऽथ ।
मोक्षप्रदः शास्तरसुसम्मतश्च जिज्ञासुबोध्यो भव सावधानः ।।१८६।।
```

---

## Literal Translation

(उथ) - Indeclinable, particle (here: "then" or exclamatory, often serves as a discourse marker)
(दशमप्रश्रीत्तरे) - compound noun (tatpuruṣa, locative singular feminine)
 दशल (दशम) - "tenth"
 प्रश्रीत्तरे (प्रश्रीत्तरा) - "chapter" or "section"
(वेष्णवनिवासयोग्यस्थाननिरूपणम्) - compound noun (bahuvrīhi)
 वेष्णव (वैष्णव) - "Vaishnava" (devotee of Vishnu)
 निवास (निवास) - "residence" or "dwelling"
 योग्य (योग्य) - "suitable" or "proper"
 स्थान (स्थान) - "place"
 निरूपणम् (निरूपण) - "explanation" or "determination"
(महामते) - (mahāmatā, vocative singular masculine) base: महामति "great-minded"
(प्रोच्य) - (procya) gerundive or verbal adjective from "प्रोच्" "to speak, to mention"
(तदीयम्) - (tadīyam) pronoun adjective neuter singular accusative "that own"
(आप्यम्) - (āpyam) indeclinable or noun neuter singular accusative meaning "also" or possibly "indeed/additionally"
(तेषां) - (teṣāṃ) pronoun genitive plural "of them"
(निवासः) - (nivāsaḥ) noun masculine nominative singular "residence"
(तु) - (tu) indeclinable particle "but"
(निरूप्यते) - (nirūpyate) verb passive present indicative 3rd singular "is determined/explained"
(अथ) - (atha) indeclinable "then"
(मोक्षप्रदः) - compound noun (bahuvrīhi), nominative singular masculine
 मोक्ष (mokṣa) "liberation"
 प्रद (prada) "giver"
(शास्तरसुसम्मतः) - compound adjective (tatpuruṣa), nominative singular masculine
 शास्त्र (śāstra) "scripture"
 सुसम्मत (su-sammata) "well-approved, accepted"
(च) - (ca) indeclinable "and"
(जिज्ञासुबोध्यो) - compound participle, nominative singular masculine
 जिज्ञासु (jijñāsu) "inquirer, seeker"
 बोध्यो (bodhya) "to be understood"
(भव) - verb imperative 2nd singular from "भू" "be"
(सावधानः) - adjective nominative singular masculine "attentive, vigilant"

---

## Smooth Translation

Literal translation:
Then, o great-minded one, mentioning (procyā), that own also of them residence however is determined then.  
Liberation-giver, well-approved by scripture, and to be understood by the seeker, be attentive.
