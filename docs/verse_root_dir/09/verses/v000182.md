## Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
=== मूलपाठः ===
```

---

## Transliteration (IAST)

```text
मार्गे ततः सोऽर्चिरुपैति मुक्तकस्तथाऽर्चिषोऽहो दिनतः सुरार्चितः ।
आपूर्यमाणं विविधेस्तु वासरैः पक्षं प्रभूतोत्तमशर्मविज्वरः ।।१८२।।
```

## Smooth Translation

On the path (mārge, loc. sg.) then (tataḥ) he (saḥ) approaches (upaiti) the fire (arci, acc. sg.) free (muktaka, subj.) thus (tathā) O (vocative) of the fire's (arciṣaḥ, gen. sg.) day (dina, abl. sg.) worshipped by gods (surārcitaḥ, nom. sg. past passive participle).  
Filling (āpūryamāṇaṃ, acc. sg.) various (vividheṣu, loc. pl.) with days (vāsaraiḥ, inst. pl.) the wing (pakṣaṃ, acc. sg.) distinguished (prabhūt, nom. sg. past passive participle) uttamaśarmavijvaraḥ (name/compound).
