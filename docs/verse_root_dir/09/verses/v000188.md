## Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
=== मूलपाठः ===
```

---

## Transliteration (IAST)

```text
वेकुण्ठदेशे खलु वासुदेवमामोदसञ्ज्े त्वथ कर्षणाख्यम् ।
प्रद्युम्रमल्जाक्षमपि प्रमोदे सम्मोद ईशन्तु तथाऽनिरुद्धम् ।।१८८॥।
```

---

## Literal Translation

वे-कुण्ठ-देशे (ve-kuṇṭha-deśe), वेकुण्ठ (ve-kuṇṭha), masculine locative singular; देश (deśa), masculine locative singular; tatpuruṣa  
खलु (khalu), indeclinable (emphatic particle)  
वासुदेवम् (vāsudevam), वासुदेव (Vāsudeva), masculine accusative singular  
आमोद-सञ्जे (āmoda-sañje), आमोद (āmoda) noun, masculine locative singular + संज(1) (sañja), noun masculine locative singular; bahuvrīhi compound "joy and festival" locative singular  
त्वथ (tvatha), indeclinable (corrupt form of 'tatha' or 'tvayam atha' unclear, treated as indeclinable)  
कर्षण-आख्यम् (karṣaṇa-ākhyam), कर्षण (karṣaṇa) masculine accusative singular + आख्य (ākhyá) (called named) accusative singular; tatpuruṣa compound  
प्रद्युम्रम् (pradyumram), प्रद्युम्न (Pradyumna) masculine accusative singular  
अल्जाक्षम् (aljākṣam), अल्जाक्ष (aljākṣa), masculine accusative singular  
अपि (api), indeclinable (also)  
प्रमोदे (pramode), प्रमोद (pramoda), masculine locative singular  
सम्मोद् (sammod), masculine (masc/neuter) nominative/vocative singular, imperative verb "rejoice" (3rd person plural imperative) middle voice (assumed from root)  
ईशन्तु (īśantu), verb, 3rd person plural imperative, active voice, from root ईश् (īś) "to rule" or "to be lord"  
तथा (tathā), indeclinable (thus)  
अनिरुद्धम् (aniruddham), अनिरुद्ध (Aniruddha) masculine accusative singular

---

## Smooth Translation

In the land of Vaikuṇṭha indeed Vasudeva (is present) in a joyous festival, likewise called Karṣaṇa.  
Pradyumna, Aljākṣa also in joy and pleasure rejoice, may they rule thus Aniruddha.
