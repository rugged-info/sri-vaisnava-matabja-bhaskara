# Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
SOURCE_PAGES: page_0279.txt

=== मूलपाठः ===


गुरुद्रुहे नो न शठाय चेदं न नास्तिकायोपदिशेत्कदाचित् ।
नावैष्णवायापि रहस्यमेतन्न चाथ दैन्याहतवेदनाय ।।२०२।।


gurudruhe no na shaṭhāya cedaṃ na nāstikāyopadiśetkadācit |
nāvaiṣṇavāyāpi rahasyametanna cātha dainyāhatavedanāya ||202||


gurudruhe (guru-druha, guru-druha, masculine singular locative, tatpuruṣa)  
no (naḥ, first person plural genitive or dative; here dative)  
na (na, indeclinable negative particle)  
shaṭhāya (shaṭha, śaṭha, masculine singular dative)  
cedaṃ (ca + idaṃ, indeclinable + pronoun, nominative/accusative singular neuter; conditional indeclinable "if")  
na (na, indeclinable negative particle)  
nāstikāya (nāstika, adjective, masculine singular dative)  
upadiśet (upadiś, verb root "to instruct", verb form 3rd person singular optative active present)  
kadācit (kadācit, indeclinable adverb "ever")  
nā (na, indeclinable negative particle)  
avaiṣṇavāya (a + vaiṣṇava, adjective, masculine singular dative, tatpuruṣa or bahuvrīhi)  
api (api, indeclinable particle "also")  
rahasya (rahasya, neuter singular nominative/accusative noun "secret")  
etat (etat, demonstrative pronoun, singular neuter nominative/accusative)  
na (na, indeclinable negative particle)  
ca (ca, indeclinable particle "also/and")  
atha (atha, indeclinable particle "now/then")  
dainyāhata (dainya + āhata, dainya (neuter noun), āhata (participle "struck/hit"), masculine singular dative)  
vedanāya (vedanā, feminine singular dative "to suffering/pain")  


If anyone is a betrayer of the guru, or a cheat, (cedam na) never should he instruct a nāstika (atheist) ever.  
Nor should this secret be told even to a non-Vaishnava, for the pain caused by helplessness.  


If someone is a betrayer of the guru or dishonest, then he should never under any circumstance teach an atheist.  
Nor even to a non-Vaishnava should this secret ever be revealed, for it causes painful distress.
```
