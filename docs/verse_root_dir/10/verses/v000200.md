## Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
=== मूलपाठः ===
```

---

## Transliteration (IAST)

```text
तोताद्रिसञ्ज्ञादिषु वैष्णवोत्तमैरेवं तथा तुङ्शयादिकं प्रभुम्।
कार्यो निवासो नितरां शुभार्थिभिराराधयद्धिः सकलार्थदायिनम् ।।२००।।
```

---

## Literal Translation

तोताद्रिसञ्ज्ञादिषु (totādrisañjñādiṣu): tota-ādri-sañjñā-ādiṣu; tatpuruṣa; locative plural neuter; "in those called Totadri and similar"
वैष्णवोत्तमैः (vaiṣṇavottamaiḥ): vaiṣṇava-uttam-āḥ; instrumental plural masculine; tatpuruṣa compound; "by the best of the Vaiṣṇavas"
एवं (evaṁ): indeclinable; "thus"
तथा (tathā): indeclinable; "also"
तुङ्शयादिकं (tuṅśayādikaṁ): tuṅśaya-ādikaṁ; bahuvrīhi; accusative singular masculine; "Tuṅśaya and others"
प्रभुम् (prabhum): prabhu; accusative singular masculine; "the Lord"

---

## Smooth Translation

कार्यः (kāryaḥ): kārya; nominative singular masculine; "to be done"
निवासः (nivāsaḥ): nivāsa; nominative singular masculine; "dwelling"
नितरां (nitarāṁ): adverb; "exceedingly"
शुभार्थिभिः (śubhārthibhiḥ): śubha-artha-bhiḥ; instrumental plural masculine; "by auspicious-minded ones"
आराधयत् (ārādhayat): verb; root आराध्, 3rd person singular imperfect active; "was worshipping"
धिः (dhiḥ): dhiḥ; noun nominative singular feminine; "intellect" or "mind"
सकलार्थदायिनम् (sakalārthadāyinam): sakala-artha-dāyinam; accusative singular masculine; tatpuruṣa; "giver of all benefits"
