## Smooth Translation

At the greatly auspicious confluence of the Ganges, Gaṅgā, and the ocean, (one) who is Viṣṇu and also Rāghava,  
in the permanent abode of the qualities of the moon, surrounded by sages, in glorious Citrakūṭa, the illustrious one,  
in the village of Nandi, on the mountain of noble fame, the illustrious terror of demons, the lord, in the beautiful place,  
the revered one possessing universal forms, eminent Kṣatra, shines in the pure illumination.
