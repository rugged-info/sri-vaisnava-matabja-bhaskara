# Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
SOURCE_PAGES: page_0278.txt

=== मूलपाठः ===


सदाऽनुसन्धेयमिमं त्रिकालं मुमुक्षभिस्तत्परमार्थमित्थम् ।
ज्ञात्वा न चैवास्ति सुवेदनीयं जिज्ञासुभिस्तैरविशिष्यमाणम् ।।२०१।।

sadā'nusandheyamimaṃ trikālaṃ mumukṣabhistatparamārthamittham ।
jñātvā na caivāsti suvedanīyaṃ jijñāsubhistairaviṣiṣyamāṇam ॥201॥

सदा (sadā) — sadā — indeclinable, adverb, "always"  
अनुसन्धेयम् (anusandheyam) — anusandheya — verbal adjective (gerundive), neuter singular accusative, "to be attended to"  
इमं (imaṃ) — idam — pronoun, masculine/neuter singular accusative, "this"  
त्रिकालं (trikālaṃ) — trikāla — noun, masculine singular accusative, "the three times"  
मुमुक्षभिः (mumukṣabhiḥ) — mumukṣa — noun, masculine plural instrumental, "by the seekers of liberation"  
तत् (tat) — tat — pronoun, neuter singular accusative, "that"  
परमार्थम् (paramārtham) — paramārtha — noun, masculine singular accusative, "supreme goal"  
इत्थम् (ittham) — iti + suffix, indeclinable, "thus, in this manner"  
ज्ञात्वा (jñātvā) — jñā — verbal participle (gerund), "having known"  
न (na) — na — indeclinable, negation particle, "not"  
च (ca) — ca — indeclinable, conjunction, "and"  
एव (eva) — eva — indeclinable, particle, "indeed"  
अस्ति (asti) — as — verb, 3rd person singular present indicative active, "exists"  
सुवेदनीयं (suvedanīyam) — suvedanīya — verbal adjective (gerundive), neuter singular accusative, "to be well-understood"  
जिज्ञासुभि: (jijñāsubhiḥ) — jijñāsu — noun, masculine plural instrumental, "by the inquirers"  
तैः (taiḥ) — tat — pronoun, masculine/neuter plural instrumental, "by them"  
अविषिष्यमाणम् (aviṣiṣyamāṇam) — avīṣiṣyamāṇa — present passive participle, neuter singular accusative, "being distinguished, to be specially marked"  

Always this (imaṃ) three times (trikālaṃ) must be attended to (anusandheyam) by seekers of liberation (mumukṣabhiḥ) — that (tat) supreme truth (paramārtham) in this way (ittham).  
Having known (jñātvā) this, not (na) indeed (eva) is (asti) it (suvedanīyam) to be well-understood (suvedanīyam) by the inquirers (jijñāsubhiḥ) distinguished (aviṣiṣyamāṇam) by them (taiḥ).  

Seekers of liberation should always attend to this threefold time, that supreme truth, thus. Having known it, indeed, it is not to be clearly distinguished by the inquirers.
```
