## Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
=== मूलपाठः ===
```

---

## Transliteration (IAST)

```text
जितेद्धियः प्रत्रः सन् बुधः श्रीजानकीपतिम् ।
अनुष्ठाय मतं चेदं परमं स्थानमाप्रुयात् ।।२०३॥
```

---

## Literal Translation

जितेद्धियः (jiteddhiyaḥ), जित (jit), adjective (प्रथमा एकवचन पुल्लिङ्ग ऋत्विकसत्त्ववाचक)
प्रत्रः (pratraḥ), प्रत्र (pratra), noun masculine nominative singular
सन् (san), अस् (as), present participle active nominative singular masculine
बुधः (budhaḥ), बुद्ध (buddha), adjective/noun masculine nominative singular
श्रीजानकीपतिम् (śrījānakīpatim), श्री-जानकी-पतिः (śrī-jānakī-pati), compound tatpuruṣa, noun masculine accusative singular
अनुष्ठाय (anuṣṭhāya), अनुष्ठा (anuṣṭhā), gerund (absolutive form)
मतं (mataṃ), मत् (mat), noun neuter accusative singular (object)
चेदं (cedaṃ), च एतानि (ca + etaṃ), indeclinable particle ('and' + demonstrative pronoun neuter accusative singular)
परमं (paramaṃ), परम (param), adjective neuter accusative singular
स्थानम् (sthānam), स्थान (sthāna), noun neuter accusative singular
आप्रुयात् (āpruyāt), causative verb root प्रि (pri) with prefix आ (ā), optative mood 3rd person singular active

---

## Smooth Translation

(jiteddhiyaḥ) — "having conquered the intellect" (nom. sg. m. part. adjectival phrase)
(pratraḥ) — "wise man" (nom. sg. m.)
(san) — "being" (present participle active)
(budhaḥ) — "intelligent, wise" (nom. sg. m.)
(śrījānakīpatim) — "the lord of Sri Janaki" (acc. sg. m.)
(anuṣṭhāya) — "performing, having done" (gerund)
(mataṃ) — "the opinion, belief" (acc. sg. n., object)
(cedaṃ) — "and this" (particle with demonstrative pronoun)
(paramaṃ sthānam) — "supreme place" (accusative neuter singular)
(āpruyāt) — "may reach, may attain" (optative 3rd singular active, causative verb form)
