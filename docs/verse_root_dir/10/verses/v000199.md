# Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
SOURCE_PAGES: page_0277.txt

=== मूलपाठः ===


सुरेन्द्रवन्धं वरदं त्वहर्दिवं सुनिर्मले श्रीशुभवारणाचले ।
काञ्यां तथा श्रीकमलायताक्षकं समर्चनीयं बुधवैष्णवोत्तमैः ।।१९९।।

surendravandhaṃ varadaṃ tvahardivaṃ sunirmale śrīśubhavāraṇācale ।
kāñyāṃ tathā śrīkamalayatākṣakaṃ samarcaṇīyaṃ budhavaṃṣṇavottamaiḥ 199 ॥

सुरेन्द्रवन्धं (surendrāvandhaṃ), सुरेन्द्र + वन्धं, tatpuruṣa compound; accusative singular neuter  
वरदं (varadaṃ), वरद, adjective; accusative singular neuter  
त्व (tva), indeclinable particle (emphatic)  
हरदिवं (haradivaṃ), हर + दिव, bahuvrīhi compound; accusative singular neuter  
सुनिर्मले (sunirmale), सु + निर्मल, adjective; locative singular masculine/neuter (likely locative singular)  
श्रीशुभवारणाचले (śrīśubhavāraṇācale), श्री + शुभ + वारण + अचले, bahuvrīhi compound; locative singular masculine  
काञ्यां (kāñyāṃ), काञ्या, noun feminine; accusative singular  
तथा (tathā), indeclinable adverb (also, likewise)  
श्रीकमलायताक्षकं (śrīkamalayatākṣakaṃ), श्री + कमल + अय + ताक्षकं, tatpuruṣa compound; accusative singular neuter  
समर्चनीयं (samarcaṇīyaṃ), समर्चनीय, adjective; neuter singular accusative; gerundive/participle indicating suitability  
बुधवैष्णवोत्तमैः (budhavaíṣṇavottamaiḥ), बुद्ध + वैष्णव + उत्तम + ऐः, bahuvrīhi compound; instrumental plural masculine

"sureṃdravandhaṃ" = homage to the lord of gods (surendra), accusative singular  
"varadaṃ" = bestowing boons, adjective, accusative singular  
"tva" = indeed (particle)  
"haradivaṃ" = like the sun, accusative singular  
"sunirmale" = very pure, locative singular  
"śrīśubhavāraṇācale" = on the auspicious Varana hill, locative singular  
"kāñyāṃ" = to the maiden, accusative singular  
"tathā" = likewise  
"śrīkamalayatākṣakaṃ" = having lotus-like eyes (kamala = lotus), auspicious, accusative singular  
"samarcaṇīyaṃ" = worthy of worship, accusative singular  
"budhavaiṣṇavottamaiḥ" = by the best of wise Vaiṣṇavas, instrumental plural

Homage to the lord of gods, the boon-giver, indeed like the sun, very pure on the auspicious Varana hill. To the maiden likewise, lotus-eyed and auspicious, worthy of worship by the best of wise Vaiṣṇavas.

Homage to the lord of gods, the boon-giver, shining like the sun, very pure on the auspicious Varana hill. Likewise, to the maiden with lotus-like eyes, worthy of worship by the foremost wise Vaiṣṇavas.
```
