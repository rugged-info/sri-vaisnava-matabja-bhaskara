# Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
SOURCE_PAGES: page_0277.txt

=== मूलपाठः ===


श्रीविदरुलं तं किल पाण्डुरङ्ग श्रीवेङ्कटाद्रौ तु रमासखं च ।
नारायणं श्रीमति यादवाद्रौ नृसिंहमित्थं घटिकाचलेऽपि ।।१९८।।

śrīvidarulaṃ taṃ kila pāṇḍuraṅga śrī veṅkaṭādrau tu ramāsakhaṃ ca |
nārāyaṇaṃ śrīmati yādavādrau nṛsiṃhamitthaṃ ghaṭikācale'pi ||198||

श्रीविदरुलं (śrī-vidarulaṃ) — śrīvidarula, accusative singular, masculine; tatpuruṣa compound (śrī + vidarula)
तँ (taṃ) — taṃ, pronoun, accusative singular, masculine; demonstrative pronoun
किल (kila) — kila, indeclinable, particle meaning “indeed” or “surely”
पाण्डुरङ्ग (pāṇḍuraṅga) — pāṇḍuraṅga, nominative singular, masculine; a proper noun (name)
श्रीवेङ्कटाद्रौ (śrī-veṅkaṭādrau) — śrī veṅkaṭa-adrau, locative dual; tatpuruṣa compound (śrī + veṅkaṭa + adrau)
तु (tu) — tu, indeclinable, particle, “but” or “indeed”
रमासखं (ramāsakhaṃ) — rama-asakhaṃ, accusative singular, masculine; bahuvrīhi compound (rama + asakha)
च (ca) — ca, indeclinable, conjunction “and”
नारायणं (nārāyaṇaṃ) — nārāyaṇa, accusative singular, masculine; proper noun, the deity’s name
श्रीमति (śrīmati) — śrīmati, locative singular feminine; proper noun or respectful title indicating goddess or consort (here, consort of Vishnu)
यादवाद्रौ (yādavādrau) — yādava-adrau, locative dual; tatpuruṣa compound (yādava + adrau)
नृसिंहमित्थं (nṛsiṃhamitthaṃ) — nṛsiṃha-mitthaṃ, accusative singular, masculine; bahuvrīhi compound (nṛsiṃha + mittha)
घटिकाचलेऽपि (ghaṭikācale'pi) — ghaṭikācala + api; locative singular + indeclinable particle “also”

Śrīvidarulaṃ taṃ kila pāṇḍuraṅga śrī veṅkaṭādrau tu ramāsakhaṃ ca |
Nārāyaṇaṃ śrīmati yādavādrau nṛsiṃhamitthaṃ ghaṭikācale'pi ||

Literal translation (with notes):  
(śrīvidarulam) the revered Vidarula (accusative singular masculine, direct object)  
(taṃ) him (demonstrative pronoun, accusative singular masculine, direct object)  
(kila) surely / indeed (particle)  
(pāṇḍuraṅga) Paṇḍuranga (nominative singular masculine, subject)  
(śrī veṅkaṭādrau) at the two Veṅkaṭa mountains (locative dual)  
(tu) but / however (particle)  
(ramāsakhaṃ) friend of Rāma (accusative singular masculine, direct object)  
(ca) and (conjunction)  
(nārāyaṇaṃ) Narayana (accusative singular masculine, direct object)  
(śrīmati) to the goddess (locative singular feminine)  
(yādavādrau) at the two Yadava (mountains/places) (locative dual)  
(nṛsiṃhamitthaṃ) friend of Nṛsiṃha (accusative singular masculine, direct object)  
(ghaṭikācale'pi) also at the Ghaṭikācalam (locative singular + particle “also”)

Paṇḍuranga, indeed, is that Vidarula; and at the two Veṅkaṭa hills, Rama’s friend; Narayana to the goddess at the two Yadava hills; the friend of Nṛsiṃha also at Ghaṭikācalam.

Smooth English translation:  
Indeed, that Vidarula is Paṇḍuranga; at the twin Veṅkaṭa hills, he is Rama’s companion; Narayana with the goddess is at the twin Yadava hills; and he is also the friend of Nṛsiṃha at Ghaṭikācalam.
```
