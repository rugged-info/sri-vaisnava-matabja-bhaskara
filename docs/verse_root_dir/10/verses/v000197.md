## Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
=== मूलपाठः ===
```

---

## Transliteration (IAST)

```text
श्रैताद्रौ त्वथ सिंहरूपिणसमथी श्रीधर्मपुर्या तथा  
योगानन्दमशेषदेवसुनतं श्रीकाकुले तु प्रभुम् ।  
देवेर्वन्द्यमथान्ध्रनायकमिह श्रीदं तथाऽहोबिले  
तस्मिञ् श्रीगरुडादिसजञ्ज्ञ उचिते देवं हिरण्यार्दनम् ।।१९७।।
```

---

## Literal Translation

श्रैताद्रौ (śraitādrau) - śraitā + drau; dual locative/vocative? (compound unclear, possibly bahuvrīhi),  
त्व (tva) - tvad, pronoun, 2nd person singular nominative/vocative, indeclinable (particle/emphasis),  
अथ (atha) - atha, indeclinable, "then", particle,  
सिंहरूपिणसमथी (siṁharūpiṇasamathī) - siṁha-rūpiṇa + samathī; bahuvrīhi compound: siṁha (lion, masc), rūpiṇa (having form/shape), samathī (calm/steady one, fem), singular nominative feminine;  
श्रीधर्मपुर्या (śrīdharmapuryā) - śrī-dharma-puryā; tatpuruṣa locative singular feminine (from śrī-dharma-pura, "glorious dharma city");  
तथा (tathā) - tathā, indeclinable, "thus", "also", particle;

---

## Smooth Translation

योगानन्दमशेषदेवसुनतं (yogānandamaśeṣadevasunataṁ) - yoga-ananda + maśeṣa + devasunataṁ; complex bahuvrīhi compound, masculine singular accusative; yoga-ananda (joy of yoga), maśeṣa (without remainder/complete), devasunata (dev-sunata = deity praised) "fully praised deity";  
श्रीकाकुले (śrīkākule) - śrī-kā-kule; locative singular compound, "at the family of Śrīkā", (kula = family),  
तु (tu) - tu, indeclinable, "but", particle;  
प्रभुम् (prabhum) - prabhu, noun, masculine singular accusative, "lord";
