# Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
SOURCE_PAGES: page_0119.txt

=== मूलपाठः ===


प्रसन्नलावण्यसुभ्नमुखाम्बुजं जगच्छरण्यं शरणं नरोत्तमम् ।
दयापरं दाशरथिं महोत्सवं स्मरामि रामं सह सीतया सदा ।।५९।।

prasannalāvaṇyasubhnamukhāmbujaṃ jagaccharaṇyaṃ śaraṇaṃ narottamam ।
dayāparaṃ dāśarathiṃ mahotsavaṃ smarāmi rāmaṃ saha sītayā sadā ।।59।।

प्रसन्नलावण्यसुभ्नमुखाम्बुजं (prasannalāvaṇyasubhnamukhāmbujaṃ)  
 - prasanna (prasanna), adj., nominative/accusative singular neuter, "pleased, clear"  
 - lāvaṇya (lāvaṇya), noun, nominative/accusative singular neuter, "beauty, grace"  
 - subhna (subhna), adj., nominative/accusative singular neuter, "beautiful, radiant"  
 - mukha (mukha), noun, nominative/accusative singular neuter, "face"  
 - ambujaṃ (ambuja), noun, accusative singular masculine, "lotus"  
 (compound tatpuruṣa or bahuvrīhi, compound meaning "lotus-faced having pleasing grace and beauty")  

जगच्छरण्यं (jagaccharaṇyaṃ)  
 - jagat (jagat), noun, nominative singular neuter, "world"  
 - śaraṇyaṃ (śaraṇya), adj./noun, accusative singular neuter, "refuge, shelter"  
 (tatpuruṣa compound meaning "refuge of the world")  

शरणं (śaraṇaṃ)  
 - śaraṇa (śaraṇa), noun, accusative singular neuter, "refuge"  

नरोत्तमम् (narottamam)  
 - nara (nara), noun, nominative singular masculine, "man"  
 - uttama (uttama), adj., nominative singular masculine, "best"  
 (tatpuruṣa compound "best among men")  

दयापरं (dayāparaṃ)  
 - dayā (dayā), noun, accusative singular neuter, "compassion"  
 - para (para), adj., accusative singular neuter, "supreme, beyond"  
 (tatpuruṣa compound "supreme in compassion")  

दाशरथिं (dāśarathiṃ)  
 - dāśaratha (dāśaratha), noun, accusative singular masculine, "son of Daśaratha" (epithet of Rāma)  

महोत्सवं (mahotsavaṃ)  
 - maha (maha), adj., accusative singular masculine/neuter, "great"  
 - utsava (utsava), noun, accusative singular masculine/neuter, "festival, celebration"  
 (tatpuruṣa compound "great festival")  

स्मरामि (smarāmi)  
 - verb from √smṛ (to remember), 1st person singular present indicative active, "I remember"  

रामं (rāmaṃ)  
 - rāma (rāma), noun, accusative singular masculine, "Rāma"  

सह (saha)  
 - indeclinable, "with"  

सीतया (sītayā)  
 - sītā (sītā), noun, instrumental singular feminine, "Sītā"  

सदा (sadā)  
 - indeclinable adverb, "always"  

Literal translation (with grammatical notes):  
"I remember (1st person sing. pres.) Rāma (acc.) the best among men (nominative masc.), the refuge (acc. neut.) of the world (nom. neut.), the lotus-faced (acc. masc.) having clear grace and beauty; (I remember) the great festival (acc. masc.), the son of Daśaratha (acc. masc.), supreme in compassion (acc. neut.), always with Sītā (instr. fem.) together (indeclinable)."

Smooth English translation:  
"I always remember Rāma, the best of men, the refuge of the world, the lotus-faced one adorned with clear grace and beauty; the compassionate son of Daśaratha, together with Sītā, during the great festival."
```
