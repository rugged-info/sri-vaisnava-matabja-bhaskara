# Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
SOURCE_PAGES: page_0117.txt

=== मूलपाठः ===


मुनिमनस्सुमधुत्रतचुम्बितस्फुटलसन्मकरन्दपदाम्बुजम् ।
बलवददृश्रुतदिव्यधनुश्शरामहितजानुविलम्बिमहाभरुजम् ।। ५७।।  

munimanssumadhutratacumbitasphuṭalasanmakarandapadāmbujam ।  
balavadadṛśrutadivyadhanuśśarāmahitajānavilambimahābharujam ॥ 57 ॥  

मुनी (muni), मुनि, masculine, nominative singular  
मनस् (manas), मनस्, neuter, nominative singular (compounded with मुनि in bahuvrīhi)  
सुमधु (sumadhu), सुमधु, adjective, nominative singular neuter (describes तत्)  
त्रत (trata), त्रत्, adjective, nominative singular neuter (describes तत्)  
च (ca), indeclinable, conjunction  
उम्बित (umbita), उपसर्ग पद, past participle, nominative singular neuter (part of compound describing पदम्)  
स्फुटल (sphuṭala), adjective, nominative singular neuter (describes पदम्)  
सन् (san), present participle, adjective, nominative singular neuter (describes पदम्)  
मकरन्द (makaranda), noun, neuter, nominative singular (in tatpuruṣa compound with पदम्)  
पदा (padā), पद्, noun, feminine, nominative singular (in tatpuruṣa compound with अम्बुजम्)  
अम्बुजम् (ambujam), noun, masculine, accusative singular (compound head)  

बलवत् (balavat), adjective, nominative singular masculine (describes धरुजम्)  
दृश्रुत (dṛśruta), adjective, nominative singular masculine (describes धनुः)  
दिव्य (divya), adjective, nominative singular masculine (describes धनुः)  
धनुः (dhanuḥ), noun, masculine, nominative singular (compound head)  
श (śa), indeclinable (sandhi form of विशेषण "श" or possibly connecting particle)  
रामहित (rāma-hita), tatpuruṣa compound, adjective, nominative singular masculine (describes विलम्बि)  
जानु (jānu), noun, neuter, nominative singular (in tatpuruṣa compound with विलम्बि)  
विलम्बि (vilambi), adjective, nominative singular masculine (describes धरुजम्)  
महाभरुज (mahābharuja), compound adjective, nominative singular masculine (describes पदम्)  

Literal translation:  
O lotus of the feet of the sage whose mind is touched by very sweet and sharp nectar,  
like mighty, sharp-heard divine bow, graceful with Rāma, the knee-suspending great-armed one.  

Smooth English translation:  
O lotus of the feet of the sage’s mind imbued with sweet and piercing nectar,  
strong as a divine bow renowned, graced by Rāma, the mighty one with great arms and bending knees.
```
