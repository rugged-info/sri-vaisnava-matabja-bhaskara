## Transliteration (IAST)

```text
इति।
परार्ध्वहाराङ्गदचारुनूपुरं सुपदाकिञ्चल्कपिशङ्वाससम् ।
लसद्घनश्यामतनुं गुणाकरं कृपार्णवं सद्धदयाम्बुजासनम् ।। ५८ ।।
```

---

## Literal Translation

iti (इति), iti, indeclinable (particle, quotation)
parārdhva-hāra-aṅgada-cāru-nūpuraṁ (परार्ध्वहाराङ्गदचारुनूपुरं), 
   parārdhva (परार्ध्व), base noun, masculine, indeclinable compounding in tatpuruṣa, 
   hāra (हारा), base noun, masculine, tatpuruṣa 
   aṅgada (अङ्गद), base noun, masculine, tatpuruṣa 
   cāru (चारु), adjective, masculine nominative 
   nūpuraṁ (नूपुरं), noun, neuter singular accusative;
supadā-kiñcala-kapi-śaṅva-āsa-sam (सुपदाकिञ्चल्कपिशङ्वाससम्), 
   supadā (सुपदा), nominal compound element, feminine nominative
   kiñcala (किञ्चल), adjective
   kapi (कपि), noun (monkey), masculine nominative
   śaṅva (शङ्व), noun (tail), masculine nominative
   āsa (आस), noun (clothing or covering), masculine nominative
   sam (सम्), indeclinable particle ('with', 'together with')
lasad-ghana-śyāma-tanuṁ (लसद्घनश्यामतनुं),
   lasad (लसद), adjective ('light', 'shining')
   ghana (घन), adjective ('dense')
   śyāma (श्याम), adjective ('dark', 'blue-black')
   tanuṁ (तनुं), noun, masculine singular accusative ('body')
guṇā-karaṁ (गुणाकरं),
   guṇa (गुण), noun masculine base 'quality' or 'virtue'
   akaraṁ (करं), verbal noun ('maker'); here combined to mean 'bestower of qualities'
kṛpā-rṇavaṁ (कृपार्णवं),
   kṛpā (कृपा), noun feminine ('mercy')
   ṛṇavaṁ (र्णव), noun neuter singular, 'ocean', 'sea'
saddhaya-ambuja-āsanam (सद्धदयाम्बुजासनम्),
   sad+daya (सद्धय), adjective ('good-hearted')
   ambuja (अम्बुज), noun masculine, 'lotus'
   āsanam (आसनम्), noun neuter singular, 'seat'
