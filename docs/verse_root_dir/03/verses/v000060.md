# Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
SOURCE_PAGES: page_0119.txt

=== मूलपाठः ===


दविभुजस्यैव रामस्य सर्वशक्तेः प्रियोत्तम ।
ध्यानमेवं विधातव्यं सदा रामपरायणैः ।।६०।।  

davibhujasyaiva rāmasya sarvaśakteḥ priyottama  
dhyānamevaṃ vidhātavyaṃ sadā rāmaparāyaṇaiḥ  

दविभुजस्य (davibhujasya), दविभुज (compound: तातपुरुषः, genitive singular masculine),  
ऐव (aiva), एव (indecinable, emphatic particle),  
रामस्य (rāmasya), राम (noun, genitive singular masculine),  
सर्वशक्तेः (sarvaśakteḥ), सर्वशक्ति (compound: बहुव्रीहि; genitive singular feminine),  
प्रियोत्तम (priyottama), प्रिय (compound: बहुव्रीहि), उत्तम (adjective, nominative singular masculine)

ध्यानम् (dhyānam), ध्यान (noun, accusative singular neuter),  
एवम् (evaṃ), एवम् (indeclinable, adverb),  
विधातव्यं (vidhātavyaṃ), विधातव्य (participle gerundive, accusative singular neuter),  
सदा (sadā), सदा (indeclinable, adverb),  
रामपरायणैः (rāmaparāyaṇaiḥ), रामपरायण (compound: तत्पुरुषः), instrumental plural masculine

Literal translation (with grammatical notes):  
"Only of the two-armed Rama (genitive case), of all power (genitive case), most dear (nominative masculine singular).  
Meditation thus (adverb) to be established (gerundive accusative singular) always by those devoted to Rama (instrumental plural)."

Smooth English translation:  
"The two-armed Rama alone, the dearest of all powers,  
Meditation like this should always be practiced by those devoted to Rama."
```
