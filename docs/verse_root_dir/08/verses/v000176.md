# Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
SOURCE_PAGES: page_0243.txt

=== मूलपाठः ===


उथि नवमप्रश्रीत्तरे प्राप्यनिरूपणम्  
तथाविधेस्तैः परमार्थभूतं सुवेष्णवैः प्राप्यमथीच्यते यत् ।  
जितेद्धियैरात्मरतेर्बुधाग्यैर्महत्तमैः स्वाभिमतार्थदोहम् ।।१७६ ।।

uthi navamapraśrīttare prāpyanirūpaṇam  
tathāvidhestaiḥ paramārthabhūtaṃ suveṣṇavaih prāpyamathīcyate yat  
jiteddhiyairātmaraterbudhāgyairmahattamaiḥ svābhimatārthadoham

(उथि) (उथि) (indeclinable, particle)  
(नवम) (नवम) (adjective, nominative singular neuter)  
(अप्रश्रीत्तरे) (अप्रश्रीत्तरे) (locative singular feminine, compound)  
(प्राप्यनिरूपणम्) (प्राप्यनिरूपणम्) (neuter, nominative singular, verbal noun/gerund compound)  

(तथा) (तथा) (indeclinable, particle)  
(विद्-एस्-तैः) (एतैः) (pronoun, instrumental plural masculine)  
(परमार्थ-भूतं) (परमार्थ-भूतं) (adjective, nominative singular neuter, tatpuruṣa)  
(सुवेष्णवैः) (सुवेष्णवैः) (instrumental plural masculine, bahuvrihi)  
(प्राप्यम्) (प्राप्यम्) (accusative singular neuter, verbal adjective)  
(अथीच्यते) (अथीच्यते) (verb, present passive indicative 3rd singular)  
(यत्) (यत्) (relative pronoun, nominative singular neuter)  

(जिते) (जिते) (past passive participle, instrumental plural masculine)  
(धियैः) (धि) (noun, instrumental plural feminine)  
(आत्मरतेः) (आत्मरति:) (noun, instrumental plural masculine)  
(बुधाग्यैः) (बुधाग्यैः) (noun, instrumental plural masculine)  
(महत्तम-ैः) (महत्तम) (superlative adjective, instrumental plural masculine)  
(स्वाभिमत-आर्थ-दोहम्) (स्वाभिमत-आर्थ-दोहम्) (neuter noun compound, accusative singular)  

Thus (उथि) in the new ninth prashriti, by these (तथा...तैः) true (परमार्थ-भूतं) great intense (सुवेष्णवैः) means (बहुव्रीहि) (प्राप्यम्) is to be apprehended (अथीच्यते), that (यत्) which (जिते धियैः आत्मरतेः बुधाग्यैः महत्तमैः) by conquered intellects, self-delights, wise men, and the greatest ones (instrumental plurals), is the self-chosen (स्वाभिमत) desired meaning (आर्थ) granter (दोहम्).

In the new ninth chapter, that ultimate reality, to be realized by these great and intense means, is explained; it is the self-chosen desired object for wise and self-delighting intellects who have conquered.
```
