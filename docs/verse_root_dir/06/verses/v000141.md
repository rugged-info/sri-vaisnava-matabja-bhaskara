## Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
=== मूलपाठः ===
```

---

## Transliteration (IAST)

```text
अनादिकर्मोत्करजातनानादेहाभिमानी सुमतोऽथ बद्धः ।
स चाऽच्युताहेतुकृपाबलेनाविद्याक्रियावासरुचिप्रवृत्तेः ।।१४१।।
```

---

## Literal Translation

अनादि (anādi), अनादि, adjective, m. nom. sg. (without beginning);
कर्म (karma), कर्म, neut. nom. sg. (action, deed);
उत्कर (utkara), उत्कर, adjective, m. nom. sg. (excellent, high);
जात (jāta), जात्, adjective, m. nom. sg. (born, arisen);
अनान (anāna), अनान, adjective, m. nom. sg. (without many, here "not numerous");
अदेहाभिमानी (adehābhimānī), compound of अ (a, negation) + देह + अभिमानी, masculine nominative singular; deha (body) + abhimāna (pride, identification), m. nom. sg., so "not identifying with the body";
सुमत (sumata), सुमत, adjective, m. nom. sg. (well-minded, wise);
अथ (atha), ath, indeclinable (then, now);
बद्धः (baddhaḥ), बद्ध, adjective, m. nom. sg. (bound, tied);
स (sa), स, pronoun m. nom. sg. (he);
च (ca), indeclinable (and);
अच्युत-हेतु (acyutāhetu), compound tatpuruṣa: अच्युत (Achyuta, Vishnu) + हेतु (hetu, cause), m. inst. sg. (by the cause of Achyuta);
कृपा (kṛpā), कृपा, f. inst. sg. (by mercy);
बलेन (balena), बल्, neut. inst. sg. (by strength);
अविद्या (avidyā), अविद्या, f. abl. sg. (from ignorance);
कृया (kriyā), कृया, f. abl. sg. (from activity, action);
अवसरुचि (avasaruci), compound tatpuruṣa or bahuvrīhi; here abl. sg. fem. ambiguous but roughly "lack of liking for opportunity/time";
प्रवृत्तेः (pravṛtteḥ), प्रवृत्ति, f. gen. sg. (of duty, activity, proceeding)

---

## Smooth Translation

Literal translation (with grammatical notes):
"Without beginning, action-excellent, arisen, not many, not-body-identifying, well-minded, then bound; 
he also (he) by Achyuta-cause, by mercy, by strength, from ignorance, from activity, from lack-of-liking-for-opportunity/proceeding."
