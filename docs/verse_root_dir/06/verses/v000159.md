## Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
=== मूलपाठः ===
```

---

## Transliteration (IAST)

```text
समुच्यते सम्प्रति लक्षणं सन्महात्मनां सदुगुणवैष्णवानाम् ।
विरिद्चिशम्भुश्रितरामचन्द्रपदारविन्दस्थितचेतसां हि ।।१५९॥।
```

---

## Literal Translation

samucyate (samucyate, √uc 'to gather, to collect', 3rd person singular present indicative passive)  
samprati (sam + prati, indeclinable, adverb; sam = 'together', prati = 'towards')  
lakṣṇaṃ (lakṣṇa, masculine/neuter, singular, accusative; 'mark, sign, characteristic')  
sanmahātmanāṃ (saṃ + mahātmanāṃ, tatpuruṣa compound; mahātmanāṃ = mahātman (great soul) instrumental plural; saṃ as prefix intensifying or together)  
saduguṇavaiṣṇavānām (compound: saduguṇa + vaiṣṇavānām, bahuvrīhi; vaiṣṇavānām = vaiṣṇava (follower of Viṣṇu) genitive plural; sadu + guṇa = possessing good qualities)

---

## Smooth Translation

virid (virid, adjective or noun; probably a compound part or name component)  
ciśambhuśritāramacandrapadāravindasthitacetasāṃ (compound consisting of ciśambhu + śritāram + acandra + padāravinda + sthita + cetasāṃ; cetasāṃ = cetas (mind) genitive plural; padāravinda = padā + aravinda (lotus-like feet); sthita = past participle 'situated'; ciśambhu = with Śambhu (Śiva), śrit = śruta/śrit (having refuge), aram probably ram (Rāma))  
hi (indeclinable particle: indeed)
