## Smooth Translation

"As stated (yathoktaṃ) by the compassionate ocean (dayānidheḥ) who is the accomplisher (sādhaka) of all human purposes (samasta puruṣārthānām), by the revered one (śrīmataḥ), due to previous realization (pūrvasiddhatvāt), they (viduḥ) know this (imam) means of realization (siddhopāyam). On falling down (prapatati) in the sign (lakṣaṇe), the lawgiver (vidhī) thinks (mante) in the alternate sentences (vākyāntareṣu) and also at the beginning of the commentary and tradition (bhāṣyādau sampradāye), the means (upāyatvaṃ) is established (sthitam) in Brahman (brahmaṇi), thus (iti).
