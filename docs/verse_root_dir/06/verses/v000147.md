=== मूलपाठः ===


यथोक्तं 'समस्तपुरुषार्थानां साधकस्य दयानिधेः। श्रीमतः पूर्वसिद्धत्वात् सिद्धोपायमिमं
विदुः | प्रपततेर्लक्षणे मन्ते विधी वाक्यान्तरेषु च। भाष्यादौ सम्प्रदाये चोपायत्वं ब्रह्मणि स्थितम्'
इति।
पुरुषकारिकनिष्ठास्तु हरिस्वातन्तर्यमैक्ष्य च ।
कृपाप्रचुरमाचार्य मत््वोपायमवस्थिताः ।।१४६ ।।
द्विविधास्ते निजाचार्यकृपामात्रप्रपन्नकाः ।
तथा सेवातिरेकप्रपत्रश्चेति सदा सताम् ।।१४७।।

yathoktaṃ 'samastapuruṣārthānāṃ sādhakasya dayānidheḥ। śrīmataḥ pūrvasiddhatvātt siddhopāyamimaṃ
viduḥ | prapataterlakṣaṇe mante vidhī vākyāntareṣu ca। bhāṣyādau sampradāye copāyatvaṃ brahmaṇi sthitam'
iti।
puruṣakārikaniṣṭhāstu harisvātantaryamaikṣya ca ।
kṛpāpracuramācārya matvopāyamavasthitāḥ ।।146।।
dvividhaste nijācāryakṛpāmātraprapannakāḥ ।
tathā sevātirekaprapatraśceti sadā satām ।।147।।

yathoktaṃ (yathā + uktaṃ) - yathā (adv.: as stated) + uktaṃ (pp. of vad; verbal adjective) - indeclinable  
'samastapuruṣārthānāṃ (samasta + puruṣārtha + -āṇām) - samasta (adj., mfn, gen.pl: entire, all) + puruṣārtha (comp.: puruṣa + artha / puruṣa-artha; mfn) + gen.pl. - of all human purposes/goals  
sādhakasya (sādhaka + -sya) - sādhaka (m.s.) - doer/one who accomplishes + gen.sg. - of the accomplisher  
dayānidheḥ (dayā + nidhaḥ) - dayā (f.) + nidhaḥ (m.) compound tatpuruṣa gen.sg. - of the treasure of compassion  
śrīmataḥ (śrīmat + -aḥ) - śrīmat (adj., mfn. nom.sg.) - glorious, venerable  
pūrvasiddhatvāt (pūrva + siddhatva + āt) - pūrva (adj. mfn. nom.sg.), siddhatva (n., nom.sg.) + āt (ablative suffix) - from prior accomplishment  
siddhopāyam (siddha + upāya + m.) - siddha (adj.), upāya (m.) compound bahuvrīhi or tatpuruṣa accusative sg. - the accomplished means/way, acc.sg.  
imam (idam) - pron.dem. acc.sg. n. - this  
viduḥ (√vid, 3pl perfect) - they knew  
prapataterlakṣaṇe (papatati + lakṣaṇa, loc.sg.) - prapatati (verb) negative imperative/participle phrase; likely prapatater-lakṣaṇe loc. - in the characteristic of falling?

vidhī (vidhī) - nom.sg. m. - the method, rule/procedure  
vākyāntareṣu (vākyāntara + -eṣu) - vākyāntara (comp.: vākyā + antara, n.) loc.pl. - in the alternate sentences  
ca - indeclinable - and  
bhāṣyādau (bhāṣya + ādau) - bhāṣya (n.), ādau (loc.sg.) - in the beginning of the commentary

copāyatvaṃ (ca + upāyatvaṃ) - ca (and) + upāyatva (n.) acc.sg. - the means/tool/agency, with 'ca', acc.sg.  
brahmaṇi (brahma + -i) - brahma (n.) loc.sg. - in Brahman  
sthitam (sthita, pp of sthā) - sthitam (pp.), n., nom.sg./n, neuter - established  
iti (thus) - indeclinable  

puruṣakārikaniṣṭhāstu (puruṣa + kārika + niṣṭhā + tu) - puruṣa (m.), kārika (f.), niṣṭhā (f.), tu (indeclinable) - but firmly devoted to the teachings of the Purusha (term in vedantic texts referring to the cosmic person or reality)  
harisvātantaryamaikṣya (hari + svātantrya + ekṣya) - hari (m.), svātantrya (independence, m.), ekṣya (seeing/considering) - having seen the independence of Hari (Vishnu)

sadā (always) - indeclinable

Literal translation (with grammatical notes):

As stated by the treasure of compassion of the accomplisher of all human purposes, from the venerable's prior accomplishment, they understood this accomplished means. (Perfect verb viduḥ: they knew.) He thinks in the distinctive marks (lakṣaṇe) and considers the method (vidhī) in alternate sentences, and also in the beginning of the commentary and in the tradition that the means is established in Brahman.  
But those firmly devoted to the Purusha teachings, having looked upon Hari's independence, and established in the means thought by the compassionate teacher, are two kinds, having surrendered only to the compassion of their own teacher. Thus, exceeding service and prapatra (?), thus are always of the virtuous.

Smooth English translation:

As declared by the compassionate accomplisher of all human goals, from the venerable one's prior success, they understood this perfected method. He contemplates the distinctive features and considers the method in various statements, both in the commentary's beginning and in the tradition, confirming that the means is established in Brahman.  
However, those firmly devoted to the teachings of Purusha, recognizing Hari's independence, and abiding in the means taught by the compassionate teacher, are of two types: those who surrender solely to their own teacher’s compassion. Thus, by constant dedicated service and submission, they remain ever among the virtuous.
