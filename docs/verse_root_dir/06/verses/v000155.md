## Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
=== मूलपाठः ===
```

---

## Transliteration (IAST)

```text
भागवताः केवलाश्च कादाचित्का अपि द्विधा ।
तत्र भागवता बोध्या ये तेषु भगवत्पराः ।।१५५।।
```

---

## Literal Translation

भागवताः (bhāgavatāḥ) - भागवत (bhāgavata), masculine, nominative plural  
केवलाः (kevalāḥ) - केवल (kevala), masculine, nominative plural  
च (ca) - indeclinable, conjunction 'and'  
कादाचित्का (kādācitkā) - कदाचित् (kadācit, 'sometimes/at some time') with emphatic क (ka), indeclinable, adverb  
अपि (api) - indeclinable, particle 'also/even'  
द्विधा (dvidhā) - द्विधा (dvidhā), feminine, nominative singular (adverbial usage)

---

## Smooth Translation

तत्र (tatra) - indeclinable, adverb 'there'  
भागवता (bhāgavatā) - भागवत (bhāgavata), neuter plural instrumental (here instrumentive plural)  
बोध्या (bodhyā) - बोध्य (bodhya), feminine nominative plural (gerundive/participle meaning 'to be known')  
ये (ye) - relative pronoun masculine/feminine/neuter plural nominative 'those who'  
तेषु (teṣu) - तत् (tat), neuter locative plural 'in those'  
भगवत्पराः (bhagavatparāḥ) - भगवत्पर (bhagavat-para), masculine nominative plural, a tatpuruṣa compound (bhagavat + para)
