## Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
=== मूलपाठः ===
```

---

## Transliteration (IAST)

```text
अथाऽन्त्यो सहमानस्तत्क्षणमेव तु संसृत्तिम् ।  
तथेव भगवत्प्राप्तौ सत्वरस्वान्त उच्यते ।।१५०।।
```

---

## Literal Translation

अथ (atha), अथ्, indeclinable (conjunction, "then, now")  
अन्त्यः (antyaḥ), अन्त्यः, masculine singular nominative (noun, "end")  
सहमानः (sahamānaḥ), सह + मान: (पठ् धातु प्रत्ययः), present active participle masculine singular nominative (participle, "struggling, engaged with")  
तत्क्षणम् (tatkṣaṇam), तत् + क्षणम्, neuter singular accusative (compound: tatpuruṣa; noun, "that moment")  
एव (eva), एव्, indeclinable (emphatic particle, "indeed")  
तु (tu), तु, indeclinable (conjunction, "but")  
संसृत्तिम् (saṃsṛttim), संसृत्ति, feminine singular accusative (noun, "withdrawal, detachment")

---

## Smooth Translation

तथेव (tatheva), तत् + एव, indeclinable compound (emphatic, "just so")  
भगवत्प्राप्तौ (bhagavatprāptau), भगवत् + प्राप्तौ, masculine dual locative (compound: tatpuruṣa; noun + noun, "arrival at (the) divine")  
सत्वरः (satvaraḥ), सत्वर, masculine singular nominative (adjective, "quick, soon")  
स्वान्त (svānta), स्व + अन्तः, masculine singular nominative (compound: bahuvrīhi; noun + noun used adjectivally, "one's own end")  
उच्यते (ucyate), प्रयुज्य धातु, present passive indicative 3rd singular (verb, "is said")
