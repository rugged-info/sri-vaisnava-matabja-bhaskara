## Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
=== मूलपाठः ===
```

---

## Transliteration (IAST)

```text
सीतापतेः संशृणुयात्रिरन्तरं कथां च गायेत्सुयशोऽङ्कितां मुहुः ।
रूपं तदीयं च चराचरात्मकं पश्यन् सतां सङ्गमुदारधीश्चरेत् ।।१६१।।
```

---

## Literal Translation

1) सीतापतेः (sītāpateḥ), सीतापतिः, genitive singular, tatpuruṣa compound  
2) संशृणुयात्रि (saṃśṛṇuyātri), saṃśṛṇu + āyātri (imperative + agent noun), bahuvrīhi  
3) अन्तरम् (antaraṃ), antar, accusative singular neuter, noun  
4) कथां (kathāṃ), kathā, accusative singular feminine, noun  
5) च (ca), indeclinable, conjunction  
6) गायेत् (gāyet), √gā (to sing), verb, optative, 3rd person singular active  
7) सुरयशः (suyaśaḥ), suyaśa, vocative singular masculine, noun  
8) अंकिताम् (aṅkitām), aṅkita, accusative singular feminine, adjective  
9) मुहुः (muhuḥ), indeclinable, adverb (repeatedly)  
10) रूपं (rūpaṃ), rūpa, accusative singular neuter, noun  
11) तदीयं (tadīyaṃ), tadīya (tad + īya), accusative singular neuter, adjective (possessive: "his/her/its")  
12) च (ca), indeclinable, conjunction  
13) चराचरात्मकं (carācarātmakaṃ), carācar + ātmaka, accusative singular neuter, compound adjective  
14) पश्यन् (paśyan), √paś (to see), present participle, nominative singular masculine active  
15) सताम् (satām), sat, genitive plural, noun  
16) सङ्गम् (saṅgam), saṅgama, accusative singular masculine, noun  
17) उदारधीशे (udāradhīśe), udāra-dhīśa (noble lord), locative singular masculine, compound noun  
18) चरेत् (caret), √car (to move/live), optative, 3rd person singular active

---

## Smooth Translation

Literal translation:  
"Of Sītāpati (Rāma), (he) who hears repeatedly the journey, (and) also the inner story may sing, that marked with good fame repeatedly;  
(He) seeing the form his, both movable and immovable in nature, (of) the good ones' union in the noble lord (Dwelling), may move/live."
