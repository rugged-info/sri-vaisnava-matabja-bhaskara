## Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
=== मूलपाठः ===
```

---

## Transliteration (IAST)

```text
भागवताः केवलाश्च कादाचित्का अपि द्विधा ।
तत्र भागवता बोध्या ये तेषु भगवत्पराः ।।१५५।।
```

## Smooth Translation

Literal translation (with grammatical notes):  
"The devotees (bhāgavatāḥ) alone (kevalāḥ) at some time indeed (kādācitkā api) in two ways (dvidhā).  
There (tatra) the devotees (bhāgavatā, instrumental plural) to be known (bodhyā, feminine plural nominative) who (ye, masculine plural nominative) among them (teṣu, locative plural) are devoted to Bhagavat (bhagavatparāḥ, masculine plural nominative)."
