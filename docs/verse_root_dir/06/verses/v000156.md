# Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
SOURCE_PAGES: page_0221.txt

=== मूलपाठः ===


भागवताः केवलाश्च कादाचित्का अपि द्विधा ।
तत्र भागवता बोध्या ये तेषु भगवत्पराः ।।१५५।।

bhāgavatāḥ kevalāśca kādācitkā api dvidhā |
tatra bhāgavatā bodhyā ye teṣu bhagavatparāḥ ||155||

भागवताः (bhāgavatāḥ), भागवत (noun, masculine, plural, nominative),  
केवलाः (kevalāḥ), केवल (adjective, masculine/feminine/neuter plural, nominative),  
अपि (api), अपि (indeclinable, particle),  
कादाचित् (kādācit), कादाचित् (indeclinable, adverb),  
का (kā), का (pronoun, feminine singular nominative),  
अपि (api), अपि (indeclinable, particle),  
द्विधा (dvidhā), द्विधा (adjective/indeclinable, feminine singular nominative),  
तत्र (tatra), तत्र (indeclinable, adverb),  
भागवता (bhāgavatā), भागवत (noun, masculine/feminine plural instrumental),  
बोध्या (bodhyā), बोध्य (participle adjective, feminine plural nominative),  
ये (ye), य (pronoun, masculine plural nominative),  
तेषु (teṣu), तत् (pronoun, masculine/neuter plural locative),  
भगवत्पराः (bhagavatparāḥ), भगवत्पर (compound tatpuruṣa: भगवान् + पर, masculine plural nominative)  

Literal translation (with grammatical notes):  
"The devotees (bhāgavatāḥ) alone (kevalāḥ) at some time indeed (kādācitkā api) in two ways (dvidhā).  
There (tatra) the devotees (bhāgavatā, instrumental plural) to be known (bodhyā, feminine plural nominative) who (ye, masculine plural nominative) among them (teṣu, locative plural) are devoted to Bhagavat (bhagavatparāḥ, masculine plural nominative)."

"The devotees alone at some time indeed (are) twofold.  
There (are) the devotees (to be) known who among them are devoted to Bhagavat."

---

भगवद्धोग्यभूत्यादिसाक्षात्कारसुखाश्रयाः ।
श्रीराममानसा नित्यं तदनुध्यानतत्पराः ।।१५६।।

bhagavaddhogyabhūtyādisākṣātkārasukhāśrayāḥ |
śrīrāmamānasa nātyaṃ tadanudhyānatataparáḥ ||156||

भगवद्धोग्यभूत्यादिसाक्षात्कारसुखाश्रयाः (bhagavaddhogyabhūtyādisākṣātkārasukhāśrayāḥ),  
भगवत् (bhagavat, noun root), धोग्य (dhogya, verbal adjective 'to be endured'),  
भूति (bhūti, noun), आदि (ādi, indeclinable 'etc.'),  
साक्षात्कार (sākṣātkāra, noun), सुख (sukha, noun), आश्रय (āśraya, noun) - compound word (bahuvrīhi), masculine plural nominative  

श्रीराममानसा (śrīrāmamānasa), श्रीराम (proper noun) + मानस (mānasa, noun 'mind'), instrumental singular,  
नित्यं (nityaṃ), नित्य (indeclinable/adverb 'always'),  
तद् (tad), तत् (pronoun neuter singular accusative),  
अनुध्यानत (anudhyānata), अनुध्यान (verbal adjective present participle 'meditating'), masculine singular accusative,  
तत्पराः (tatparāḥ), तत्पर (adjective 'devoted'), masculine plural nominative  

Literal translation (with grammatical notes):  
"Those devoted to Bhagavat, endurance, prosperity, etc., being direct realization, being refuge of happiness (masculine plural nominative).  
By mind devoted to Śrī Rāma always (adverb), that (accusative singular neuter) meditating and devoted (masculine plural nominative)."

"Those who are devoted to Bhagavat, to endurance, prosperity, and direct realization, refuge of happiness;  
always meditating on that with mind devoted to Śrī Rāma, are devoted."

---

The devotees alone are at times indeed twofold;  
there are those devotees to be known who among them are devoted to Bhagavat.  

Those devoted to Bhagavat, endurance, prosperity, and direct realization, refuge of happiness;  
with mind fixed always on Śrī Rāma, are devoted accordingly.
```
