## Transliteration (IAST)

```text
भागवताः केवलाश्च कादाचित्का अपि द्विधा ।
तत्र भागवता बोध्या ये तेषु भगवत्पराः ।।१५५।।
भगवद्धोग्यभूत्यादिसाक्षात्कारसुखाश्रयाः ।
श्रीराममानसा नित्यं तदनुध्यानतत्पराः ।।१५६।।
केचिद् गुणानुसन्धानपराः कैडकर्यतत्पराः ।
इत्थं महर्षिभिः प्रोक्ता द्विविधा भगवत्पराः ।।१५७।।
```

## Literal Translation

भागवताः (bhāgavatāḥ), भागवत (bhāgavata), nominative plural masculine;
केवलाः (kevalāḥ), केवल (kevala), nominative plural masculine;
अच (ca), indeclinable conjunction ‘and’;
कादाचित्का (kādācitkā), कदाचित् (kādācit), indeclinable adverb ‘sometimes’ + कारक suffix -का used here as an emphatic particle;
अपि (api), indeclinable particle ‘also’;
द्विधा (dvidhā), द्वि (dvi) +धा (dhā), nominative feminine singular, adverbial use ‘in two ways’;
तत्र (tatra), indeclinable adverb ‘there’;
भागवता (bhāgavatā), भगवतः (bhagavataḥ), instrumental singular masculine ‘by the devoted’;
बोध्या (bodhyā), बोध्य (bodhya), nominative feminine singular gerundive or verbal adjective ‘to be known’;
ये (ye), relative pronoun nominative plural masculine ‘who’;
तेषु (teṣu), तेष (teṣa), locative plural masculine ‘among them’;
भगवत्पराः (bhagavatparāḥ), भगवतः + पर (para), nominative plural masculine compound tatpuruṣa ‘devoted to the Lord’;
भगवद्धोग्यभूत्यादि-साक्षात्कार-सुख-आश्रयाः (bhagavad-dhogya-bhūty-ādi-sākṣātkāra-sukhāśrayāḥ), compound tatpuruṣa and bahuvrīhi (long compound), nominative plural masculine, words meaning ‘relating to enjoyment, well-being etc. of the Lord, direct realization, pleasure as refuge’;
श्रीराममानस-नित्यं (śrīrāmamānasa-nityaṁ), compound word (tatpuruṣa), adverbial accusative singular ‘always in the mind of Lord Śrī Rāma’;
तद्-अनुध्यान-तत्पराः (tad-anudhyāna-tatparāḥ), compound tatpuruṣa (tad + anudhyāna + tatpara), nominative plural masculine ‘devoted to meditation on that’;
केचित् (kecit), indefinite pronoun ‘some’;
गुणानुसन्धानपराः (guṇānusandhānaparāḥ), compound tatpuruṣa, nominative plural masculine ‘devoted to investigation of qualities’;
कैडकर्यतत्पराः (kaiḍakryatatparāḥ), compound tatpuruṣa, nominative plural masculine, ‘engaged exclusively in devilish deeds’;
इत्थं (itthaṁ), indeclinable adverb ‘thus’;
मಹर्षಿಭिः (maharṣibhiḥ), महर्षि (maharṣi), instrumental plural masculine ‘by the great sages’;
प्रोक्ता (proktā), perfect passive participle nominative plural masculine, ‘declared’;
द्विविधा (dvidhā), feminine singular nominative adverb ‘twofold’;
भगवत्पराः (bhagavatparāḥ), nominative plural masculine ‘those devoted to the Lord’.

---

## Smooth Translation

Lord’s devotees and the merely devoted are also, sometimes, twofold.
There, the devoted to the Lord (bhāgavatā) are to be known:
those among them who are devoted to the Lord (bhagavatparāḥ),
linked with enjoyment, well-being, etc. of the Lord, direct realization and refuge in pleasure;
those always devoted to meditation on that (the Lord Śrī Rāma) in their mind;
some are devoted to the investigation of qualities,
others are devoted exclusively to devilish deeds.
Thus, the great sages have declared,
there are two kinds of devotees to the Lord.
