# Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
SOURCE_PAGES: page_0215.txt

=== मूलपाठः ===


विहाय चान्यत्परमं दयानिधिं प्राप्यं समर्थं निरपायमीश्वरम् ।
उपायमेतेऽध्वसीय सुस्थिता ज्ञेयाः प्रपन्नाः सततं हरिप्रिया: ।।१४५।।

vihāya cānyatparamaṁ dayānidhiṁ prāpyaṁ samarthaṁ nirapāyamīśvaram ।
upāyamete'dhvasīya susthitā jñeyāḥ prapannāḥ satataṁ haripriyāḥ ।।145।।

विहाय (vihāya), verb-root + क्त्वा (gerund), from verb विह (viha), karmapravṛtti gerund ("having forsaken")
चान्यत्परमं (cānyatparamaṁ), c + anyat + paramam, compound (tatpuruṣa?); c = indeclinable enclitic, anyat (anyat, adj., acc.sg.n.), paramam (parama, adj., acc.sg.n.)
दयानिधिं (dayānidhiṁ), dayā (dayā, noun, f.), nidhí (nidhi, noun, m. = treasure), compound (bahuvrīhi or tatpuruṣa), here accusative singular feminine (due to compounding)
प्राप्यं (prāpyaṁ), verb-root + क्त्वा (gerund), from verb प्राप् (prāpt), karmapravṛtti gerund ("having obtained")
समर्थं (samarthaṁ), samartha (adj., acc.sg.m.)
निरपायमीश्वरम् (nirapāyamīśvaram), compound: निर (nir, prefix "without") + अपाय (apāya, noun, m.) + ईश्वर (īśvara, noun, m.), acc.sg.m.
उपायमेतेऽध्वसीय (upāyamete'dhvasīya), upāya (upāya, noun, m., nom.pl.), ete (ete, pronoun, nom.pl.m.), अध्वसीय (adhvasīya), participle, causative, pres.act, plural nominative masculine
सुस्थिता (susthitā), participle, perfect passive or present active participle, fem.sg.nom, root स्था (sthā)
ज्ञेयाः (jñeyāḥ), verbal adjective or gerundive, must-be-known, nom.pl.m.
प्रपन्नाः (prapannāḥ), prapanna (participle, perfect passive, nom.pl.m.), "surrendered"
सततं (satataṁ), adj., acc.sg.m.
हरिप्रिया: (haripriyāḥ), compound: हरि (hari, noun, m.) + प्रिया (priyā, adj.), nom.pl.m.

(Literal translation: "Having forsaken the highest other (goal), having obtained the treasure of compassion, capable, without danger Lord; these means on the path to be followed, well-established, to be known, surrendered, always dear to Hari.")

Smooth English translation: "Having abandoned all other supreme goals, having attained the treasure of compassion, the capable and faultless Lord; these means must be followed, firmly established, known by the surrendered ones, always dear to Hari."
```
