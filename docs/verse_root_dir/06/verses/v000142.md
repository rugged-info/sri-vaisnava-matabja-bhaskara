# Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
SOURCE_PAGES: page_0211.txt

=== मूलपाठः ===


अनादिकर्मोत्करजातनानादेहाभिमानी सुमतोऽथ बद्धः ।
स चाऽच्युताहेतुकृपाबलेनाविद्याक्रियावासरुचिप्रवृत्तेः ।।१४१॥

anādikarmotkarajātanānādehābhimānī sumatoऽtha baddhaḥ ।
sa cāऽcyutāhetukṛpābalenāvidyākriyāvāsarucipravṛtteḥ ॥141॥

anādi-karma-utkara-jāta-nānā-adeha-abhimānī (anādi: beginningless; karma: action; utkara: special, prominent; jāta: born; nānā: various; adeha: without body; abhimānī: proud, conceited) - tatpuruṣa compound  
sumataḥ (sumat, masculine singular nominative; good-minded, wise)  
atha (indeclinable; then)  
baddhaḥ (baddha, masculine singular nominative; bound)  
saḥ (saḥ, masculine singular nominative; he)  
ca (indecinable; and)  
acyutāhetukṛpābalena (acyuta-hetu-kṛpā-bala-ena: 'by the power of Acyuta's cause and mercy'; acyuta: proper noun, genitive singular; hetu: cause, instrumental singular; kṛpā: mercy, instrumental singular; bala: strength, instrumental singular; ena: instrumental suffix joining) - bahuvrīhi compound  
avidyākriyāvāsarucipravṛtteḥ (avidyā-kriyā-vāsaru-cipravṛtteḥ: 'from the activities of ignorance, ardor, desire, and volition'; avidyā: ignorance, instrumental singular; kriyā: action, instrumental singular; vāsaru: ardor, instrumental singular; cī (ci with sandhi) + pravṛtteḥ (pravṛtti, instrumental singular)) - tatpuruṣa compound

Beginningless, special actions have arisen; (he is) the conceited one without a body, the wise one, now bound.  
He (saḥ), by the power of Acyuta’s cause, mercy, and strength, from the activities of ignorance, passion, desire, and volition.

The wise and proud one without a beginning and body, engaged in many special actions, is now bound.  
He, by Acyuta’s cause, mercy, and strength, is bound due to ignorance, passion, desire, and volition.

विमोक्तुमिच्छुस्तु मुमुक्षरुक्तः सम्बन्धतः प्राज्ञसुसम्मतो यम् ।
तथैव सांसारिकभीगमिच्छुर्बुभुक्षरन्यः कथितो बुधेनदरैः ।।१४२।।

vimoktumicchustu mumukṣaruktaḥ sambandhataḥ prājñasusammato yam ।
tathaiva sānsārikabhīgamicchurbubhukṣarnyaḥ kathito budh(en)adaraiḥ ॥142॥

vimoktum icchuḥ tu (vimoktuṃ: to be liberated, infinitive; icchuḥ: desires, masculine singular nominative from verb; tu: but)  
mumukṣa-ruktaḥ (mumukṣa-ruktaḥ: addressed to a mumukṣu (seeker of liberation), masculine singular vocative or nominative)  
sambandhataḥ (sambandha + taḥ, from sambandha, instrumental singular: 'from connection')  
prājña-su-sammataḥ (prājña + su + sammataḥ: prājña (wise person), su (good), sammataḥ (accepted); masculine singular nominative)  
yam (yam: relative pronoun; masculine singular accusative: whom)  
tatha eva (indeclinable: likewise indeed)  
sānsārika-bhīga-micchuḥ (sānsārika: worldly; bhīga: soaked, afflicted; micchuḥ (icchuḥ with prefix): wishing; masculine singular nominative)  
bubhukṣa-rnyaḥ (bubhukṣa + arnyaḥ, probably a typo in sandhi, possibly bubhukṣa-rnyaḥ = bubhukṣa-rnyaḥ = bubhukṣar ṇyaḥ; interpreting as bubhukṣa-rnyaḥ, bubhukṣa meaning eager or desiring; or bubhukṣa-rnyaḥ as a compound - more context needed)  
kathitaḥ (kathita, masculine singular nominative: spoken, told)  
buddhena adaraiḥ (buddhena: by the wise; adaraiḥ: by elders; instrumental plural)

He who is sent as a mumukṣa wishing liberation, accepted by the wise from connection,  
likewise the worldly one wishing to be soaked (immersed), the eager for liberation, is spoken of by the wise elders.

The one who, as a seeker of liberation, desires liberation and is approved by the wise due to connection,  
similarly, the worldly aspirant longing to be fully immersed is described by the wise elders.

(Note on bubhukṣarnyaḥ: Possibly “bubhukṣa-arnyaḥ,” a compound or typo; interpreted here as “bubhukṣa-rnyaḥ” = ‘eager for liberation’, aligning with context.)
```
