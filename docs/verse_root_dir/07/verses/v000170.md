## Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
=== मूलपाठः ===
```

---

## Transliteration (IAST)

```text
दिव्येषु देशेषु सतां प्रसङ्गं तदीयकैङ्कर्यपरायणः सन् ।
यावच्छरीरान्तमहर्दिवं तत्कथामुदारां शृणुयाद्धवघ्रीम् ।।१७०।।
```

---

## Literal Translation

दिव्येषु (divyeṣu), दिव्य (divya, adjective), locative plural neuter  
देशेषु (deṣeṣu), देश (deśa, masculine), locative plural  
सतां (satāṃ), सत् (sat, adjective/noun 'good ones'), genitive plural masculine  
प्रसङ्गं (prasaṅgaṃ), प्रसङ्ग (prasaṅga, masculine), accusative singular  
तदीयकैङ्कर्यपरायणः (tadīyakaiṅkaryaparāyaṇaḥ), तत् (tat, pronoun ‘that’) + ईय (īya, adjective-forming suffix), कैङ्कर्य (kaiṅkarya, noun ‘service, homage’) + परायण (parāyaṇa, adjective ‘devoted to’), nominative singular masculine compound (tatpuruṣa)  
सन् (san), सन् (asat-sampratyaya participle ‘being’), nominative singular masculine participle  
यावत् (yāvat), indeclinable, conjunction ‘as long as, until’  
शरीरान्त (śarīrānta), शरीर (śarīra, neuter) + अन्त (anta, masculine noun ‘end’), compound (tatpuruṣa), nominative singular neuter  
महन् (mahan), adjective ‘great, mighty’, vocative singular neuter (or nominative singular neuter as part of compound)  
दिवं (divaṃ), दिवस (divasa, masculine) shortened to दिव (diva, neuter or masculine) ‘day, sky’, accusative singular neuter  
तत् (tat), तद् (tat, pronoun ‘that’), accusative singular neuter  
कथा (kathā), कथा (kathā, feminine) nominative singular ‘story, speech’  
उदारां (udārāṃ), उदार (udāra, adjective ‘noble, generous’), accusative singular feminine  
शृणुयात् (śṛṇuyāt), शृणु (śṛṇu), verb root ‘hear!’, optative mood, 3rd person singular active  
धवघ्रीम् (dhavaghrīm), धवघ्री (dhavaghrī, feminine), accusative singular feminine ‘female deer’

---

## Smooth Translation

Divine lands in which good people’s connection, being devoted to their service, (exists).  
As long as the great day comes to an end (i.e., until the end of the great day), may the noble story be heard of the female deer.
