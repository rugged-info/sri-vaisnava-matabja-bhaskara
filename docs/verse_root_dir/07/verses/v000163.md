## Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
=== मूलपाठः ===
```

---

## Transliteration (IAST)

```text
पञ्चायुधाङ्कङ्कितवेष्णवा ये विप्रा अथ क्षत्रियवैश्यशूद्राः ।
स्तियस्तथाऽन्येऽपि च विष्णुरूपा जगत्पवित्रप्रपवित्रिणस्ते ।।१६३।।
```

---

## Literal Translation

पञ्चायुधाङ्कङ्कितवेष्णवा (pañcāyudhāṅkaṅkitaveṣṇavā): pañca (five, numeral); āyudha (āyudha, neuter, nominative plural, 'weapons'); aṅkaṅkita (aṅkaṅkita, past passive participle, nominative plural, 'marked'); veṣṇava (veṣṇava, masculine/neuter, nominative plural, 'adorned, wearing'); compound: tatpuruṣa (pañcāyudha + aṅkaṅkita + veṣṇava)
ये (ye): relative pronoun, nominative plural, masculine/feminine; base form: yaḥ
विप्रा (viprā): noun, masculine, nominative plural; base form: vipra (Brahmins)
अथ (atha): indeclinable, conjunctive particle, 'then, moreover'
क्षत्रियवैश्यशूद्राः (kṣatriyavaśyaśūdrāḥ): compound (bahuvrīhi/tatpuruṣa) consisting of kṣatriya (masc. nom. pl.), vaiśya (masc. nom. pl.), śūdra (masc. nom. pl.), together denoting the three varnas

---

## Smooth Translation

स्तियः (stiyaḥ): probably a misprint or variant, likely स्त्रियः (striyaḥ), feminine nominative plural, 'women', base form स्त्री (strī)
तथा (tathā): indeclinable, adverb/conjunction 'also, likewise'
अन्ये (anye): adjective/pronoun, masculine nominative plural, 'others'
अपि (api): indeclinable particle, 'also, even'
च (ca): indeclinable conjunction, 'and'
विष्णुरूपा (viṣṇurūpā): compound adjective, feminine nominative plural; viṣṇu (proper noun), rūpā (form/appearance)
जगत्पवित्रप्रपवित्रिणस्ते (jagatpavitraprapavitriṇaste): compound of jagat (neuter nominative singular, 'world') + pavitra (adjective, neuter nom. sing., 'pure') + prapavitra (adjective, neuter nom. sing., 'impure') + ṇa (suffix) + te (third person plural pronoun, 'they'); here functioning as a complex bahuvrīhi or karmadhāraya compound denoting 'purifying and polluting the world'; or parsed as jagat-pavitra-prapavitrinaḥ + te (they)
