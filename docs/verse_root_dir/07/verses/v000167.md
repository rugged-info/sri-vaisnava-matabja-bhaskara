# Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
SOURCE_PAGES: page_0235.txt

=== मूलपाठः ===


( अधाष्मप्रश्ीत्तरे वेष्णवकालक्षेपनिरूपणम्)  
कार्यो महात्मभिर्नित्यं कालक्षेपो मुमुक्षुभिः ।  
परमात्मपरैरित्यं वैष्णवैरिति वर्ण्यते ।।१६७।।  

kāryo mahātma-bhír nityaṃ kālakṣepo mumukṣubhiḥ ।  
paramātma-parair ityam vaiṣṇavair iti varṇyate ॥167॥  

कार्यो (kāryaḥ) kārya neut. nom. sing., "to be done" (gerund as noun)  
महात्मभिः (mahātma-bhiḥ) mahātman (mahātman-), masc. instr. pl., "by great souls"  
नित्यं (nityaṃ) nitya neut. acc. sing., "always" (adverbial use)  
कालक्षेपः (kālākṣepaḥ) kālakṣepa masc. nom. sing., "time spending" or "passing of time"  
मुमुक्षुभिः (mumukṣubhiḥ) mumukṣu (mumukṣu-), masc. instr. pl., "by those desiring liberation"  
परमात्मपरैः (paramātma-paraiḥ) paramātma + para instr. pl., tatpuruṣa compound, masc. instr. pl., "by supreme souls"  
इत्यम् (ityam) iti indeclinable, "thus"  
वैष्णवैः (vaiṣṇavaiḥ) vaiṣṇava masc. instr. pl., "by Vaiṣṇavas"  
इति (iti) indeclinable, used to quote or mark words  
वर्ण्यते (varṇyate) varṇ (to describe) 3rd person sing. indic. middle passive, "is described"  

"Work (function) (is) by great souls always, time-spending (work) by those desiring liberation (is also) by supreme souls. Thus (it) is described by Vaiṣṇavas." (Note: The phrase "kāryaḥ... kālakṣepaḥ" links the concept of "work" and "passing time".)  

"The work is always performed by great souls; the passing of time is (also) done by those desiring liberation. Thus it is described by the Vaiṣṇavas."
```
