# Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
SOURCE_PAGES: page_0233.txt

=== मूलपाठः ===


तदर्धनात्तत्पदनीरपानात्तत्सङ्गतेस्तत्प्रणतेर्विधानात् ।
नृणां हि तच्छिष्टसुभीजनाच्च स्यात्कोटिजन्मार्जितपापनाशः ।।१६५।।

tadar dhanāt tat padanīr apānāt tatsaṅgates tat praṇater vidhānāt।
nṛṇāṁ hi tac chiṣṭa-subhījanācca syāt koṭi janmārjita-pāpanāśaḥ।।165।।

tad (pronoun, neuter nominative/accusative singular, "that")
ardhanāt (ardhanāt, noun, ablative singular, "half-wealth" or "partial wealth")
tat (pronoun, neuter nominative/accusative singular, "that")
pada-nīr-apānāt (compound tatpuruṣa: pada + nīr + apānāt; nouns, ablative singular — pada (foot/step), nīr (water), apānā (drink); together "from the drinking of foot-water")
tat (pronoun, neuter nominative/accusative singular, "that")
saṅgateḥ (saṅgataḥ, noun, ablative singular, "from association/meeting")
tat (pronoun, neuter nominative/accusative singular, "that")
praṇater (praṇataḥ, noun, genitive singular, "of the bowing/bowing down")
vidhānāt (vidhānāt, noun, ablative singular, "from the procedure/institution/method")

nṛṇām (nṛṇām, noun, genitive plural, "of men/people")
hi (indeclinable, emphatic particle, "indeed")
tat (pronoun, neuter nominative/accusative singular, "that")
chiṣṭa-subhījanāḥ (compound tatpuruṣa: chiṣṭa + subhījana + āḥ; adjective plural masculine nominative, "remaining food and drinks")
ca (indeclinable, conjunction, "and")
syāt (verb, 3rd person singular, optative/conditional of √as "to be", "may be")
koṭi (noun, nominative singular, "crore/ten million")
janma-ārjita-pāpa-nāśaḥ (compound tatpuruṣa: janma + ārjita + pāpa + nāśaḥ; noun masculine nominative singular, "destroyer of sins earned by birth")

From that half-wealth, from that drinking of foot-water, from that association, from that bowing procedure (ablative instrumental cause),
indeed of men (genitive), that remaining pure food and drink (nominative plural) and,
may there be the destroyer of sins earned over crore (ten million) births.

From that partial wealth, from that drinking of water at the feet, from that association, from that act of obeisance,
indeed for humans, that leftover pure food and drink will become the destroyer of sins accumulated over millions of births.
```
