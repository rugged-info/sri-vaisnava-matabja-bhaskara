# Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
SOURCE_PAGES: page_0238.txt

=== मूलपाठः ===


स्रानादिकर्माणि विधाय नित्यं ग्रन्थानमून् संशृणुयादशक्तः।
श्रीरामसन्नामसुकीर्तनञ्च द्रयानुसन्धानमथो विदध्यात् ।।१६९।। 

srānādikarmāṇi vidhāya nityaṃ granthānamūn saṃśṛṇuyādaśaktaḥ।
śrīrāmasannāmasukīrtanañca drayānusandhānamatho vidadhyāt।।169।। 

स्रानादि (srānādi), स्रानादि (srāna + ādi), tatpuruṣa compound (with ādi meaning "etc.")
कर्माणि (karmāṇi), कर्मन् (karma), accusative plural neuter
विधाय (vidhāya), विधातुम् (vidhā) verbal gerund (absolutive)
नित्यम् (nityam), नित्य (nitya), adverb
ग्रन्थान् (granthān), ग्रन्थ (grantha), accusative plural masculine
ऊन् (ūn), ऊन (ūna), accusative plural masculine (nominal adjective)
संशृणुयात् (saṃśṛṇuyāt), संशृणु (saṃśṛṇu), optative singular active
अशक्तः (aśaktaḥ), अशक्त (aśakta), nominative singular masculine adjective
श्रीरामसन्नामसुकीर्तनम् (śrīrāmasannāmasukīrtanam), श्रीराम (śrīrāma) + सन्नाम (sannāma) + सुकीर्तन (sukīrtana), bahuvrīhi compound accusative singular neuter
च (ca), indeclinable conjunction "and"
द्रय (draya), द्रय (draya), accusative singular neuter
अनुसन्धानम् (anusandhānam), अनुसंधान (anusandhāna), accusative singular masculine
अथ (atha), indeclinable conjunction "then"
अथो (atho), indeclinable adverb "then"
विदध्यात् (vidadhyāt), विध (vidha) verbal optative singular active

Having performed the actions (karmāṇi) starting with bathing etc. (srānādi), always (nityam), the unbroken (ūn) sacred texts (granthān); let the incapable (aśaktaḥ) listen (saṃśṛṇuyāt).
And the sacred name (śrīrāmasannāmasukīrtanam) of Lord Śrī Rāma, along with the flowing (draya) investigation (anusandhānam), then (atho) let him firmly undertake (vidadhyāt).

Having always performed the preliminary rites such as bathing and so forth, let the incapable listen to the unbroken scriptures.
And let him then sincerely engage in the chanting of the holy names of Lord Śrī Rāma along with the practice of continuous meditation.
```
