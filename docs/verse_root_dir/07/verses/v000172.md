=== मूलपाठः ===


ईश्वरस्याप्यभिमानद्रेषित्वादैन्यप्रियत्वाच्च' (२७) इत्युक्तम्। गुरूपदिष्टान् मन्ताञ्जपन्तो वासं
कुर्युरित्यत्र गुरूपदिष्टानिति परम्पराप्राप्ततया तेषामेवोष्टसाधकत्वात्। मन्त्रान्
श्रीरामतारकमन्तराजादीन्, द्रयादिसहिताभिप्रायेण बहुवचनम्। एेकान्तिकेन मन्त्ानुष्ठानेनापि
कालक्षेपविधेः सम्पूर्तिर्भवति, मन्वरजपस्य स्वाध्यायरूपत्वादिति भावः।
भक्त्यादियुक्तस्य तथाऽनहङ्कृतेर्महात्मनस्तस्य निदेशपालनम्।
उपायमेनं चरमं निरन्तरं सुवैष्णवो यं विदधात्वतद्धितः ।।१७२।।

īśvarasyāpyabhimānadreṣitvādainyapriyatvācca' (27) ityuktam। gurūpadiṣṭān mantāñjapanto vāsaṃ
kuryurityatra gurūpadiṣṭāniti paramparāprāptatayā teṣāmevoṣṭasādhakatvāt। mantrān
śrārāmatārakamantarājādīn, drayādisahitābhiprāyeṇa bahuvacanam। ekaantikena mantānuṣṭhānenāpi
kālākṣepavidheḥ sampūrtirbhavati, manvarjapasya svādhyāyarūptvāditi bhāvaḥ।
bhaktyādiyuktasya tathā'nahaṅkṛtermahātmanastasya nideśapālanam।
upāyamenaṃ caramaṃ nirantaraṃ suvaiṣṇavo yaṃ vidadhātvatadhhitaḥ ।।172।।

Literal translation (with grammatical notes):

"Because even of God (īśvarasya apy), due to the pride's cruelty (abhimānadreṣitvā) and due to being disliked by others (ainyapriyatvāt ca), it is said (iti uktam '27'). Those who chant (japantaḥ) the mantras (mantān) prescribed by the guru (gurūpadiṣṭān), perform dwell (vāsaṃ kuryuḥ) — thus, there where those prescribed by the guru (gurūpadiṣṭāni) [are], according to traditional succession (paramparā prāptatayā), by their lip only (teṣām eva oṣṭa) the accomplishment (sādhakatvāt) arises (sādhakatvam, 'capacity for accomplishment'). The mantras (mantrān), such as Śrī Rāma, Tāraka, and Antarāja (śrīrāmatārakamantarājādīn), with Draya and others, accompanied by their intention (sahitābhiprāyeṇa), are in plural (bahuvacanam). Even by exclusive performance (ekāntikena mantānuṣṭhānena api), the completion (sampūrtir) of the time-wasting method (kālākṣepavidheḥ) occurs (bhavati). The meaning (bhāvaḥ) is that (iti) the form of self-study (svādhyāyarūptvāt) of japa (manvarjapasya) is known (manva-ṛjapasya), with devotion and others joined (bhaktyādiyuktasya) and likewise of the ego-less great soul (anahaṅkṛter mahātmanas) his instruction-following (nideśapālanam). By this method (upāyam enaṃ), the ultimate (caramaṃ), continuous (nirantaram) good Vaiṣṇava (suvaiṣṇavo) performs that appointed (yam vidadhāt) ."

Smooth English translation:

"It is said (verse 27) that even God, due to arrogance and being disliked by others, does not intervene. Those who chant the mantras prescribed by the guru reside there; by traditional succession, the mere utterance of their lips leads to accomplishment. The mantras, such as Śrī Rāma, Tāraka, Antarāja, and others with Draya, accompanied by sincere intention, are in the plural. Even exclusive performance of these mantras leads to the completion of the discipline of time utilization. This signifies that japa in the form of self-study, when accompanied by devotion and similarly the instruction-following of the ego-free great soul, is effective. By this method, the devoted and constant Vaiṣṇava continually carries out that appointed instruction to the utmost."
