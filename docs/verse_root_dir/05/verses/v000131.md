## Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
=== मूलपाठः ===
```

---

## Transliteration (IAST)

```text
देशादौ हि प्रशस्ते स वर्तमानश्चतुर्विधः ।।१३१।
```

---

## Literal Translation

देशादौ (deśādau), देश (deśa), locative dual; हि (hi), particle; प्रशस्ते (praśaste), प्रशस्त (praśasta), nominative plural masculine; स (sa), pronoun, nominative singular masculine; वर्तमानः (vartamānaḥ), present participle, nominative singular masculine; च (ca), indeclinable conjunction; चतुर्विधः (caturvidhaḥ), compound (tatpuruṣa), nominative singular masculine

---

## Smooth Translation

In/from two places (locative dual) indeed (particle), excellent (nominative plural masculine); he (nominative singular masculine) is present (participle masculine singular) and (conjunction) fourfold (nominative singular masculine)
