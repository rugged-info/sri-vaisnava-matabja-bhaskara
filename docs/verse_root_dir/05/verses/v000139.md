## Transliteration (IAST)

```text
प्राप्तुं परां सिद्धिमकिञ्चनो जनो द्विजातिरिच्छञ्छरणं हरि व्रजेत् ।
परं दयालु स्वगुणानपेक्षितक्रियाकलापादिकजातिबन्धनम् ।।१३९।।
```

जनो (jano), जन (jana), noun, nominative singular masculine  
द्विजातिरिच्छञ्छरणं (dvajātiricchañchharaṇaṁ), द्विजाति (dvijāti) + इच्छ (iccha) + शरणं (śaraṇaṁ) compound (bahuvrīhi), accusative singular neuter

स्वगुणानपेक्षितक्रियाकलापादिकजातिबन्धनम् (svaguṇānapekṣitakriyākalāpādikajātabandhanam), compound of स्वगुणान् (svaguṇān, svaguṇa + accusative plural neuter), अपेक्षित (apekṣita, past passive participle), क्रियाकलापा( kriyākalāpā) + आदि (ādi, suffix meaning 'and others') + जाति (jāti) + बन्धनम् (bandhanam), noun, accusative singular neuter

---

## Smooth Translation

"प्राप्तुं परां सिद्धिमकिञ्चनो जनो द्विजातिरिच्छञ्छरणं हरि व्रजेत् ।"  
To obtain (infinitive) the supreme perfection (acc. fem. sing.) any person (nom. sing. masc.) of the twice-born caste (acc. neut. sing. bahuvrīhi compound) wishes (optative subjunctive, 3rd pers. sing.) to go to Hari (vocative).
