## Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
=== मूलपाठः ===
```

---

## Transliteration (IAST)

```text
बाह च पादौ च प्रसार्य साञ्जलिः स्तवैः स्तुवन् यश्च नमेद्रघूत्तमम् ।
शतैः क्रतूनां तु सुदुर्लभां गतिं स चाप्रुयाद्रामपरायणो जनः ।।१३७।।
```

---

## Smooth Translation

With arms and feet stretched forth, (showing) reverence, praising with hymns he who also worships Nemedra, the best.  
(Although hundreds of sacrifices) are indeed very hard to attain (the) rare path, this person devoted to Rāma should not attain it.
