## Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
=== मूलपाठः ===
```

---

## Transliteration (IAST)

```text
आवाहनासनाभ्यां च पादाद्याचमनैस्तथा  
स्रानवस्त्ोपवीतैश्च गन्धपुष्पसुधूपकैः ।।१३२।।
```

---

## Literal Translation

आवाहनासनाभ्यां (āvāhanāsanābhyāṁ), आवाहनासन (āvāhanāsana), ā (prefix), vāhana (noun compound "vāhana"='carrying', here 'āhavana'='invocation' or 'calling'), āsana (noun 'seat, position'), (instr. dual)  
च (ca), indeclinable, = and  
पादाद्याचमनैः (pādādyācamanaistathā), पादादि (pādādi), पाद (pāda, m. noun 'foot'), आदि (ādi, indeclinable 'etc.'), आचमन (ācamana, m. noun 'sipping water, ritual sipping'), (instr. pl.)  
तथा (tathā), indeclinable 'thus, also'

---

## Smooth Translation

स्रानवस्त् (srānavast), स्रानवस् (srānavas), sānava (noun?), here treated as indeclinable or to be parsed as a compound member  
उपवीतैः (upavītaish), उपवीत (upavīta), m. noun 'sacred thread', (instr. pl.)  
च (ca), indeclinable = and  
गन्धपुष्पसुधूपकैः (gandhapuṣpasudhūpakaiḥ), gandha (m. noun 'smell, fragrance'), puṣpa (n. noun 'flower'), sudhūpa (m. noun 'pleasant smoke, incense'), compound tatpuruṣa bahuvrīhi compound—(instr. pl.) 'with fragrant flower and incense smoke'
