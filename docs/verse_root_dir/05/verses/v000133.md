## Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
=== मूलपाठः ===
```

---

## Transliteration (IAST)

```text
आवाहनासनाभ्यां च पादाद्याचमनैस्तथा  
स्रानवस्त्ोपवीतैश्च गन्धपुष्पसुधूपकैः ।।१३२।।  
दीपनैवेद्यताम्बूलप्रदक्षिणविसर्जनैः  
षोडशा्चप्रिकारिस्तमेतैरर्चेत्सदा सुधीः ।।१३३ ।।
```

---

## Literal Translation

आवाहनासनाभ्यां (āvāhanāsanābhyām) - आहवनासन (āvāhanāsana), ā- (prefix), ablative/instrumental dual;  
च (ca) - indeclinable conjunction "and";  
पादाद्याचमनैः (pādādyācamanaaiḥ) - पादादि (pādādi), ablative/instrumental plural; compound of पाद (pāda, "foot") + आदि (ādi, "etc.");  
तथा (tathā) - indeclinable adverb "thus";  
स्रानवस्त् (srānavast) - sraṇavas (sraṇa + vas), nominative singular masculine;  
उपवीतैः (upavītaḥ) - उपवीत (upavīta), instrumental plural masculine; sacred thread;  
च (ca) - indeclinable conjunction "and";  
गन्धपुष्पसुधूपकैः (gandhapuṣpasudhūpakaiḥ) - compound bahuvrīhi of गन्ध (gandha, "fragrance/smell") + पुष्प (puṣpa, "flower") + सुधू (sudhū, "incense") + पक (paka, "smoke"), instrumental plural masculine.

---

## Smooth Translation

दीपनैः (dīpanaiḥ) - दीपन (dīpana), instrumental plural masculine; "lights";  
एव (eva) - indeclinable particle "indeed";  
अेद्यताम्बूलप्रदक्षिणविसर्जनैः (edyatāmbūlapradakṣiṇavisarjanaiḥ) - compound tatpuruṣa of अेद्यताम्बूल (edyatāmbūla, edible berries or similar) + प्रदक्षिण (pradakṣiṇa, "circumambulation") + विसर्जन (visarjana, "offering/releasing"), instrumental plural masculine;  
षोडशाः (ṣoḍaśāḥ) - ordinal numeral nominative plural feminine, "sixteen";  
च (ca) - indeclinable conjunction "and";  
अप्रीकारः (aprikāraḥ) - appears to be अप्रीकार (aprikāra), nominative plural masculine; "offerings/arrangements" (contextually);  
तमेतैः (tametaiḥ) - demonstrative pronoun instrumental plural masculine "with these";  
अर्चेत् (arcet) - verb root अर्च् (arc), optative singular, "should worship";  
सदा (sadā) - indeclinable adverb "always";  
सुधीः (sudhīḥ) - nominative plural masculine "wise persons".
