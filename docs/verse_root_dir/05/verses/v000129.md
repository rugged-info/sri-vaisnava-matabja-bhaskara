=== मूलपाठः ===

---

## Transliteration (IAST)

```text
१६२ श्रीवैष्णवमतान्नभास्करः  
समर्य्यं कर्माणि शुभानि वैष्णवो रामाय भक्ष्यं च निवेद्य भक्षयेत् ।  
अहर्दिवं वीतभयं समुत्तमं विमुक्तधीः स्वाघनिवृत्तिकामनः ।।१२९।।
```

---

## Literal Translation

१६२ (saṅkhyā, indeclinable, numeral)  
श्रीवैष्णवमतान्नभास्करः (śrīvaiṣṇavamatānnabhāskaraḥ), śrīvaiṣṇava- + matā- + anna- + bhāskaraḥ, compound (tatpuruṣa), masculine nominative singular  
समर्य्यं (samaryyaṃ), samaryya (verbal adjective), indeclinable verbal adjective (participle, present active participle, accusative singular neuter)  
कर्माणि (karmāṇi), karma (कर्म), noun, neuter nominative/accusative plural  
शुभानि (śubhāni), śubha (शुभ), adjective, neuter nominative/accusative plural  
वैष्णवः (vaiṣṇavaḥ), vaiṣṇava (वैष्णव), adjective or noun, masculine nominative singular  
रामाय (rāmāya), Rāma (राम), noun, masculine dative singular  
भक्ष्यं (bhakṣyaṃ), bhakṣya (भक्ष्य), adjective/participle, neuter nominative/accusative singular  
च (ca), indeclinable conjunction  
निवेद्य (nivedya), nivedya (verbal root), gerund/participial form (present active participle, indeclinable)  
भक्षयेत् (bhakṣayet), verb root √bhakṣ (to eat), verb, optative mood, 3rd person singular active  
अहः (ahaḥ), noun, masculine nominative singular  
दिवं (divaṃ), noun, neuter accusative singular  
वीतभयं (vītabhayaṃ), vīta (past participle of √vī, "to go away") + bhaya (fear), adjective, neuter nominative/accusative singular  
समुत्तमं (samuttamaṃ), samuttama (superlative adjective), neuter nominative/accusative singular  
विमुक्तधीः (vimuktadhīḥ), vimukta (past participle, "liberated") + dhī (feminine or masculine noun "intellect"), masculine nominative singular  
स्वाघनिवृत्तिकामनः (svāghanivṛttikāmanah), compound: svāghani + vṛtti + kāmanah (desiring self-house-return), tatpuruṣa compound, masculine nominative singular

## Smooth Translation

Literal translation (grammatical notes in parentheses):  
162 śrīvaiṣṇava- + mated grain (bhāskaraḥ) (subject)  
After cleansing (verbal adjective, accusative neuter singular) the actions (nominative plural) auspicious (nominative plural), the Vaiṣṇava (subject, nominative singular) to Rāma (dative singular) edible food and offering giving (gerund + conjunction), he should cause eating (optative, 3rd person singular).  
Day and night (accusative singular) fearless (accusative singular) the highest liberated intellect (nominative singular) desiring self-home-return (nominative singular).
