# Verse — मूलपाठः

```text
SOURCE_PAGES: page_0203.txt

=== मूलपाठः ===


मनोमिलिन्दस्तव पादपङ्कजे रमार्चिते संरमतां भवे भवे ।
यशः श्रुतौ ते मम कर्णयुग्मकं त्वद्धक्तसङ्गोऽस्तु सदा मम प्रभो ।। १३५।। 

manomilindastava pādapañkaje ramārcite saṃramatāṃ bhave bhave ।
yaśaḥ śrutau te mama karṇayugmakaṃ tvaddhaktasaṅgo'stu sadā mama prabho ।। 135 ।।

मनोमिलिन् (manomilin), मनोमिलिन् (compound, bahuvrīhi)
स्तव (stava), स्तव (indecinable particle)
तव (tava), त्व (pronoun, 2nd person singular, possessive)
पादपङ्कजे (pādapaṅkaje), पदपङ्कज (pādapaṅkaja, masculine, locative singular)
रामर्चिते (ramārcite), रामर्चित (ramārcita, masculine/neuter, locative singular, past participle)
संरमतां (saṃramatāṃ), संरमता (saṃramatā, feminine, accusative singular, abstract noun)
भवे (bhave), भव (bhava), masculine/neuter, locative singular, vocative singular (indeclinable particle in repetition)
भवे (bhave), same as above, repetition for emphasis
यशः (yaśaḥ), यशस् (yaśas), masculine, nominative singular
श्रुतौ (śrutau), श्रुत (śruta), masculine/neuter, dual, locative
ते (te), pronoun, 2nd person singular, possessive
मम (mama), pronoun, 1st person singular, possessive
कर्णयुग्मकम् (karṇayugmakam), कर्णयुग्म (karṇayugma), neuter, accusative singular, compound (tatpuruṣa - "ear-pair")
त्वद्धक्तसङ्गः (tvaddhaktasaṅgaḥ), तव् (tva), 2nd person singular possessive + अधक्त (adhakta, indeclinable, "unattached") + सङ्ग (saṅga), masculine, nominative singular, compound (bahuvrīhi)
अस्तु (astu), indeclinable, imperative mood, 3rd person singular, "may it be"
सदा (sadā), indeclinable adverb "always"
मम (mama), pronoun, 1st person singular, possessive
प्रभो (prabho), प्रभु (prabhu), masculine vocative singular

"manomilin tava pādapañkaje ramārcite saṃramatāṃ bhave bhave"
- मनोमिलिन् (manomilin): "mind-joining" (a bahuvrīhi compound describing the subject, possibly the speaker or an addressed person)
- तव (tava): your (2nd person singular possessive)
- पादपङ्कजे (pādapañkaje): in the lotus of the feet (locative singular)
- रामर्चिते (ramārcite): worshipped by Rāma (locative singular past participle)
- संरमतां (saṃramatāṃ): desire, longing, supplication (accusative singular)
- भवे भवे (bhave bhave): O existence, O being (vocative repeated for emphasis)

"yaśaḥ śrutau te mama karṇayugmakam tvaddhaktasaṅgo'stu sadā mama prabho"
- यशः (yaśaḥ): fame (nominative singular)
- श्रुतौ (śrutau): in the heard scriptures (locative dual)
- ते (te): your (2nd person singular possessive)
- मम (mama): my (1st person singular possessive)
- कर्णयुग्मकम् (karṇayugmakam): pair of ears (accusative singular)
- त्वद्धक्तसङ्गः (tvaddhaktasaṅgaḥ): with no association, detached from you (nominative singular bahuvrīhi compound)
- अस्तु (astu): let it be (imperative, 3rd singular)
- सदा (sadā): always
- मम (mama): my (1st person singular possessive)
- प्रभो (prabho): O Lord (vocative singular)

"O mind-joining one, in your lotus feet, worshipped by Rama, may there be desire (or pleasure, devotion) in every existence.
May the fame about you, in the scriptures heard, my pair of ears, may my detachment (from you) never be—always, O my Lord."

O mind-joining one, in your lotus feet, worshipped by Rama, may devotion arise in every existence.  
May your fame in the scriptures reach my ears; may I never be separated from you, always, my Lord.
```
