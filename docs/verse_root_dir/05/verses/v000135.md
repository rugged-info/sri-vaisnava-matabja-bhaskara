## Transliteration (IAST)

```text
मनोमिलिन्दस्तव पादपङ्कजे रमार्चिते संरमतां भवे भवे ।
यशः श्रुतौ ते मम कर्णयुग्मकं त्वद्धक्तसङ्गोऽस्तु सदा मम प्रभो ।। १३५।।
```

रामर्चिते (ramārcite), रामर्चित (ramārcita, masculine/neuter, locative singular, past participle)

## Smooth Translation

"manomilin tava pādapañkaje ramārcite saṃramatāṃ bhave bhave"
- मनोमिलिन् (manomilin): "mind-joining" (a bahuvrīhi compound describing the subject, possibly the speaker or an addressed person)
- तव (tava): your (2nd person singular possessive)
- पादपङ्कजे (pādapañkaje): in the lotus of the feet (locative singular)
- रामर्चिते (ramārcite): worshipped by Rāma (locative singular past participle)
- संरमतां (saṃramatāṃ): desire, longing, supplication (accusative singular)
- भवे भवे (bhave bhave): O existence, O being (vocative repeated for emphasis)
