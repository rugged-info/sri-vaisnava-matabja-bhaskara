=== मूलपाठः ===


श्रीमन्तं श्रुतिवेद्यमद्श्ुतगुणग्रामाग्यरलाकरं  
प्रयः स्वेक्षणसंसुलज्ितमहीजाताक्षिकोणेक्षितम् ।  
भक्तारोषमनोभिवाज्छितचतुर्व्गप्रदं स्वर्द्धमं  
रामं स्मेरमुखाम्बुजं शुचिमहानीलाश्मकान्तिं भजे ।।१।।

śrīmantaṃ śrutivedyamadśutaguṇagrāmāgyaralākaraṃ  
prayaḥ svekṣaṇasaṃsulajitamahījātākṣikoṇekṣitam ।  
bhaktāroṣamanobhibhājchitacaturvagapradaṃ svarddhamaṃ  
rāmaṃ smeramukhāmbujaṃ śucimahānīlāśmakāntiṃ bhaje ।।1।।

ś्रीमन्तं (śrīmantaṃ), śrīmanta, adj. acc. sg. m. (wealthy, glorious)  
श्रुतिवेद्यम् (śrutivedyam), śruti-vedya (compound: tatpuruṣa), neut. acc. sg. (to be known by hearing/scripture)  
अद्श्ुतगुणग्रामाऽग्यारलाकरं (adśutaguṇagrāmāgyaralākaraṃ), (compound compound; needs segmentation):  
अद्श्ुतगुण (adśutaguṇa) - (likely a compound or misspelling, see note)  
ग्राम (grāma), neut. m. (village)  
आज्ञ (ājña), fem. (command)  
रालाकरं (rālakaraṃ), (likely a compound or misreading)  
(Note: This word seems corrupted or incorrectly joined; will treat as a compound denoting something like "bearing important features")  
प्रयः (prayaḥ), neut. nom. sg. (effort, going)  
स्वेक्षण (svekṣaṇa), neut. nom. sg. (self-observation)  
संसुलजितम् (saṃsulajitam), neut. nom. sg. (to untangle, compound verb participle)  
महिजात (mahījāta), adj. perf. nom. sg. m. (born of earth)  
आक्षि (ākṣi), fem. acc. pl. (eyes)  
कोण (koṇa), masc. acc. sg. (angle)  
एक्षितम् (ekṣitam), neut. nom. sg. (looked upon, seen)  
भक्तारोष (bhaktāroṣa), masc. nom. pl. (devotes, worshippers)  
मनो (mano), neut. gen. sg. (of mind)  
भिवाज्छित (bhibhājchit), part. perf. (divided, manifest)  
चतुर्वग (caturvaga), adj. nom. sg. m. (four-legged)  
प्रदं (pradaṃ), neut. nom. sg. (giver)  
स्वर्धमं (svarddhamaṃ), neut. nom. sg. (self-increasing, flourishing)  
रामं (rāmaṃ), masc. acc. sg. (Rama)  
स्मेरमुख (smeramukha), masc. acc. sg. (smiling face)  
अम्बुजं (ambujaṃ), masc. acc. sg. (lotus - literally 'born of water')  
शुचिमहानीला (śucimahānīlā), adj. acc. sg. m. (pure, great blue)  
अश्मकांतिं (aśmakāntiṃ), fem. acc. sg. (light, splendor of gemstone)  
भजे (bhaje), verb, pres. ind. 1st or 3rd sg. (worship)

Literal translation:  
"Śrīmantaṃ" (Acc. sg. m., glorious one), "śrutivedyam" (Acc. sg. n., something known from sacred texts/hearing), "adśutaguṇagrāmāgyaralākaraṃ" (Acc. sg. m., bearing the marks of pure qualities and command of villages),  
"prayaḥ" (Nom. sg. n., effort), "svekṣaṇasaṃsulajitam" (Nom. sg. n., by self-observation untangled), "mahījātākṣikoṇekṣitam" (Nom. sg. n., viewed at the angle of earth-born eyes).  
"Bhaktāroṣamanobhibhājchitacaturvagapradaṃ" (Nom. sg. m., the giver of four-legged animals divided among devotees' minds), "svarddhamaṃ" (Nom. sg. n., self-flourishing),  
"Rāmaṃ" (Acc. sg. m., Rama), "smeramukhāmbujaṃ" (Acc. sg. m., lotus-faced smiling), "śucimahānīlāśmakāntiṃ" (Acc. sg. f., shining with pure great blue gemstone-radiance), "bhaje" (I worship).

Smooth English translation:  
I worship the glorious Rama, known through sacred scriptures, bearing pure qualities like a powerful lord of villages;  
who is the effort uncovered by self-inspection, seen through the glance of earth-born eyes;  
the generous giver to devotees who cherish him in their hearts;  
the flourishing one with a smiling lotus-like face, shining with the pure, radiant blue luster of a great gem.
