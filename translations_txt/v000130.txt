=== मूलपाठः ===


१६२ श्रीवैष्णवमतान्नभास्करः  
समर्य्यं कर्माणि शुभानि वैष्णवो रामाय भक्ष्यं च निवेद्य भक्षयेत् ।  
अहर्दिवं वीतभयं समुत्तमं विमुक्तधीः स्वाघनिवृत्तिकामनः ।।१२९।।  
* संवित्करः *  
पूर्णतः पापनिवृत्ति एवं मोक्ष की कामना रखने वाले वेष्णवोँ को चाहिए कि अपने सभी शुभ  
कर्मो को प्रभु श्रीरामजी के श्रीचरणों में समर्पित करता जाए तथा भगवान् श्रीराम को नैवेद्य  
अर्पित करके भगवटप्रसाद ही ग्रहण करे।  
* पदपरामर्शः *  
"राज्यं चाहं च रामस्य' (वा.रा. २.८२.१६) इति श्रीभरतोक्तत्यात्मा भवत्वात्मीयं वा सर्वमपि  
भगवते समर्पणीयमिति समर्पणप्रधानेऽस्मिन् वेष्णवधर्मे तदर्पणबुद्धया क्रियमाणकर्मणामपि  
न बन्धनकारित्वं "तत्कुरुष्व मदर्पणम् (९.२७) इत्युक्रम्य "मोक्ष्यसे कर्मबन्धनैः' (९.२८) इति  
गीतोक्तेः, अत इद्थमर्पणमेव भक्तानां तव्यसादान्द्धीतिनिवर्तकं सिद्धयतीति; तच्छिक्षयति  
समप्येति। कर्मफलाद्विमुक्ताय विमुक्तये धीर्यस्य, यद्रा विमुक्ता असङ्कुचिता  
धीर्यस्योदारमनाः, स्वस्याधानां निवृत्तौ कामना समीहा यस्य, निजपापविनाशनेच्छुर्वेष्णवो  
विष्णुदेवकार्य भक्तः शुभानि कर्माणि रामाय भगवते समर्प्य समुत्तममुत्तमोत्तमं भक्ष्यं भोज्यं  
तस्यै भगवते अहर्दिवं प्रत्यहं निवेद्य समर्प्य वीतभयं निश्चिन्तं भक्षयेद् भुञ्जीत । वीतभयमिति  
क्रियाविशेषणम्। अहनि च दिवा चेति द्रन्द्रे चतुरेति निपातनादहर्दिवं प्रत्थमित्सर्थः।  
णुभकर्मसमर्पणं नाम भगवत् एव कर्माराध्यत्वं कर्तृत्वं भीक्तृत्वञ्चेति बुद्धयार्पणम्, एतेन  
स्वकर्तृत्व-समर्पणादात्मसमर्पणमुक्तम्, उत्तमवस्तुसमर्पणेन ममतात्याग आत्मीय-  
समर्पणच्चोक्तं भवति। भगवत्प्रसादग्रहणात्सतत्वशुद्धिः कर्मसमर्पणाच्च तत्फलभीतिनिवृति-  
भवतीति भावः। उक्तञ्च "आहारशुद्धौ सत्त्वशुद्धिः" इति। भगवद््रसादसेविनां  
मायाविजयोऽञ्जसा भागवते वर्ण्यते 'त्वयोपभुक्तस्त्रगगन्धवासोऽ लङ्कार चर्चिताः।  
उच्छिष्टभीजिनो  
दासास्तव मायां जयेमहि (११.६.४६) इति।  
अर्चावतारोऽपि च देशकालप्रकर्षहीनः श्रितसम्मतश्च ।  
सहिष्णुरप्राकृतदेहयुक्तः पूर्णोऽर्चकाधीनसमात्मकृत्यः ।।१३०।।

162 śrivaishṇvamatānnabhaaskaraḥ  
samaryyaṃ karmāṇi śubhāni vaiṣṇavo rāmāya bhakṣyaṃ ca nivedya bhakṣayet।  
ahardivaṃ vītabhayaṃ samuttamaṃ vimuktadhīḥ svāghanivṛttikāmanaḥ।।129।।  
* saṃvitkaraḥ *  
pūrṇataḥ pāpanivṛtti evaṃ mokṣa kī kāmanā rakhane vāle veiṣṇavoṃ ko chāhiye ki apane sabhī śubha  
karmo ko prabhu śrīrāmajī ke śrīcaraṇoṃ meṃ samarpit kartā jāe tathā bhagavān śrīrāma ko naivedya  
arpit karke bhagavaṭprasāda hī grahaṇa kare।  
* padaparāmarśaḥ *  
"rājyaṃ cāhaṃ ca rāmasya' (vā.rā. 2.82.16) iti śrībharatoktatatyātmā bhavatvātmīyaṃ vā sarvamapi  
bhagavate samarpaṇīyamiti samarpaṇapradhāne'smin veiṣṇavadharme tadarpaṇabuddhayā kriyamāṇakarmaṇāmapi  
na bandhanakāritvaṃ "tatkuruṣva madarpaṇam (9.27) ityukramya "mokṣyase karmabandhanaiḥ' (9.28) iti  
gītokteḥ, ata idthamarpaṇameva bhaktānāṃ tvayasādānddhītinivartakaṃ siddhayatīti; tacchikṣayati  
samapyeti। karmaphalādivimuktāya vimuktaye dhīryasya, yadrā vimuktā asaṅkucitā  
dhīryasyodāramanāḥ, svasyādhānāṃ nivṛttau kāmanā samīhā yasya, nijapāpavināśanecchurveiṣṇavo  
viṣṇudevakārya bhaktaḥ śubhāni karmāṇi rāmāya bhagavate samarṇya samuttamamuttamottamaṃ bhakṣyaṃ bhojyaṃ  
tasyai bhagavate ahardivaṃ pratyahaṃ nivedya samarṇya vītabhayaṃ niścintaṃ bhakṣayed bhujñīta। vītabhayamiti  
kriyāviśeṣaṇam। ahani ca divā ceti drandre catureti nipātanādahardivaṃ prathamitṣarthaḥ।  
ṇubhakarmasamarpaṇaṃ nāma bhagavat eva karmārādhyatvaṃ kartṛtvaṃ bhīktrtvaṃceti buddhayārpaṇam, etena  
svakartṛtva-samarpaṇādātmasamarpaṇamuktam, uttamavastusamarpanena mamatātyāga ātmīya-  
samarpaṇaccoktaṃ bhavati। bhagavatprasādagrahaṇātsatatvaśuddhiḥ karmasamarpanācca tatphalabhītinivṛti-  
bhavatīti bhāvaḥ। uktañca "āhāraśuddhau sattvaśuddhiḥ" iti। bhagavadprasādasevināṃ  
māyāvijayo'ñjasā bhagavate varṇyate 'tvayopabhuktastragagandhavāso' laṅkāra carcitāḥ।  
ucchiṣṭabhījino  
dāsāstava māyāṃ jayemahi (11.6.46) iti।  
arcavātāro'pi ca deśakālaprakarṣahīnaḥ śritasammataśca ।  
sahiṣṇuraprākṛtadehayuktaḥ pūrṇo'rcakādhīnasamātmakṛtyaḥ।।130।।

१६२ (numeral) श्रीवैष्णवमतान्नभास्करः (śrivaishṇvamatānnabhaaskaraḥ)  
- श्रीवैष्णवमतान् (śrī-vaishṇava-matān): śrī (honorific) + vaiṣṇava (devotee of Vishnu) + matān (mātaḥ, plural accusative or genitive/locative plural, in compound: "views/opinions of the Vaiṣṇavas")  
- नभास्करः (nabhaaskaraḥ): noun, masculine, nominative singular, meaning "sun" or "illuminator"  

समर्य्यं (samaryyaṃ)  
- samaryya (verb root √mṛ, causative form sam-√mṛ + ḍu ending): verb in verbal noun or imperative sense, from sam (prefix) + mṛ (to rub, send), neuter accusative singular, meaning "offering/dedicate"  
कर्माणि (karmāṇi)  
- karma (noun): neuter, plural, accusative, "actions/deeds"  
शुभानि (śubhāni)  
- śubha (adj.): neuter, plural, accusative, "auspicious/good"  
वैष्णवः (vaiṣṇavaḥ)  
- vaiṣṇava (noun): masculine, nominative singular, "a devotee of Viṣṇu"  
रामाय (rāmāya)  
- rāma (noun): masculine, dative singular, "to Rama" (indirect object)  
भक्ष्यं (bhakṣyaṃ)  
- bhakṣya (adj. or gerundive): neuter, singular, accusative, "to be eaten" or "food offered"  
च (ca)  
- indeclinable, conjunction, "and"  
निवेद्य (nivedya)  
- verb, gerund or verbal noun from √vid / √ved (to offer, present), neuter singular, "offered" or "having been offered"  
भक्षयेत् (bhakṣayet)  
- verb: 3rd person singular, optative or imperative mood, active voice from √bhakṣ (to eat), "should eat/consume"  

अहर्दिवं (ahardivaṃ)  
- ahan (noun): masculine or neuter, accusative singular "day" + divam (noun neuter accusative singular "day/light"), combined as a compound indicating "daily, every day"  
वीतभयं (vītabhayaṃ)  
- vīta (past participle or perfect participle of √muc - to free), neuter accusative singular + bhaya (fear, masculine singular accusative) → vītabhaya = "fearless" (compound/gerundive)  
समुत्तमं (samuttamaṃ)  
- superlative adjective: neuter singular accusative, "the highest, excellent"  
विमुक्तधीः (vimuktadhīḥ)  
- vimukta (past participle of √muc - freed) + dhī (feminine noun, wisdom, mind), nominative singular feminine or masculine, "one whose intellect is liberated/wisdom freed"  
स्वाघनिवृत्तिकामनः (svāghanivṛttikāmanah)  
- svāgha (sva + āgha, own sins) + nivṛtti (cessation, noun feminine, nominative singular) + kāma (desire, masculine nominative singular) + naḥ (negative particle for verbal root)  
Compound meaning: "one whose desire for cessation of own sins" (or "who desires cessation from one's own sins")  

---

Literal translation:  
162 The sun of the Śrī-Vaiṣṇava doctrine (illuminator of the opinions of the devotees of Viṣṇu).  
One who offers all auspicious actions to Rāma (dative) and, having offered food (bhakṣyaṃ) as an offering (nivedya), should partake thereof.  
Daily (ahardivaṃ), fearless (vītabhayaṃ), most excellent (samuttamaṃ), wise (vimuktadhīḥ), desiring cessation from own sins,  
he (the Vaiṣṇava) should dedicate (samaryyaṃ) all auspicious deeds to Lord Śrī Rāma and feed (bhakṣayet) himself without fear.  

---

Smooth English translation:  
162 The illuminator of the Śrī-Vaiṣṇava teachings,  
One who dedicates all auspicious deeds to Rāma and, having offered the food to Him, should partake of it.  
Daily, fearless, supremely excellent, and wise, desiring freedom from his own sins,  
the Vaiṣṇava should wholeheartedly dedicate all good actions to Lord Rāma and eat without fear.
