=== मूलपाठः ===


अथ पञ्चमप्रश्रीत्तरे सर्वोत्तमधर्मनिरूपणम्  
एवं तेऽभिहितं वत्स! प्रकृष्टं मुक्तिसाधनम् |  
उत्तमं सर्वधर्माणां शृणु धर्म सनातनम् ।। १२० ॥।

atha pañcamapraśrīttare sarvottamadharmanirūpaṇam  
evaṃ te'bhihitaṃ vatsa! prakṛṣṭaṃ muktisādhanam |  
uttamaṃ sarvadharmāṇāṃ śṛṇu dharma sanātanam || 120 ||

अथ (atha), अव (ava), अव्यय (indeclinable/cunjunction)  
पञ्चमप्रश्रीत्तरे (pañcamapraśrīttare), पञ्चम (pañcama; ordinal number, masculine/neuter singular locative), प्र+श्रृ (pra+śṛ; prefix+root, compound part), इत्तर (ittra?), tatpuruṣa compound  
सर्वोत्तमधर्मनिरूपणम् (sarvottamadharmanirūpaṇam): सर्वोत्तम (sarvottama; सर्व+उत्तम, bahuvrīhi compound, neuter singular nominative), धर्म (dharma; masculine/neuter, singular, genitive?), निरूपणम् (nirūpaṇam; noun, neuter singular nominative)  
एवं (evaṃ), अव्यय (indeclinable/adverb)  
तेऽभिहितं (te'bhihitaṃ): तु (tu; indeclinable?), त्व (tvā; pronoun, dative singular?), अभिहितं (abhihitaṃ; past passive participle, neuter singular accusative)  
वत्स (vatsa), noun masculine vocative singular  
प्रकृष्टं (prakṛṣṭaṃ), adjective, neuter singular accusative  
मुक्तिसाधनम् (muktisādhanam): मोक्ष (mokṣa) + साधनम् (sādhana), compound tatpuruṣa, neuter singular accusative  
उत्तमं (uttamaṃ), adjective, neuter singular accusative  
सर्वधर्माणां (sarvadharmāṇāṃ): सर्व (sarva) + धर्म (dharma) + आणाम् (ṇāṃ) plural genitive masculine, so "of all dharmas"  
शृणु (śṛṇu), verb, imperative singular, active voice, "hear/listen"  
धर्म (dharma), masculine noun, neuter singular nominative  
सनातनम् (sanātanam), adjective, neuter singular nominative

अथ (then)  
पञ्चमप्रश्रीत्तरे (in the fifth section)  
सर्वोत्तमधर्मनिरूपणम् (the most excellent exposition of dharma)  
एवं (thus)  
तेऽभिहितं (to you, stated)  
वत्त्स! (O child!)  
प्रकृष्टं (excellent)  
मुक्तिसाधनम् (means of liberation)  
उत्तमं (best)  
सर्वधर्माणां (of all dharmas)  
शृणु (listen)  
धर्म (dharma)  
सनातनम् (everlasting)

Then, in the fifth section, the most excellent exposition of dharma has been stated to you, O child! Hear the excellent means of liberation, the best of all dharmas, the eternal dharma.
