=== मूलपाठः ===


चेतनः प्राणी तद्यतिरेकेणाचेतनो जडः, ज्ञानाश्रयचेतनरमणस्य सग्वेऽचेतनरमणं कथ युज्यते। तत्रोच्यते- मोदते भगवान् भूतेर्बालः क्रीडनकेरिव' (हरि पु.२.१०२.३७) इत्यत्रेव चेतनाचेतनोभयवाचकभ्रूतशब्दप्रयोगवदत्रापि चेतनाचेतनपर्द युक्तम्। सद्वेषदर्थे नजचेतनतिर्यग्जातीयानां खगमृगादीनां विरहलीलायां स्थावरादिभ्यः स्वसंश्लेषदानेन च रमणं युज्यते। यद्रा चेतनः स्यात्सहदय इति कोषात्ससहदयानां भक्तिद्रुतचेतसामसहदयानामचेतनानां निसर्गक्रूरमनर्सा राक्षसानामपि स्वाभिरामगुणैः रमण इति। अतो 'रामः कमलपत्राक्षः सर्वसत्वमनोहरः' इति (२.६६.८) वात्मीकीयरामायणपद्ये बहुसतत्वमनोहर इत्यनुत्क्त्वा सर्वसततवमनोहर इत्युक्तम्। इत्थं भगवतो रामस्य स्वाधीनलीलोपकरणत्वमनितरचेतनप्रर्यत्वं च रमणे लभ्यते। स सर्वविधबन्धुत्वं सर्वप्राप्यत्वमेव च । सर्वप्रापकता तेन तथा चोभयलिङ्खता ।।२२।।

cetanaḥ prāṇī tadyatirekeṇācetano jaḍaḥ, jñānāśrayacetanaramaṇasya sagve'cetunaramaṇaṃ katha yujyate। tatrocyate- modate bhagavān bhūterbālaḥ krīḍanakèriva' (hari pu.2.102.37) ityatreva cetanācetanobhayavācakabhrūtaśabdaprayogavadatrapi cetanācetanaparda yuktam। sadveṣadarthe najacetanatiryagjātīyānāṃ khagamṛgādīnāṃ virahalīlāyāṃ sthāvarādibhyḥ svasansleṣadānena ca ramaṇaṃ yujyate। yadrā cetanaḥ syātsahadaya iti koṣātsasahadayānāṃ bhaktidrutacetasāmasahadayanāmacetānāṃ nisargakrūramanarsā rākṣasānāmpi svābhi rāmag uṇaiḥ ramaṇa iti। ato 'rāmaḥ kamalapatrākṣaḥ sarvasatvam anoharaḥ' iti (2.66.8) vātamīkīyarāmāyaṇapadye bahusatvam anohara ityanutktvā sarvasatatvamanohara ityuktam। itthaṃ bhagavato rāmasya svādhīnalīlopakaraṇatvamanitaracetanaprar yatvaṃ ca ramaṇe labhyate। sa sarvavidhabandh utvaṃ sarvap rāpyatvameva ca । sarvap rāpakatā tena tathā cobhaya liṅkhatā ।।22।।

The conscious living being is different; unconscious is something else. (cetanaḥ prāṇī tadyatirekeṇā acetanaḥ jaḍaḥ)  
How can the conscious enjoyer resting upon knowledge be connected with the unconscious enjoyer? (jñānāśrayacetanaramaṇasya sagve'cetunaramaṇaṃ katha yujyate)  
Here it is said: “The Lord delights in creatures like a child playing” (tatrocyate- modate bhagavān bhūterbālaḥ krīḍanakèr iva) (Hari Purāṇa 2.102.37)  
Here too, as in the use of words indicating both conscious and unconscious, the union of conscious and unconscious is appropriate.  
For the purpose of both good and hatred, among aquatic, terrestrial, and quadruped creatures, birds and animals, in the play of separation and union from immobile beings and others, the union is suitable.  
Because if the conscious is kind-hearted, then from the treasury of the kind-hearted, devotees with quick minds and kind hearts among the unconscious also, and even among the cruel and ungraceful demons, the play with self-pleasing qualities is suitable.  
Therefore, “Rama, lotus-eyed, charming all living beings,” (2.66.8) is said in the Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa verse; having said “charming many beings,” it is also said “charming to all beings.”  
Thus, the independent play of the Lord Rama, and the leading of the non-other conscious and unconscious is found in the delight.  
He is the embodiment of all relationships and the possessor of all achievements.  
By him is all attainment, and also the sign or mark of both. (verse 22)  

The conscious living being is one; the unconscious another. How is the conscious enjoyer resting on knowledge joined with the unconscious enjoyer? Here it is said: "The Blessed Lord delights in creatures like a child at play" (Hari Purāṇa 2.102.37). In this context, even the usage of words denoting both conscious and unconscious is appropriate. For the sake of both love and hate, the play of separation among aquatic, terrestrial, and quadruped creatures, birds, animals, and others, and the union by the immobile beings and others is suitable. If the conscious is kind-hearted, then from the treasury of the kind-hearted—devotees, the quick-minded, the kind-hearted among the unconscious, even among the cruel and ungraceful demons—the delightful union with pleasing qualities is proper. Hence, "Rama, lotus-eyed, beloved of all beings" (2.66.8) is uttered in the verses of Vālmīki's Rāmāyaṇa; having declared "beloved of many beings," it is also expressed as "beloved of all beings." Thus the independent play of Lord Rama and the function of the conscious and unconscious are found in this delight. He encompasses all kinships and is indeed the possessor of all attainments. By him is all attainment and the characteristic of both. (Verse 22)
