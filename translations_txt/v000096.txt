=== मूलपाठः ===


कृष्णजन्माष्टमी सोक्ता तस्यां कृष्णव्रतोत्सवम् ।
कुर्वीति विधिसंयुक्तं चतुर्वर्गफलप्रदम् ।।१५।।

kṛṣṇajanma-āṣṭamī soktā tasyāṁ kṛṣṇavrato-tsavam |
kurvīti vidhisaṁ-yuktaṁ caturvarga-phala-pradam ||15||

कृष्णजन्माष्टमी (kṛṣṇajanma-āṣṭamī), कृष्णजन्माष्टमी, तत्संबंधी समास (tatpuruṣa - Krishna+janma+āṣṭamī)
सोक्ता (soktā), सूक्त (sūkta), स्त्रीलिङ्ग, एकवचन, कर्तृवाचक तत्पुरुष समास कर्मणि (participle passive, feminine singular, nominative)
तस्यां (tasyām), तद् (tat), संज्ञा सर्वनाम (demonstrative pronoun), स्त्रीलिङ्ग, एकवचन, सप्तमी (locative case)
कृष्णव्रतोत्सवम् (kṛṣṇavrato-tsavam), कृष्णव्रत+उत्सवम्, तत्पुरुष समास, पुल्लिङ्ग, एकवचन, कर्तृवाच्य (nominative singular)
कुर्वीति (kurvīti), कुर्व (kṛ) क्रिया धातु, लृट् लकार, मध्य पद, तीसरा पुरुष, एकवचन (present tense, indicative, middle, 3rd person singular)
विधिसंयुक्तं (vidhisaṁ-yuktaṁ), विधि+संयुक्त (tatpuruṣa समास), पुल्लिङ्ग, एकवचन, कर्मणि (accusative singular)
चतुर्वर्गफलप्रदम् (caturvarga-phala-pradam), चतुर्वर्ग+फल+प्रदम्, बहुव्रीहि समास, पुल्लिङ्ग, एकवचन, कर्मणि (accusative singular)

"कृष्णजन्माष्टमी सोक्ता तस्यां कृष्णव्रतोत्सवम्।"
Krishnajanma-āṣṭamī (Krishna birth-eighth [day], feminine nominative singular) is so-called (participle passive, feminine nominative singular) in that (locative singular) Krishna vow-festival (masculine nominative singular).

"कुर्वीति विधिसंयुक्तं चतुर्वर्गफलप्रदम्।"
He/she/it does (3rd person singular present indicative) with law combined (accusative singular) the four-class fruit bestower (accusative singular).

Krishnajanma-āṣṭamī is so-called on that [day]; the Krishna vow-festival, performed according to proper ritual, bestows the fruits of all four aims of human life.

The festival of Krishna’s birth on the eighth day (Krishnajanma-āṣṭamī), thus named, is the Krishna vow-festival. When performed with proper ritual, it grants the fruits of the four goals of human life.
