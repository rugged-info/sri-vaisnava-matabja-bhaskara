=== मूलपाठः ===


रामप्रसादहेतुर्हिं न्यासोऽयं विनिगद्यते ।

IAST: rāmaprasādakheturhiṁ nyāso'yaṁ vinigadyate ।

(rāmaprasāda-khetuḥ) (hiṁ) (nyāsaḥ) (ayam) (vinigadyate)

- rāmaprasāda (rāmaprasāda), masculine, singular, nominative, tatpuruṣa compound (rāma + prasāda)  
- hetuḥ (hetu), masculine, singular, nominative  
- hi (hi), indeclinable, particle (emphatic)  
- nyāsaḥ (nyāsa), masculine, singular, nominative  
- ayam (ayam), masculine, singular, nominative, demonstrative pronoun  
- vinigadyate (vinigadyate), verb, 3rd person, singular, present, passive voice  

Literal translation:  
"The establishment (nyāsaḥ) is indeed (hi) the cause (hetuḥ) of Rama's grace (rāmaprasāda); this (ayam) is proclaimed."

Smooth English translation:  
"This establishment is indeed the cause of Rama's grace; it is proclaimed."  

नित्यसूरैः सदाचारैर्हरिपादान्नमानसैः ।।११४।।

IAST: nityasūraiḥ sadācārairharipādānnamānasaḥ । 114 ।

(nitya-sūraiḥ) (sadācāraiḥ) (haripādān) (namānasaḥ)

- nitya (nitya), masculine/neuter, plural, instrumental  
- sūra (sūra), masculine, plural, instrumental  
- sadācāra (sadācāra), masculine, plural, instrumental  
- hari-pādaān (hari+pāda), masculine, plural, accusative, tatpuruṣa compound  
- namānasa (namānasa), masculine/neuter, plural, instrumental  

Literal translation:  
"By the eternal heroes (nityasūraiḥ), by the virtuous conduct (sadācāraiḥ), by the feet of Hari (haripādān), by respectful minds (namānasaḥ)."

Smooth English translation:  
"By the eternal heroes, the virtuous conduct, the feet of Hari, and reverent minds."

(Note: The verse number ११४ is not part of the verse's words and thus omitted in breakdown and translations.)
