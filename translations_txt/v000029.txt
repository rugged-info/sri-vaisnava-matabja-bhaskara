=== मूलपाठः ===


बीजेनैवाथ जीवस्य स्वरूपं प्रतिपाद्यते ।  
रामायेति परस्यापि चतुर्थ्या तत्फलस्य च ।।२९॥।

bījenaithevaatha jīvasya svarūpaṃ pratipādyate ।  
rāmāyeti parasyāpi caturthyā tatphalasya ca ।।29॥

बीजेनैवाथ (bījenaivaatha): bīja (base: बीज bīja, noun masculine, instrumental singular), एव (eva, indeclinable particle), अथ (atha, indeclinable particle)  
जीवस्य (jīvasya): जीव (jīva, noun masculine, genitive singular)  
स्वरूपं (svarūpaṃ): स्वरूप (svarūpa, noun neuter, accusative singular)  
प्रतिपाद्यते (pratipādyate): प्रतिपाद् (pratipād, verb root), प्रतिपालयति (causative form), present middle/passive indicative, third person singular  
रामायेति (rāmāyeti): राम (rāma, noun masculine, dative singular), इति (iti, indeclinable particle, quotation marker)  
परस्यापि (parasya api): परस् (para, noun masculine, genitive singular), अपि (api, indeclinable particle)  
चतुर्थ्या (caturthyā): चतुर्थ (caturtha, adjective/fractional numeral), instrumental singular feminine  
तत्फलस्य (tatphalasya): तत् (tat, pronoun/neuter stem used here as adjective), फल (phala, noun neuter, genitive singular)  
च (ca): indeclinable conjunction

By seed alone indeed (emphatic) of the living being the true nature is expounded (middle voice "is expounded").  
"To Rama" (dative, quoted) also of the other (parasya) by the fourth (instrumental feminine, implying "fourth" case or "quarter") of that fruit also.  

By the seed alone the true nature of the living being is expounded.  
The word "Rama" is also applied to the other (being) in the fourth (case/portion) of that fruit.
