=== मूलपाठः ===


मुमुक्षवोऽपि द्विविधा महर्षिभिः प्रोक्ता अकामाः स्मृतिभक्तिशालिनः।
वेदोक्तवणश्रिमकर्मकारिणस्तूपासकादिप्रतिभेदभेदिताः ।।१४३।।

mumuṣavo'pi dvividhā maharṣibhiḥ proktā akāmāḥ smṛtibhaktiśālinaḥ।
vedoktavaṇaśrīmakarmakāriṇastūpāsakādiprathibhedabheditāḥ।।143।।

मुमुक्षवः (mumuṣavaḥ), मुमुक्ष (mūKuṣa) (noun, masculine, nominative plural) - aspirants  
अपि (api), अपि (particle/indeclinable) - also  
द्विविधा (dvividhā), द्विविध (adjective, feminine, nominative plural) - twofold, of two kinds  
मহर्षिभिः (maharṣibhiḥ), महर्षि (noun, masculine, instrumental plural) - by great sages  
प्रୋक्ताः (proktāḥ), प्रोक्त (past participle of √vac, masculine, nominative plural) - spoken, said  
अकामाः (akāmāḥ), अकाम (adjective, masculine, nominative plural) - desireless  
स्मृतिभक्तिशालिनः (smṛtibhaktiśālinaḥ), स्मृति (noun, feminine, nominative singular) + भक्ति (noun, feminine, nominative singular) + शालिन (adjective, masculine, nominative plural) compound tatpuruṣa - endowed with remembrance and devotion  

वेदोक्तवणश्रिमकर्मकारिणः (vedoktavaṇaśrīmakarmakāriṇaḥ), वेद (noun, masculine, nominative plural) + उक्त (past participle of √vac, masculine, nominative plural) + वण (noun, neuter) + श्री (noun, feminine) + कम् (root verbal noun, "karman") + कारीणः (participle, active) compound tatpuruṣa - those who perform actions indicated by the Veda, Vedic, glorious  
तूपासकादि (tūpāsakādi), तूपासक (noun, masculine, nominative plural) + आदि (indeclinable suffix) - beginning with worshippers  
प्रतिभेदभेदिताः (pratibhedabheditāḥ), प्रति (prefix) + भेद (noun, masculine) + भेदित (past participle of √bhed, masculine, nominative plural) - differentiated and classified  

Aspirants (mumuṣavaḥ), also (api), are said (proktāḥ) by the great sages (maharṣibhiḥ) to be twofold (dvividhā), desireless (akāmāḥ), endowed with remembrance and devotion (smṛtibhaktiśālinaḥ).  
Those who perform the actions mentioned in the Veda (vedoktavaṇaśrīmakarmakāriṇaḥ), beginning with worshippers and others (tūpāsakādiprathibhedabheditāḥ), are classified and differentiated.  

Even aspirants, too, are described by great sages as twofold, desireless, and endowed with devotion and remembrance.  
Those engaged in Vedic actions—beginning with worshippers—are distinctly classified and differentiated.
