=== मूलपाठः ===


श्रीकूर्माचल उत्तमे च सदयं कर्म सुरेशेडितं  
नीलाद्रौ पुरुषोत्तमं त्वथ महासिंहं च सिंहाचले ।  
श्रीमन्तं तुलसीवने तु गदिनं सर्वार्थदं तप्पिये  
क्षत्र श्रीकृतशौचके तु सततं पापापहं चेश्वरम् ।।१९६ ।।  

śrīkūrmācala uttame ca sadayaṃ karma sureṣeḍitaṃ  
nīlādrau puruṣottamaṃ tvatha mahāsiṃhaṃ ca siṃhācale ।  
śrīmantaṃ tulasīvane tu gadinaṃ sarvārthadaṃ tappiye  
kṣatra śrīkṛtaśaucake tu satataṃ pāpāpahaṃ ceśvaram ।।196।।  

श्रीकूर्माचल (śrīkūrmācala) — श्रीकूर्माचल (proper noun; nominative singular masculine; tatpuruṣa compound)  
उत्तमे (uttame) — उत्तम (adj., nominative singular masculine, positive degree)  
च (ca) — च (indeclinable; conjunction)  
सदयं (sadayaṃ) — सदय (adj., accusative singular neuter)  
कर्म (karma) — कर्म (noun neuter, accusative singular)  
सुरेशेडितं (sureṣeḍitaṃ) — सुरेश + ईडित (compound; bahuvrīhi; past passive participle, accusative singular neuter)  

नीलाद्रौ (nīlādrau) — नीलाद्र (proper noun, locative dual masculine)  
पुरुषोत्तमं (puruṣottamaṃ) — पुरुषोत्तम (proper noun, accusative singular masculine)  
त्वथ (tvatha) — तथा (indeclinable; adverb)  
महासिंहं (mahāsiṃhaṃ) — महा + सिंह (compound; tatpuruṣa; accusative singular masculine)  
च (ca) — च (indeclinable; conjunction)  
सिंहाचले (siṃhācala) — सिंहाचल (proper noun, locative singular masculine)  

श्रीमन्तं (śrīmantaṃ) — श्रीमन्त (adj., accusative singular masculine)  
तुलसीवने (tulasīvane) — तुलसीवन (proper noun, locative singular masculine)  
तु (tu) — तु (indeclinable; adversative particle)  
गदिनं (gadinaṃ) — gadi (noun masculine, accusative singular)  
सर्वार्थदं (sarvārthadaṃ) — सर्वार्थ + दन् (compound; tatpuruṣa; present participle, accusative singular neuter)  
तप्पिये (tappiye) — तप्पिय (proper noun, locative singular masculine)  

क्षत्र (kṣatra) — क्षत्र (noun neuter, vocative singular)  
श्रीकृतशौचके (śrīkṛtaśaucake) — श्रीकृत + शौचक (compound; tatpuruṣa; locative singular masculine)  
तु (tu) — तु (indeclinable; adversative particle)  
सततं (satataṃ) — सतत (adj., nominative singular neuter)  
पापापहं (pāpāpahaṃ) — पाप + अपह (compound; tatpuruṣa; present participle, nominative singular neuter)  
चेश्वरम् (ceśvaram) — च + ईश्वर (compound; bahuvrīhi; nominative singular neuter)  

In the esteemed Kūrmacala (Mount Kūrma), the best and compassionate (adj.) action made by the lord of gods,  
On the two blessed Nīlādhra mountains, the supreme person (Purushottama), and likewise the great lion on Lion Mount.  
The glorious one in Tulasīvana, and Gadi, giver of all goals, O Tappiya,  
O warrior, in the sacred Śrīkṛtaśaucaka place, always sin-destroying lord.  

O warrior, in the sacred Śrīkṛtaśaucaka place, always sin-destroying lord,   
the glorious one in Tulasīvana and Gadi, giver of all benefits;  
on the two blue mountains (Nīlādhra) the supreme person (Purushottama) and the great lion on Lion Mount;  
the best and compassionate deed made by the lord of gods in the exalted Kūrmacala (Mount Kūrma).
