=== मूलपाठः ===


मार्गे ततः सोऽर्चिरुपैति मुक्तकस्तथाऽर्चिषोऽहो दिनतः सुरार्चितः ।
आपूर्यमाणं विविधेस्तु वासरैः पक्षं प्रभूतोत्तमशर्मविज्वरः ।।१८२।।
पक्षादुदङ्मासमथी षडात्मकं तेभ्यश्च सम्वत्सरमब्दतो रविम् ।
चन्द्रं ततश्चन्द्रमसोऽथ विद्युतं स तत्र तत्राखिलदेवपूजितः ।।१८३।

mārge tataḥ so'rcirupaiti muktakastathā'rciṣo'ho dinataḥ surārcitaḥ ।
āpūryamāṇaṃ vividhes tu vāsaraiḥ pakṣaṃ prabhūtottamaśarmavijvaraḥ ।।182।।
pakṣādudaṅmāsamathī ṣaḍātmaṃ tebhyaśca samvatsaramabdato ravim ।
candraṃ tataścandramaso'tha vidyutaṃ sa tatra tatrākhiladevapūjitaḥ ।।183।

On the path then the pearl-like flame approaches; thus the flame, by day, is worshipped by the gods.  
The fortnight is filled with various days; O best warrior, it is abundant and shrouded in shadow.  
From the fortnight comes the full month, calm and sixfold; from these comes the year, from the cloud the sun.  
The moon then of the lunar month, then lightning; he is there, there worshipped by all the gods.
