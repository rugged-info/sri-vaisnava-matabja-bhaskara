=== मूलपाठः ===


१९४ श्रीवैष्णवमतान्नभास्करः  
चापादिपञ्च्यायुधचिहिताङ्गकः समीक्ष्य हृष्टश्च हरिप्रियान थ ।  
तथाविधान् भक्तिपरः प्रपूजयेत्सुवैष्णवाञ्जन्मफलादि संस्तुवन् ।।१६२।।

194 śrīvaiṣṇavamatānnabhāskaraḥ  
cāpādipañcyāyudhacihitāṅgakaḥ samīkṣya hṛṣṭaśca haripriyāna tha |  
tathāvidhān bhaktiparaḥ prapūjayetsuvaishṇavāñjanmaphalādi sanstuvan ||162||

194 (numeral) śrīvaiṣṇavamatān-nabhāskaraḥ  
śrī-vaiṣṇava-matān (compounded tatpuruṣa: śrī "auspicious, venerable" + vaiṣṇava "of Viṣṇu" + matāḥ "opinions, doctrines," plural, nominative)  
nabhās-karaḥ (compounded bahuvrīhi: nabhā "sky" + kara "maker" = sun, masculine singular nominative)  
cāpa-ādi-pañc-yāyudha-cihita-aṅgakaḥ  
cāpa (bow, masculine) + ādi (and others, indeclinable) + pañca (five, indeclinable adjective) + yāyudha (projectile weapon, neuter plural) + cihita (marked, past participle) + aṅgaka (part, member, masculine singular) (compound tatpuruṣa)  
samīkṣya (gerund of samīkṣ, to look at carefully)  
hṛṣṭaś (past passive participle, masculine singular nominative, "delighted") + ca (indeclinable conjunction "and")  
hari-priyān (hari "Vishnu", priya "dear", masculine singular accusative)  
tha (interjection particle: "indeed, certainly")  
tathā-vidhān (compound bahuvrīhi: tathā "thus" + vidhān "method, manner", masculine singular nominative)  
bhakti-paraḥ (compounded tatpuruṣa: bhakti "devotion" + paraḥ "supreme, devoted to"; masculine singular nominative)  
prapūjayet (verb, 3rd person singular optative of prapūj "to worship")  
su-vaiṣṇavān (su + vaiṣṇava + ān, vocative plural masculine)  
janma-phala-ādi (compound bahuvrīhi: janma "birth" + phala "fruit" + ādi "etc." masculine singular nominative)  
saṃ-stuvan (present participle of saṃ-stu "to praise together", masculine plural nominative)

(Here the verse number 194, followed by mention of "Śrīvaiṣṇava doctrines," metaphorically called the sun in the sky, the five types of weapons starting with bow marked in parts, after seeing which, one is delighted and praises the beloved Hari. Thus the well-arranged devotee dedicated to devotion should worship the good Vaiṣṇavas, praising them along with the fruits of birth and other benefits.)

Verse 194: The glorious sun of Śrīvaiṣṇava doctrines, adorned with five kinds of weapons starting with the bow, upon observing it, is delighted and addresses the beloved Hari. In the same way, the devotee seasoned in devotional practice should worship the noble Vaiṣṇavas, praising them along with the fruits of birth and other blessings.
