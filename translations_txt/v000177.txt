=== मूलपाठः ===


ताराकनिल-चनद्रमोबहु-महस्सौदामिनी-भासको-
जय्यो वीरसपतरशस्तनिचयैर्जता च तेषां मुहुः ।।१७७।।

tārākanila-candramobahu-mahassaudāminī-bhāskō-
jayyō vīrasapatarashastanicayairjatā ca teṣāṃ muhuḥ ।।177।।

ताराकनिल-चनद्र-ओबहु-महस्-सौदामिनी-भासकः (tārākanila-candra-obahu-mahas-saudāminī-bhāsakaḥ): 
  ताराकनिल-चनद्र- (tārākanila-candra-), tatpuruṣa compound (composed of ताराः (tārāḥ) 'stars' [pl. nom. m.] + अनिल (anila) 'wind' + चन्द्र (candra) 'moon')
  ओबहु (obahu), base 'bahu' with prefix 'o' (or sandhi form, unclear, possibly a compound or connector),
  महस् (mahas), base 'mahaḥ' (great, masculine nominative singular),
  सौदामिनी (saudāminī), base 'saudāminī' (feminine nominative singular, 'the wife of king Sudāman'),
  भासकः (bhāsakaḥ), base 'bhāsaka' (masculine nominative singular, 'shining, radiant')

जय्यः (jayyaḥ) base 'jaya' noun masc. nominative singular 'victory'

वीरसपत-रशस्-तनि-चयैः (vīrasapatarashastanicayaiḥ): 
  वीर (vīra) noun masc. nominative singular 'hero'
  सप्त (sapta) 'seven'
  रशस् (raśas) 'rays' masc. instr. plural
  तनि (tani), possibly 'body' or 'possessing' (this may be an ambiguous or compound form)
  चयैः (cayaiḥ) instr. plural of चय 'collection' or 'assemblage'

जता (jatā) past participle feminine nominative singular of √जन् (jan) 'born'

च (ca) indeclinable 'and'

तेषाम् (teṣām) pronoun genitive plural 'of them'

मुहुः (muhuḥ) indeclinable 'again and again, repeatedly'

Literal translation (grammatical notes in parentheses):

The star-wind-moon-many-great-Sudāminī-shining (bhāsakaḥ) (masc. nom. sg.)—victory (jayyah) (masc. nom. sg.)—brave-seven-rays-collection (instr. pl.) born and of them repeatedly.

The verse is complex but roughly means:

Smooth English translation:

Victorious is the radiant one, the great one shining like the stars, wind, and moon; born from the assembly of seven heroic rays, and repeatedly among them.
