=== मूलपाठः ===


भाद्रेऽसिते निशीथेऽथ रोहिण्यामष्टमीतिथी ।
सिंहमरके गते सौम्ये कृष्णो जातो विधूयते ।।९४।।

bhādre'site niśīthe'tha rohiṇyāmaṣṭamītithī |
siṁhamarake gate saumye kṛṣṇo jāto vidhūyate ||94||

भाद्रेऽसि (bhādre'si), भाद्र (bhādra), vocative singular masculine/indeclinable ('bhadre' = vocative of bhadra) + असि (asi), अस् (as), present indicative active, 2nd person singular
निशीथेऽथ (niśīthe'tha), निशीथ (niśītha), locative singular neuter + अथ (atha), indeclinable, conjunction
रोहिण्यामष्टमीतिथी (rohiṇyāmaṣṭamītithī), रोहिणी (rohiṇī), genitive singular feminine + अष्टमी (aṣṭamī), nominative singular feminine + अतिथी (atithī), nominative singular feminine; compound: bahuvrīhi (the eighth guest of Rohiṇī)
सिंहमरके (siṁhamarake), सिंह (siṁha), locative singular masculine + अरक (araka), locative singular masculine; compound: tatpuruṣa (in the battle of the lion)
गते (gate), गच्छ् (gacch), past participle locative singular masculine
सौम्ये (saumye), सौम्य (saumya), locative singular masculine
कृष्णः (kṛṣṇaḥ), कृष्ण (kṛṣṇa), nominative singular masculine
जातः (jātaḥ), जात (jāta), nominative singular masculine, past participle
विधूयते (vidhūyate), विधू (vidhū), present passive indicative 3rd person singular

"O Bhadre, as you are at night, now as the eighth guest of Rohiṇī (on the) battlefield of the lion, having gone to Saumya, Krishna, born, is being dispersed."

O Bhadre, as you are here in the night, now as the eighth guest of Rohiṇī on the lion battlefield, having gone to Saumya, Krishna, having been born, is being dispersed (passing away).

O Bhadre, now at night, as the eighth guest of Rohiṇī on the lion-like battlefield, Krishna, having been born, departs to Saumya.
