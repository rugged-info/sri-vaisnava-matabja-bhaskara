In the fourth (literal translation) and fifth (smooth translation) parts, the verse is synthesized as follows:

Literal translation:  
“149. The explanation of the means of liberation in the fourth inquiry.  
That renowned and noble proclamation: 'Caste and lineage do not bind, no one. Whom one worships is Hari, who that is.'  
Even in the vernacular language it has penetrated.  
In the atom-pervading and in the divine atoms it is said.  
By the all-knowing, realized by the great souls, beyond all space and time.” (Note: some phrases are interpretative due to the fragmentary nature)

Smooth English translation:  
“149. Explanation of the means of liberation in the fourth inquiry.  
That famous noble proclamation says: ‘Caste and lineage hold no sway; whom one worships is Hari, and that alone.’  
This truth has entered even common speech.  
It is expressed at the smallest particle and in the divine atoms.  
This profound knowledge, beyond space and time, has been revealed by the all-knowing great souls.”
