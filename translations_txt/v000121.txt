=== मूलपाठः ===


दानं तपस्तीर्थनिषेवणं जपो न चास्त्यहिंसा सदशं सुपुण्यकम् ।
हिंसामतस्तां परिवर्जयेज्ननः सुधर्मनिष्ठो टृढधर्मवृद्धये ।।१२१ ॥।

dānaṁ tapas tīrtha-niṣevaṇaṁ japo na cāstyahiṁsā sadaśaṁ supuṇyakam ।
hiṁsām atastāṁ parivarjaye jnanaḥ sudharma-niṣṭho ṭṛḍha-dharma-vṛddhaye ॥121॥

दानं (dānaṁ), दान (dāna), neuter, accusative singular;  
तपस् (tapas), tapas (base), masculine/feminine/ neuter stem, singular;  
तीर्थनिषेवणं (tīrtha-niṣevaṇaṁ), compound (tatpuruṣa) of तीर्थ (tīrtha, masculine) + निष्ठेवन (niṣevaṇa, neuter), neuter, accusative singular;  
जपः (japaḥ), japa, masculine, nominative singular;  
न (na), indeclinable, negation;  
च (ca), indeclinable, conjunction;  
अस्ति (asti), verb root अस् (as), present tense, indicative, active, 3rd person singular;  
अहिंसा (ahiṁsā), ahiṁsā, feminine, nominative singular;  
सदशं (sadaśaṁ), sadha (sad + aśa), neuter, accusative singular;  
सुपुण्यकम् (supuṇyakam), adjective, neuter, accusative singular;  
हिंसा (hiṁsā), feminine, nominative singular;  
अतः (ataḥ), indeclinable (adverb), meaning "therefore";  
ताम् (tām), pronoun, feminine, accusative singular;  
परिवर्जयेत् (parivarjayet), verb root वर्ज् (varj), optative mood, 3rd person singular, active voice;  
ज्ञानः (jñānaḥ), masculine, nominative singular;  
सुधर्मनिष्ठः (sudharma-niṣṭhaḥ), compound (tatpuruṣa) of "सु" (su) + "धर्म" (dharma) + "निष्ठ" (niṣṭha), masculine, nominative singular;  
टृढधर्मवृद्धये (ṭṛḍha-dharma-vṛddhaye), compound (bahuvrīhi or purpose), masculine, dative singular.

Literal translation:  
"Gift (dānaṁ), austerity (tapas), pilgrimage-service (tīrtha-niṣevaṇaṁ), chanting (japaḥ) are not, also there is no non-violence (ahiṁsā) equal (sadaśaṁ) (to) the very meritorious (supuṇyakam).  
Violence (hiṁsā), therefore that (tām), may be avoided (parivarjayet) by the knower (jñānaḥ), devoted to good law (sudharmanṣṭho), for the strengthening (ṭṛḍha) of dharma."

Smooth English translation:  
"Charity, penance, pilgrimage service, and chanting—none is equal to non-violence in merit.  
Therefore, one who is wise and devoted to righteous conduct should avoid violence, for the strengthening of dharma."
