=== मूलपाठः ===


यथोक्तं 'समस्तपुरुषार्थानां साधकस्य दयानिधेः। श्रीमतः पूर्वसिद्धत्वात् सिद्धोपायमिमं
विदुः | प्रपततेर्लक्षणे मन्ते विधी वाक्यान्तरेषु च। भाष्यादौ सम्प्रदाये चोपायत्वं ब्रह्मणि स्थितम्'
इति।
पुरुषकारिकनिष्ठास्तु हरिस्वातन्तर्यमैक्ष्य च ।
कृपाप्रचुरमाचार्य मत््वोपायमवस्थिताः ।।१४६ ।।

kṛpāpracuramācārya matlāpāyamavasthitāḥ ।।146।।

"As stated (yathoktaṃ) by the compassionate ocean (dayānidheḥ) who is the accomplisher (sādhaka) of all human purposes (samasta puruṣārthānām), by the revered one (śrīmataḥ), due to previous realization (pūrvasiddhatvāt), they (viduḥ) know this (imam) means of realization (siddhopāyam). On falling down (prapatati) in the sign (lakṣaṇe), the lawgiver (vidhī) thinks (mante) in the alternate sentences (vākyāntareṣu) and also at the beginning of the commentary and tradition (bhāṣyādau sampradāye), the means (upāyatvaṃ) is established (sthitam) in Brahman (brahmaṇi), thus (iti).  

But (tu) the persons devoted to the Purusha-karika (purusakarikaniṣṭhāḥ), having seen the Hari's independence (harisvātantaryam), and the greatly compassionate teacher (kṛpāpracura ācārya), are situated (avasthitāḥ) in the mind's means (mat-l̄ upāyam)."

As announced, those who are compassionate repositories of the fulfillment of all human goals, the revered ones, having previously attained realization, know this means of attainment. When a sign arises, the lawgiver considers it in alternate statements and also in the beginning of the commentary and transmission; thus the means is established in Brahman. But those devoted to the Purusha-karika, having observed Hari’s independence, and the most compassionate teacher, remain fixed in the mind’s means.
