=== मूलपाठः ===


कृतार्थयितुं जात इति भावः  
तामष्टमीवेधयुतां विहाय व्रतोत्सवं तत्र करोतु भक्तः ।  
असङ्ख्यसूर्यग्रहतोऽधिका वे या केवला सा नवमीत्युपोष्या ।।८८।।  

kṛtārthayituṃ jāta iti bhāvaḥ  
tāmaṣṭamīvedhayutāṃ vihāya vratotsavaṃ tatra karotu bhaktaḥ |  
asaṅkhyasūryagrahato'dhikā ve yā kevalā sā navamītyupoṣyā ||88||  

कृतार्थयितुं (kṛtārthayituṃ), कृतार्थयितुम् (infinitive form of the verb कृतार्थयति - causative of कृ meaning 'to make accomplished or satisfied'), कृतार्थ (verbal root), infinitive  
जात (jāta), जात (past participle of √जन् 'to be born' or 'to arise'), perfect passive participle, masculine singular nominative  
इति (iti), इति (indeclinable, particle meaning 'thus' or 'so')  
भावः (bhāvaḥ), भाव (masculine singular nominative), noun meaning 'feeling, sense, intention'  

तामष्टमीवेधयुतां (tāmaṣṭamīvedhayutāṃ), तम् (demonstrative pronoun, accusative singular feminine) + अष्टमी (aṣṭamī, feminine noun, nominative/accusative singular meaning 'eighth lunar day') + वेध (veda: 'penetrate, pierce' or 'marking' here probably part of compound) + युतां (yutāṃ, past participle, feminine singular accusative of युत - 'joined, united'), compound tatpuruṣa or bahuvrīhi (need context)  
विहाय (vihāya), verb from √वि-हृ (separating), gerund, ablative absolute participle meaning 'having abandoned'  
व्रतोत्सवं (vratotsavaṃ), व्रत (vrata), masculine noun, accusative singular + उत्सव (utsava), masculine noun, accusative singular; compound tatpuruṣa meaning 'vow festival'  
तत्र (tatra), indeclinable adverb meaning 'there'  
करोतु (karotu), कृ verb, imperative mood, 3rd person singular, active voice meaning 'let him do'  
भक्तः (bhaktaḥ), भक्त (masculine singular nominative), noun meaning 'devotee, servant'  

असङ्ख्यसूर्यग्रहतोऽधिका (asaṅkhyasūryagrahato'dhikā), असङ्ख्य (asaṅkhya), adjective, feminine singular nominative meaning 'innumerable' + सूर्यग्रहण (sūryagrahaṇa), masculine noun, singular ablative meaning 'solar eclipse' + अधिका (adhikā), feminine singular nominative adjective meaning 'more' or 'abundant' compound bahuvrīhi meaning 'more than innumerable solar eclipses'  
वे (ve), indeclinable particle, vocative or emphatic particle  
या (yā), relative pronoun, feminine singular nominative meaning 'who'  
केवला (kevalā), adjective, feminine singular nominative meaning 'alone, only, mere'  
सा (sā), pronoun, feminine singular nominative 'she, that'  
नवमीत्युपोष्या (navamītyupoṣyā), नवमी (navamī) feminine noun, nominative singular meaning 'ninth lunar day' + इति (iti) indeclinable particle + उपोष्या (upoṣyā) future participle, feminine singular nominative of उपोष् 'to fast' = 'to be fasted on the ninth'  

Literal translation:  
(The) feeling (bhāvaḥ) that (iti) the born (jāta) is to make accomplished (kṛtārthayituṃ) —  
Having abandoned (vihāya) that (tām) joined with the eighth lunar day (aṣṭamīvedhayutām) vow festival (vratotsavaṃ), there (tatra) may the devotee (bhaktaḥ) perform (karotu).  
More abundant than (adhikā) innumerable (asaṅkhya) solar eclipses (sūryagrahaṇa), who (yā) is that mere (kevalā) one (sā) who is to fast on the ninth lunar day (navamītyupoṣyā).  

Smooth English translation:  
The feeling arises that the vow festival joined with the eighth lunar day having been abandoned, the devotee should perform the vow festival there.  
That fast kept on the ninth lunar day is more meritorious than countless solar eclipses, so the one who fasts on the ninth alone is the supreme devotee.
