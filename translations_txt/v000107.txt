=== मूलपाठः ===


समुच्यते तैर्नृभिरस्वतन्तरकैः सदा सदाचारपरायणैवरैः ।।१०७।।

samucyate tainṛbhirasvatantarakaiḥ sadā sadācāraparāyaṇaivaraiḥ ।।107।।

समुच्यते (samucyate), समुच्य (samucya), verb, 3rd person singular, present indicative, passive;  
तैः (taiḥ), तैः, pronoun, ablative/instrumental masculine/neuter plural (from तत्), indeclinable;  
नृभिः (nṛbhiḥ), नृभि, noun, instrumental plural, masculine (compound with अस्वतन्तरकैः);  
अस्वतन्तरकैः (asvatantarakaiḥ), अस्वतन्तरक, noun, instrumental plural, masculine;  
सदा (sadā), सदा, indeclinable adverb;  
सदाचारपरायणैवरैः (sadācāraparāyaṇaivaraiḥ), सदाचारपरायण (tatpuruṣa compound), adjective/noun, instrumental plural masculine;

(Literally: "Is grouped/assembled by those men free from control (नृभि अस्वतन्तरक in instrumental plural) always by (सदा) those devoted to good conduct (सदाचारपरायणैवरैः instrumental plural).")

Smooth English translation:  
"They are always assembled by those men who are independent and devoted to good conduct."
