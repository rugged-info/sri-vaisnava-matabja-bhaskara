=== मूलपाठः ===


वर्षलक्षकम्' इति।  
न स्तियं परिगृह्णीयाद्विरक्तो वैष्णवः कचित् ।  
मन्वादिस्मृतिशास्त्रेषु तत्रिषेधस्य शासनात् ।।१२४।।  

varṣalakṣakam' iti।  
na stiyaṁ parigṛhṇīyād virakto vaiṣṇavaḥ kacit।  
manvādimṛtiśāstreṣu tatriṣedhasya śāsanāt।।124।।  

(वर्षलक्षकम्) varṣalakṣakam, वर्षलक्षकम्, neuter, accusative singular  
(इति) iti, indeclinable, particle (quotation marker)  
(न) na, indeclinable, negation  
(स्तियं) stiyaṁ, स्तियम्, neuter accusative singular  
(परिगृह्णीयात्) parigṛhṇīyāt, परिगृह्णीयात्, verb, optative/conditional mood, 3rd person singular  
(विरक्तः) viraktaḥ, विरक्त, masculine nominative singular (participle)  
(वैष्णवः) vaiṣṇavaḥ, वैष्णव, masculine nominative singular  
(कचित्) kacit, कचित्, indeclinable, particle (emphatic)  
(मन्वादि) manvādi, मन्वादि, masculine/neuter, locative plural, compound (tatpuruṣa: मनु + आदि)  
(स्मृतिशास्त्रेṣu) smṛtiśāstreṣu, स्मृतिशास्त्र, masculine locative plural (द्वन्द्व compound)  
(तत्र) tatra, indeclinable, adverb (there)  
(इषेधस्य) iṣedhasya, इषेध, masculine/genitive singular (compound constituent)  
(शासनात्) śāsanāt, शासन, neuter, ablative singular  

Not "varṣalakṣakam" (a hundred thousand years), thus one should not accept "stiyaṁ" (the fortress) being lost. A detached Vaiṣṇava certainly (kacit) should not accept the fortress being lost, because of the prohibitions in the Manvādi Smṛti scriptures.
