=== मूलपाठः ===


जीवो द्विधाऽमन्यत बद्धमुक्तभेदेन पूर्वेस्तु महर्षिवर्यैः ।
बद्धोऽपि तत्र द्विविधो मुमुक्षुबुभुक्षुभेदाद्रदितो मतज्ञैः ।।१४० ।।

jīvo dvidhā'manyata baddhamuktabhedena pūrvestu maharṣivaryaiḥ ।
baddho'pi tatra dvidhayo mumukṣububhukṣubhedādradito matajñaiḥ ॥140॥

The jīva (living being) is 2-fold understood (anyata, "considered") by distinctions of bound and free (baddha-mukta-bhedena). But in former times by great sages and best ones (pūrve tu maharṣivaryaiḥ).  
The bound one also there is 2-fold, by the difference of mumukṣu (desirer of liberation) and bubhukṣu (desirer of enjoyment), marked (raditaḥ) by knowledgeable thinkers (matajñaiḥ).  

The living being is considered twofold by the difference of bound and free, but in former times, by the great sages and excellent ones.  
The bound living being is also twofold there, classified by the difference between the liberation-seeker and the enjoyer, as determined by the knowledgeable.
