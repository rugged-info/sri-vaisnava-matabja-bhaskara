=== मूलपाठः ===


श्रवणादिमात्रनिष्ठाः शुद्धभक्ताः प्रकीर्तिताः ।
अन्तर्भव्यिास्तत्र तत्र तथानुक्ता मुमुक्षवः ।।१५१।।

śravaṇādimātranishṭhāḥ śuddhabhaktāḥ prakīrtitāḥ ।
antarbhavyiāstatra tatra tathānuktā mumukṣavaḥ ॥151॥

श्रवणादिमात्रनिष्ठाः (śravaṇādimātranishṭhāḥ), श्रवणादि-मात्र-निष्ठाः, tatpuruṣa; nominative plural masculine adjective;  
शुद्धभक्ताः (śuddhabhaktāḥ), शुद्ध-भक्ताः, tatpuruṣa; nominative plural masculine adjective;  
प्रकीर्तिताः (prakīrtitāḥ), प्रकीर्तित, past passive participle, nominative plural masculine adjective;  
अन्तर्भव्यि: (antarbhavyiḥ), अन्तर्भव्य, present participle, nominative plural masculine adjective;  
अस्ति (asti) abbreviated as अ (a) in compound form, indicative present third person singular verb, here in compound form with अन्तर्भव्यि;  
तत्र (tatra), indeclinable adverb (there);  
तत्र (tatra), indeclinable adverb (there);  
तथानुक्ता (tathānuktā), तद्+आनुक्ता, tatpuruṣa, past passive participle, nominative plural masculine adjective;  
मुमुक्षवः (mumukṣavaḥ), मुमुक्षु, nominative plural masculine noun.

(श्रवणादि-मात्र-निष्ठाः) "those steadfast only in listening and similar (activities)", (शुद्धभक्ताः) "pure devotees", (प्रकीर्तिताः) "are proclaimed", (अन्तर्भव्यि: अस्ति) "(are) present within", (तत्र, तत्र) "here and there", (तथानुक्ताः) "thus said/described", (मुमुक्षवः) "the seekers of liberation".

Those pure devotees, steadfast solely in practices like hearing, are thus proclaimed; they are present here and there, the seekers of liberation.

Those pure devotees, dedicated solely to practices such as listening, are thus declared; they exist here and there, these seekers of liberation.
