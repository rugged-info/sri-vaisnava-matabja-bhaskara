=== मूलपाठः ===


शालग्रामममोघदिव्यफलदं देवं हरिक्षेत्रतो-  
योध्यायां रघुपुङ्गवं गुणनिधिं श्रीरामचन्द्र प्रभुम् ।  
सत्स्थाने मथुराभिधाश्रमवरे श्रीबालकृष्णं परं  
मायायां मधुसूदनं सुरनरध्येयाङ्घ्रिपद्यं सदा ।।१९१।।

śālagrāmamamoghadivyaphaladaṃ devaṃ harikṣetrato-  
yodhyāyāṃ raghupuṅgavaṃ guṇanidhiṃ śrīrāmacandra prabhum ।  
satsthāne mathurābhidāśramavare śrībāla-kṛṣṇaṃ paraṃ  
māyāyāṃ madhusūdanaṃ suranaradhyeyaāṅghripadyaṃ sadā ॥191॥

amogha (amogha, adj. nom. sg. n.)  
divya (divya, adj. nom. sg. n.)

sadā (sadā, indeclinable adverb)

Salagram (śālagrāmam) — (an) infallible (amogha), divine (divya) fruit-giving (phaladaṃ) god (devaṃ) from Harikṣetra (harikṣetrataḥ, ablative: 'from Harikṣetra'),  
in Ayodhya (yodhyāyām, locative), Raghupungava (best of the Raghu clan) (raghupuṅgavam), treasure of virtues (guṇanidhiṃ), Lord Śrī Rāmacandra (śrīrāmacandra prabhum, acc.),  
in the good place (satsthāne), in the best (vare) Mathurā-abhidā-śrama (mathurābhidāśramavare, locative)  
Supreme (paraṃ) Śrī Bālakṛṣṇa (śrībālakṛṣṇaṃ, acc.)  
in Māyā (māyāyām, locative) Madhusūdana (madhusūdanaṃ, acc.), (who is) always worshipped by gods and men by touching his feet (suranaradhyeyaāṅghripadyaṃ sadā).

In Ayodhya, the Salagram, the infallible, divine fruit-giving god from Harikṣetra, the best of the Raghu lineage, the treasure of virtues, Lord Śrī Rāmacandra;  
and in the auspicious place, the best Mathurā-abhidā-śrama, the supreme Śrī Bālakṛṣṇa;  
and in Māyā, Madhusūdana, who is always worshipped by gods and men by touching his feet.
