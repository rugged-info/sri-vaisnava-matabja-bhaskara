=== मूलपाठः ===


रामानन्दप्रोक्तः श्रीवैष्णवमतान्नभास्करो भवतु ।
आनन्दभाष्यसोदर एषोऽन्तस्तमोविनाशाय ।।२०४।।


rāmānandaproktaḥ śrīvaiṣṇavamatān nabhāskaró bhavatu ।
ānandabhāṣyasodara eṣó'ntastamovināśāya ।।204।।


रामानन्दप्रोक्तः (rāmānandaproktaḥ), रामानन्द (rāmānanda) + प्रोकृ (pro-kṛ, past participle of √vac "to speak"), adjective, masc. sing. nom. (tatpuruṣa compound: "spoken by Rāmānanda")  
श्रीवैष्णवमतान् (śrīvaiṣṇavamatān), श्रीवैष्णवमत (śrīvaiṣṇavamata, compound tatpuruṣa: श्री (śrī, honorific) + वैष्णव (vaiṣṇava, relating to Viṣṇu) + मत (mata, opinion)), masc. acc. pl. (object)  
नभास्करः (nabhāskaraḥ), नभास्कर (nabhāskara, noun masc. sing. nom.), subject  
भवतु (bhavatu), verb, 3rd person singular, imperative, active (may he be, let him be)  
आनन्दभाष्यसोदर (ānandabhāṣyasodara), आनन्द (ānanda, bliss) + भाष्य (bhāṣya, commentary) + सोदर (sodara, born of/son), compound bahuvrīhi, masc. sing. nom. (the son of the blissful commentary)  
एषः (eṣaḥ), pronoun, masc. sing. nom. (this)  
अन्तः (antaḥ), noun masc. sing. nom. (end, darkness)  
तमः (tamaḥ), noun masc. sing. gen. (darkness, of darkness)  
विनाशाय (vināśāya), noun masc. sing. dat., used as purpose (for destruction)  


(Rāmānanda-proktah) — spoken by Rāmānanda,  
(śrīvaiṣṇava-matān) — the opinions/doctrines of the glorious Vaiṣṇavas (accusative plural, object)  
(nabhāskaraḥ) — the sun (subject)  
(bhavatu) — may he be / let there be; (imperative)  

(ānanda-bhāṣya-sodara) — the son of the blissful commentary (subject)  
(eṣaḥ) — this (masculine singular nominative)  
(antaḥ) — end/end darkness (nominative)  
(tamaḥ vināśāya) — for destruction of darkness (purpose dative).  

May the sun, which is spoken of by Rāmānanda, be the destroyer of darkness of the inner self, which is the blissful commentary’s son, and the embodiment of the glorious Vaiṣṇava doctrines.  


May the sun, praised by Rāmānanda, be the destroyer of inner darkness, the glorious embodiment of Vaiṣṇava teachings, and the child of the blissful commentary.
