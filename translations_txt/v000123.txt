=== मूलपाठः ===


सावरणनिरावरणरूपो भेदो द्योत्यते।
जलस्थलोत्पत्रशरीरिहिंसया विवर्जयेन्मां समुदारधीः सदा ।
दयापरोऽधीगतिहेतुरूपया चिराय लभ्यं भवभीनिवृत्तये ।।१२३।।

sāvaraṇanirāvaraṇarūpo bhedo dyotyate ।
jalasthalotpatraśarīrihiṁsayā vivarjayenmāṁ samudāradhīḥ sadā ।
dayāparo’dhīgatiheturūpayā cirāya labhyaṁ bhavabhīnivṛttaye ।।123।।

(सावरणनिरावरणरूपः) (सावरण; निर्आवरण; रूपः; tatpuruṣa) (बेदः) (बेद:) (द्योत्यते) (द्योत्यते; द्योति; 3rd person; singular; present; passive; verb)
(जलस्थलोत्पत्रशरीरिहिंसया) (जल; स्थल; उत्पत्र; शरीर; हिंसया; bahuvrīhi + instrumental singular feminine) (विवर्जयेन्मां) (विवर्जय (verb root); 1st person; singular; imperative; verb + माञ् (accusative singular pronoun)) (समुदारधीः) (समुदारधि; plural; masculine; nominative) (सदा) (सदा; indeclinable; adverb)
(दयापरः) (दयापरः; masculine; singular; nominative) (अधीगति) (अधीगति; feminine; singular; nominative) (हेतुः) (हेतुः; masculine; singular; nominative) (रूपया) (रूप (instrumental singular neuter)) (च) (च; indeclinable; conjunction) (चिराय) (चिराय; indeclinable; dative singular) (लभ्यं) (लभ्यम्; neuter; singular; nominative/accusative) (भवभीनिवृत्तये) (भवाभीनिवृत्तये; feminine; singular; dative)

Sāvaraṇa-nirāvaraṇa-rūpaḥ bhedaḥ dyotyate |
jalasthala-utpatra-śarīri-hiṁsayā vivarjayemaṃ samudāradhayaḥ sadā |
dayā-paraḥ adhī-gati-hetu-rūpayā ca cirāya labhyaṃ bhava-bhīni-vṛttaye ||

The differentiation (bhedaḥ) characterized as (rūpaḥ) with covering and uncovering (sāvaraṇa-nirāvaraṇa) is revealed (dyotyate).
By non-violence towards water, land, plants, and bodies (instrumental), may we avoid violence (imperative).
The ocean-like ones (plural nominative) always (sadā) are.
The one devoted to compassion (dayā-paraḥ), the cause of superior movement (adhī-gati-hetu), along with such form (rūpayā), for a long time (cirāya; dative), attainable (labhyam) for the cessation of worldly fears and sufferings (bhava-bhīni-vṛttaye; dative).

The distinction characterized by covering and uncovering is revealed.
By non-violence towards water, land, plants, and beings, may we always avoid harm.
The merciful cause of higher movement, with that form, is attainable for a long time for the ending of worldly fears and sufferings.
