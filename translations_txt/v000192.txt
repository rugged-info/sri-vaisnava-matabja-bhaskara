=== मूलपाठः ===


काश्यां भीगिशयं सनातनमथावन्त्यामवन्तीपतिं  
श्रीमद्वारवतीतिनाग्नि शुभदे श्रीयादवेन्दरं मुदा ।  
रम्ये श्रीव्रजनामके सुरनतं गोपीजनानां प्रियं  
ब्रह्मेशादिकिरीटसेवितपदाम्भीजं भुजङ्गाश्रयम् ।।१९२।।  

kāśyāṁ bhīgiśayaṁ sanātanamathāvantyāmavantīpatiṁ  
śrīmadvāravatītināgni śubhade śrīyādavendaraṁ mudā ।  
ramye śrīvrajanāmake suranataṁ gopījanānāṁ priyaṁ  
brahmeśādikirīṭasevitapadāmbhījaṁ bhujaṅgāśrayam ॥192॥  

kāśyām (kāśyā, locative singular feminine)  
bhīgiśayaṁ (bhīgiśaya, accusative singular masculine)  
sanātanam (sanātana, accusative singular neuter)  
atha (atha, indeclinable, conjunction)  
vantyām (vantyā, locative singular feminine)  
avantīpatiṁ (avantī-pati, accusative singular masculine, compound tatpuruṣa)  
śrīmat (śrīmat, indeclinable adjective) – as prefix to vāra-vatī-tināgni compound  
vāra-vatī (vāra + vatī, bahuvrīhi compound feminine)  
tināgni (ti + nāgni, bahuvrīhi compound masculine)  
śubhade (śubha + de, vocative singular masculine compound)  
śrīyā (śrī-yā, instrumental singular feminine compound)  
adavendaraṁ (ada + vīndara, accusative singular masculine compound)  
mudā (mudā, indeclinable adverb)  
ramye (ramya, locative singular neuter adjective)  
śrīvrajanāmake (śrī + vrajanāma + āk, locative singular masculine compound)  
suranataṁ (sura + nataṁ, accusative singular masculine compound)  
gopījanānāṁ (gopījana, genitive plural masculine)  
priyaṁ (priya, accusative singular neuter adjective)  
brahmeśādikiriṭasevitapadāmbhījaṁ (compound: brahmeśa + adi + kiriṭa + sevita + pada + ambhīja, accusative singular masculine)  
bhujaṅgāśrayam (bhujaṅga + āśraya, accusative singular masculine compound)  

In Kashi (locative; place) the bhīgiśaya (moist ground, water-laden)  
sanātanam (eternal [thing], accusative) and atha (indeclinable conjunction 'then'),  
vantyām (in the plentiful [feminine locative]),  
avantīpatiṁ (master of Avantī [king], accusative)  
śrīmat + vāra-vatī-tināgni (the glorious, possessing blessings and fire),  
śubhade (O giver of auspiciousness),  
śrīyā + adavendaraṁ (O lord adorned with glory, accusative),  
mudā (gladly),  
ramye (in the charming place)  
śrīvrajanāmake (named Śrīvraja, locative),  
suranataṁ (lord of gods, accusative),  
gopījanānāṁ priyaṁ (beloved of the cowherd maidens, genitive plural + accusative singular),  
brahmeśādikiriṭasevitapadāmbhījaṁ (lotus-footed [ambhīja], served by the crown worn by Brahmā and others, accusative),  
bhujaṅgāśrayam (sheltered by snakes, accusative)  

The eternal moist ground of Kashi, then, the master of Avantī, the giver of auspiciousness, the glorious lord endowed with fire and blessings, the exalted lord adorned with splendour, gladly in the charming place called Śrīvrajanāma, the protector of gods and beloved of the gopis, lotus-footed attended upon by the crowns of Brahmā and others, sheltered by serpents.  

In Kashi’s eternal moist ground, then, the great lord of Avantī, the giver of blessings and radiant fire, adorned with glory and joy, gladly dwells in the charming realm named Śrīvrajanāma—the protector of gods, beloved by the gopis, lotus-footed, crowned like Brahmā and others, and sheltered by serpents.
