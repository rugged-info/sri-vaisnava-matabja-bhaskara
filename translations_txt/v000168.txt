=== मूलपाठः ===


प्रातर्मध्याहसायं कृतशुचिकृतिभिः राममभ्यर्च्य सम्यक्  
श्रीमद्रामायणेन प्रतिदिनमखिलैभरितेन प्रपत्रैः ।  
शक्तैरानन्दभाष्यैरथ च॒ शुभतमाचार्यदिव्यप्रबन्धेः  
कालक्षेपो विधेयः सुविजितकरणैः स्वाकृतेर्यावदन्तम् ।।१६८ ।।

prātarmadhyāhasāyaṃ kṛtaśucikṛtibhiḥ rāmamabhyarcya samyak  
śrīmadrāmāyaṇena pratidinamakhilaibharitena prapatraiḥ ।  
śaktairānandabhāṣyai ratha câ śubhatamācāryadivyaprabandheḥ  
kālākṣepo vidheyaḥ suvijitakaraṇaiḥ svākṛteryāvadantam ।।168 ।।

"प्रातर्मध्याहसायम् कृतशुचिकृतिभिः रामम् अभ्यर्च्य सम्यक्"  
(At dawn, noon, and evening, with actions performed with purity,)  
"श्रीमद्रामायणेन प्रतिदिनम् अखिलैभरितेन प्रपत्रैः"  
"(by the glorious Ramayana daily, with words filled with completeness via the leaves)"  
"शक्तैः आनन्दभाष्यैः अथ च शुभतमाचार्यदिव्यप्रबन्धेः"  
"(with powerful commentaries of bliss, and also the most excellent divine treatise by the teacher)"  
"कालक्षेपः विधेयः सुविजितकरणैः स्वाकृतेर् यावदन्तम्"  
"(the passing of time is to be observed, with well-conquered instruments, according to one's own nature, up to the end)."

At dawn, noon, and evening, having worshiped Rama with pure and accomplished rites,  
daily reading the entire sacred Ramayana on leaves filled with it,  
with powerful and blissful commentaries, and also the most excellent divine treatise of the teacher,  
the passing of time is to be devoted, using well-mastered implements, according to one's own nature, until the very end.
