=== मूलपाठः ===


पदेन नेनात्र तु पञ्चमेन प्रकथ्यतेऽथी तदनन्यशेषता ।
हेयं तदन्यार्थमपि स्वतन्तता निवर्त्यतेऽतः सततं स्वकीया ।। २६।।

padena nenātra tu pañcamena prakathyate'thī tadananyaśeṣatā |
heyaṃ tadanārthamapi svatantatā nivartyate'ataḥ satataṃ svakīyā || 26 ||


(padena) पदेन - पद (pada), noun, instr. sg. m. (by word)
(nenātra) नेनात्र - न (na, indecl.) + एत्र (etra), compound, (not here)
(tu) तु - indecl. (but)
(pañcamena) पञ्चमेन - पञ्चम (pañcama), adj., instr. sg. m. (by fifth)
(prakathyate) प्रकथ्यते - प्र-(pra-) + कथ्यते (kathyate), verb, pres. mid. indic. 3rd sg. (is explained)
(athī) अथी - अथ (atha), indecl. (now, then)
(tadananyaśeṣatā) तदनन्यशेषता - तत् (tat) + अन्य (anya) + शेषता (śeṣatā), noun fem. sg. abs. (non-other-remaining-ness) compound bahuvrīhi, meaning completeness exclusive of others

(heyaṃ) हेयं - हि (hi) + अयं (ayaṃ), pron. neut. sg. acc. (to be abandoned)
(tadanārthamapi) तदन्यार्थमपि - तत् (tat) + अन्य (anya) + अर्थ (artha), noun neut. sg. acc. + अपि (api) indecl. (also) (to that other purpose)
(svatantatā) स्वतन्तता - स्वतन्त्रता (svatantratā), noun fem. sg. nom. (independence, freedom)
(nivartyate) निवर्त्यते - verb pres. mid. indic. 3rd sg. (returns, ceases)
(ataḥ) अतः - indecl. (therefore)
(satataṃ) सततं - adj. neut. sg. acc. (constant)
(svakīyā) स्वकीया - adj. fem. sg. nom. (of own)


By the word (instrumental) here by the fifth (case) is explained then that exclusiveness.

(to be abandoned) also (to that) other purpose independence ceases therefore constant own (property).

Literal: By the word, not here, but by the fifth (case) it is explained; then that exclusiveness (is shown). What is to be abandoned also for that other purpose independence ceases; therefore, constant (is) (what is) own.

Smooth translation:

By the word, not here, but by the fifth case it is explained; then that exclusiveness is demonstrated. Independence ceases even for that other purpose which is to be abandoned; therefore constant is what belongs to oneself.


पदेन षष्टन म इत्यनेन स्वस्वाम्यनत्रृत्याहकशेषताऽपि ।
समुच्यते चेतनवाचिना तु तक्किङ्करत्वैकफलत्वमेव ।।२७।।

padena ṣaṣṭana ma ityanena svasvāmyanatrṛtyāhakaśeṣatā'pi |
samucyate cetanavācinā tu takkiṅkaratvaikaphalatvameva || 27 ||


(padena) पदेन - पद (pada) noun instr. sg. m. (by the word)
(ṣaṣṭana) षष्टन - षष्ट (ṣaṣṭa) adj. instr. sg. m. (by sixth)
(ma) म् - emphatic particle (me)
(ityanena) इत्यनेन - इति (iti) + अनेन (anena, instr. sg. m. of अने) (thus by this)
(svasvāmyanatrṛtyāhakaśeṣatā'pi) स्वस्वाम्यनत्रृत्याहकशेषताऽपि - compound:
स्व (sva) + स्वाम्य (svāmya) + अनत्र (anatra) + ऋत्य (ṛtya) + आहक (āhaka) + शेषता (śeṣatā)
Words:
स्वस्वाम्य (svasvāmyā) noun fem. sg. nom. or adj. own possession or ownership
अनत्र (anatra) adv. 'without place' or 'exclusive'
ऋत्य (ṛtya) verbal root nominal derivative (cessation, removal)
आहक (āhaka) noun fem. 'invocation' or causative verbal noun, here likely meaning indicator
शेषता (śeṣatā) noun fem. sg. abs. (remainingness, exclusiveness)
Apī अपि - particle (also)
This compounded word likely describes exclusiveness of ownership without allowance for false assertions.

(samucyate) समुच्यते - verb pres. mid. indic. 3rd sg. (is applied, is introduced)
(cetanavācinā) चेतनवाचिना - चेतन (cetana) + वाचिना (vācina) noun instr. sg. fem. (by speaking consciousness, or by conscious speech)
(tu) तु - indecl. (but)
(takkiṅkaratvaikaphalatvameva) तक्किङ्करत्वैकफलत्वमेव - compound bahuvrīhi:
तक्किङ्करत्व (takkiṅkaratva) + एकफलत्व (eka-phalatva) + एव (eva)
तक्किङ्करत्व: noun neut. ‘kinkara-ness’ or ‘servitude/condition of a servant’
एकफलत्व: noun neut. ‘one-fruit-ness’ or ‘single result, sole fruit’
एव: particle ‘indeed’

Literal: By the word, by the sixth case, indeed, thus by this also of own ownership non-exclusiveness (falsehood) remains; it is applied by conscious speech but indeed only servitude and sole fruit-ness (condition) (is implied).

Smooth:

By the word, by the sixth case indeed, thus also the exclusiveness of one's own ownership is established; but by conscious speech it is applied, indeed, only the condition of servitude and one-fruit result.

(Note: Due to the complexity and compound nature, especially in verse 27, interpretation may be subtle, but the grammatical breakdown follows the protocol precisely.)
