=== मूलपाठः ===


जगत्पते श्रीश जगत्रिवास प्रभी जगत्कारण रामचन्द्र ।
नमो नमः कारुणिकाय तुभ्यं पादाब्जयुग्मे तव भक्तिरस्तु ।।१३४।

jagatpate śrīśa jagatrivāsa prabhī jagatkāraṇa rāmacandra |
namo namaḥ kāruṇikāya tubhyaṃ pādābjayugme tava bhaktirastu ||134||

जगत्पते (jagatpate), जगतः (jagataḥ), पुल्लिङ्ग, एकवचन, पितृवचन (अतिशयोक्ति), सम्बोधन  
श्रीश (śrīśa), श्री (śrī), पुल्लिङ्ग, एकवचन, नाम-रूप  
जगत्रिवास (jagatrivāsa), जगत् (jagat) + रिवास (rivāsa, 'resident'), तत्पुरुष समास  
प्रभी (prabhī), प्रभु (prabhu), पुल्लिङ्ग, एकवचन, नाम-रूप  
जगत्कारण (jagatkāraṇa), जगत् (jagat) + कारण (kāraṇa), तत्पुरुष समास  
रामचन्द्र (rāmacandra), राम (rāma) + चन्द्र (candra), तत्पुरुष समास, पुरुषनाम

नमो (namo), नमः (namah), निपात (indeclinable, indeclinable particle)  
नमः (namaḥ), नमः (namah), निपात  
कारुणिकाय (kāruṇikāya), कारुणिक (kāruṇika, 'compassionate'), पुल्लिङ्ग, एकवचन, पुरुषवचन, पुरुषेण संबोधनार्थे दत्त्वा (dative case)  
तुभ्यं (tubhyaṃ), तुमि (tumi - 2nd person pronoun), पुरुषवचन, द्विवचन, संभोधन, दातृवचन  
पादाब्जयुग्मे (pādābjayugme), पादाब्ज (pādābja, 'lotus feet') + युग्म (yugma, 'pair'), पुंल्लिङ्ग, एकवचन, सप्तमी (locative)  
तव (tava), तत् (tat) + व (va), सर्वनाम, द्वितीया व्यक्तिवाचक सर्वनाम, पुंल्लिङ्ग, एकवचन  
भक्तिरस्तु (bhaktirastu), भक्ति (bhakti, 'devotion'), स्त्रीलिङ्ग, एकवचन, विषयवाच्य, "अस्तु" (astu - इच्छार्पणवाचक, optative/imperative) 

"jagatpate" - O Lord of the world (vocative case)  
"śrīśa" - O Śrī (honorific), vocative  
"jagatrivāsa" - resident of the world (compound, vocative)  
"prabhī" - O Lord (vocative)  
"jagatkāraṇa" - cause of the world (compound, vocative)  
"rāmacandra" - Rāmacandra (proper name, vocative)  

"namo" "namaḥ" - salutations, repeated (indeclinables, expression of respect)  
"kāruṇikāya" - to the compassionate one (dative case)  
"tubhyaṃ" - to you (dative case, singular)  
"pādābjayugme" - on the pair of lotus feet (locative singular)  
"tava" - your (possessive pronoun)  
"bhaktirastu" - may (there be) devotion (optative/imperative particle "astu") 

O Lord of the world, O Śrī, O resident of the world, O Lord, O cause of the world, O Rāmacandra,  
Salutations, salutations, to you, compassionate one,  
May there be devotion of yours at the pair of your lotus feet.

O Lord of the world, O Śrī, O world-resident, O Lord, O cause of the world, O Rāmacandra,  
Repeated salutations to you, the compassionate one; may your devotees’ devotion abide at the pair of your lotus feet.
