=== मूलपाठः ===


वाक्यार्था मन्तरलस्य त्वथ निर्णीयते बुधः ।।४२।।
प्राप्यप्रापकसम्बन्धस्वरूपाभिनिरूपणम् ।
प्रधानार्थस्तु तद्युग्मकैट्कर्यस्य प्रधानता ।।४३।।

vākyarthā mantaralasya tvatha nirṇīyate budhaḥ ||42||
prāpyaprapakasambandhasvarūpābhinirūpaṇam |
pradhānārthastu tadyugmakaiṭkaryasya pradhānatā ||43||

वाक्यार्था (vākyarthā) vākyartha (vacana=singular; kāraka=none; gender=feminine) mūla (base) "vākyartha": vacana=singular, nominative, feminine; tatpuruṣa compound (vākyam + arthaḥ)
मन्तरलस्य (mantaralasya) mantarala (mūla); mantaralaḥ (maskiṇa, "mixed wine/honey")
त्वथ (tvatha) indeclinable (particle) "thus, indeed, then"
निर्णीयते (nirṇīyate) nirṇīt (base nirṇī to decide) verb, paragata laṭ, 3rd person, singular, passive voice ("is decided")
बुधः (budhaḥ) budha (mūla) noun, masculine, nominative, singular, "wise man"
प्राप्यप्रापकसम्बन्धस्वरूपाभिनिरूपणम् (prāpyaprapakasambandhasvarūpābhinirūpaṇam) compound noun, neuter, singular, nominative:
प्राप्य (prāpya) prāpya (indeclinable participle or gerund) "obtained, attained"
अप्रापक (aprapaka) a-prāpaka (mūla) "non-obtainer" or "non-receiver"
सम्बन्ध (sambandha) sambandha (mūla) noun masculine/neuter composite, means "relation"
स्वरूप (svarūpa) svarūpa noun masculine, "own form/essence"
अभिनिरूपणम् (abhinirūpaṇam) "careful exposition/definition", noun neuter, gerund nominal.
प्रधानार्थस्तु (pradhānārthastu) pradhānārtha "chief meaning", and tu "but, indeed" indeclinable particle
तद्युग्मकैट्कर्यस्य (tadyugmakaiṭkaryasya) compound: tad (that) + yugmakaiṭkarya (Yugmakaiṭkarya is a compound of yugma + kaiṭkarya)
प्रधानता (pradhānatā) pradhānatā noun, feminine, nominative, singular "prominence, chiefness"

Literal translation:
The meaning of the sentence of the 'mantra' (sacred phrase) then is decided by the wise man.
Attaining and non-attaining relations' own form careful definition.
But the chief meaning is indeed the chiefness of that pair-function.

Smooth English translation:
The wise man then decides the meaning of the mantra's sentence.
It is the detailed explanation of the nature of the relations between attainment and non-attainment.
But the main meaning is indeed the prominence of that paired function.
