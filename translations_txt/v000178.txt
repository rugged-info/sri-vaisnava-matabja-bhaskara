=== मूलपाठः ===


नवमप्रश्रोत्तरे प्राप्यनिरूपणम् २१३  
(६.१००.६३) इति रामायणोक्तेः।  
नित्यो ब्रह्मविधायकश्च पुरुषो वेदप्रदो ब्रह्मणे  
नित्यानां शरणं तपः प्रभृतिभिः सद्योगिनां दुर्लभः ।  
एकश्चेतनचेतनो भृतजगद्धयेयः स्वतन्त्रो वशी  
स प्राप्योऽस्ति मुमुक्षुभिः सुगुरुभिः सत्सक्धिभिस्तत्परैः ।।१७८ ।।

navamapraśrottare prāpyanirūpaṇam 213  
(6.100.63) iti rāmaāyaṇokteḥ.  
nityo brahmavidhāyakaśca puruṣo vedaprado brahmaṇe  
nityānāṃ śaraṇaṃ tapaḥ prabhṛtibhiḥ sadyogināṃ durlabhaḥ.  
ekaścetanacetano bhṛtajagaddhayeyaḥ svatantro vaśī  
sa prāpyo'sti mumukṣubhiḥ sugurubhiḥ satsakdhibhistatparaiḥ. 178.

नित्यम् (nityam), नित्य, neuter singular nominative; adjective "eternal"  
ब्रह्मविधायकः (brahmavidhāyakaḥ), ब्रह्मविधायक, masculine singular nominative; tatpuruṣa compound (brahma + vidhāyaka) "giver of Brahman/knowledge of Brahman"  
च (ca), indeclinable; conjunction "and"  
पुरुषः (puruṣaḥ), पुरुष, masculine singular nominative; noun "man"  
वेदप्रदः (vedapradaḥ), वेदप्रद, masculine singular nominative; tatpuruṣa compound (veda + prada) "giver of Veda/knowledge"  
ब्रह्मणे (brahmaṇe), ब्रह्मन्, masculine singular locative; "to Brahman"  
नित्यानाम् (nityānām), नित्य, masculine plural genitive; adjective "of the eternals"  
शरणम् (śaraṇam), शरण, neuter singular accusative; noun "refuge"  
तपः (tapaḥ), तपस्, masculine singular nominative; noun "austerity"  
प्रभृतिभिः (prabhṛtibhiḥ), प्रभृत्ति, masculine plural instrumental; indeclinable (compound element) "and those starting with" or "etc."  
सद्योगिनाम् (sadyoginām), स (prefix) + योगिन्, masculine plural genitive; compound "of perfect yogis"  
दुर्लभः (durlabhaḥ), दुर्लभ, masculine singular nominative; adjective "difficult to obtain"  
एकः (ekaḥ), एक, masculine singular nominative; numeral "one"  
चेतनः (cetanaḥ), चेतन, masculine singular nominative; adjective/noun "conscious"  
चेतनः (cetanaḥ), चेतन, masculine singular nominative; adjective/noun "conscious"  
भृत (bhṛta), भृत, masculine singular nominative; adjective "servant"  
जगत् (jagat), जगत्, neuter singular accusative; noun "world" (used here in tatpuruṣa compound)  
धयेयः (dhayeyah), धयेय, masculine singular nominative; adjective "worthy to be given"  
स्वतन्त्रम् (svatantram), स्वतंत्र, neuter singular nominative; adjective "independent"  
अ् (a) (saṃjñā), reflexive pronoun particle (part of word svatantro)  
वशी (vaśī), वशी, masculine singular nominative; adjective "master"  
स (sa), स, masculine singular nominative; pronoun "he"  
प्राप्य (prāpya), प्रत्यय participle, verbal noun "having obtained"  
अस् (as), अस्, present tense verb third person singular indicative active "is"  
मुमुक्षुभिः (mumukṣubhiḥ), मुमुक्षु, masculine plural instrumental; "by those desiring liberation"  
सुगुरुभिः (sugurubhiḥ), सुगुरु, masculine plural instrumental; "by good gurus"  
सत्सक्धिभिः (satsakdhibhiḥ), सतत्+सक्धि, masculine plural instrumental; compound meaning "by those with constant bond/dedication"  
तत्परैः (tatparaiḥ), तत्पर, masculine plural instrumental; adjective "by those devoted"  

Literal translation:  
(Eternal) (giver of Brahman knowledge) and (man) (giver of Veda) (to Brahman) (of the eternals) (refuge) (austerity) (by those with dedication etc.) (of perfect yogis) (difficult to obtain).  
One (conscious) (conscious) (worshipper of the world) (worthy of being given) (independent) (master).  
He (having obtained) (is) (by the desirous of liberation) (by good gurus) (by those with constant dedication) (by those devoted).  

Smooth English translation:  
The eternal one who imparts knowledge of Brahman, the man who bestows the Vedas upon Brahman, the refuge and austerity for the eternals, and is difficult to attain even by devoted perfect yogis.  
One who is conscious and the consciousness itself, the worshipper of the world, independent and masterful;  
He, having been attained by those seeking liberation, by esteemed gurus, by steadfast followers, and by the devoted, indeed exists.
