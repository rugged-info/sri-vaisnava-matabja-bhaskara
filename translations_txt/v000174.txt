=== मूलपाठः ===


मार्जनादिना भगवदालयस्य मार्जनेन पूजनपात्रप्रक्षालनेन यथाधिकारं जलाहरणादिना,  
भगवत्सपर्यर्थं पुष्पप्रचयेनाभिमतकुसुमाहरणेन तदीयनामाभ्यसनेनाविच्छिन्ननामोच्ारणेन  
नितरामल्यन्तं तन्मनाः भगवत्येव मनो यस्य, भगवति निरतिशय प्रीतिमान् सन् कालं  
क्षिपेदतिवाहयेत्तेन कालक्षेपो विधीयत इति भावः। तन्मना इति मन्मना भवेति  
'गवदुक्तत्यपेक्षाभिप्रायेणोक्तम्।  
तीर्थेषु वासेन सतां महात्मनां समागमेनाथ तदर्चनेन ।  
जिज्ञासया तद्यशसः श्रवेण तच्छरावणेन स्मरणेन तस्य ।।१७४।।

mārjanādinā bhagavadālayasya mārjanena pūjanapātraprakṣālanena yathādhikāraṁ jalāharaṇadinā,  
bhagavatsaparyarthaṁ puṣpapracayenābhimatakusumāharaṇena tadīyanāmābhyasanenāvicchinnanāmocāraṇena  
nitārāmyantaṁ tanmanāḥ bhagavaty eva mano yasya, bhagavati niratiśaya prītimān san kālaṁ  
kṣipedativāhayettena kālakṣepo vidhīyata iti bhāvaḥ। tanmanā iti manmanā bhaveti  
'gavaduktatypekṣābhiprāyeṇoktam।  
tīrtheṣu vāsena satāṁ mahātmnāṁ samāgamenaatha tadarcanena ।  
jijñāsayā tadyasaḥ śraveṇa taccharāvaṇena smaraṇena tasya ।।174।।

mārjanādinā (mārjanādinā, mārjanādina, indeclinable suffix -ādinā ‘beginning with cleaning’)  
bhagavadālayasya (bhagavad-ālaya- + sya, genitive singular masculine, tatpuruṣa compound: ‘of the temple of God’)  
mārjanena (mārjana, instrumental singular masculine, noun ‘cleaning, cleansing’)

yathādhikāraṁ (yathā + adhikāraṁ, accusative singular masculine, indeclinable + noun: ‘according to the proper authority/position’)

puṣpapracayenābhimatakusumāharaṇena (puṣpa + pracaya + ābhimata + kusuma + āharaṇena, instrumental singular feminine, complex compound: ‘by selected gathering and offering of flowers’)  
tadīyanāmābhyasanenāvicchinnanāmocāraṇena (tad- + īya + nāmābhyāsana + avicchinna + nā + ucāraṇena, instrumental singular feminine, complex compound: ‘by the uninterrupted chanting/pronunciation of those particular names’)

'gavaduktatypekṣābhiprāyeṇoktam (gavad + uktā + ti + an +  etc., indeclinable verbal compound ‘stated in dependence on what was spoken by Gava’)

(cleaning and similar acts such as cleaning the temple, washing worship vessels, fetching water, offering flowers selected as desired, uninterrupted chanting of those names, the mind thoroughly enchanted by the goddess herself, whose mind is exceedingly affectionate to the goddess, throws away time and wastes it, this waste of time should be regulated, meaning “thus”; thus becoming absorbed by that mind, this is spoken according to the expressed opinion dependent on Gava’s statement. By living in holy places, by the meeting of virtuous great persons, by that worship; by inquiry, by listening to reputation, by hearing, by listening carefully, by recalling His.)  

By means of cleaning and so forth of the temple, by washing the vessels for worship, by fetching water according to duty,  
for the sake of the Lord, by the desired gathering and offering of flowers, by the uninterrupted chanting of those names,  
the mind of one becomes thoroughly enchanted by the goddess herself, whose mind is exceedingly devoted and loving toward the goddess.  
Being so, he throws away (or passes) time extravagantly; such wastage of time should be regulated, thus is the meaning.  
“Thus absorbed by that mind,” it is said according to the opinion dependent on Gava’s statement.  
By living at pilgrimages, by the gathering of virtuous great souls, by that worship;  
by inquiry, by reputation, by hearing, by careful listening, and by remembering Him.
