=== मूलपाठः ===


विद्धाथ वेद्याग्नियुता हि रोहिणी  
ज्ञेया तु शुद्धा यदि सा परान्विता ।।९७।।

viddhātha vedyāgniyutā hi rohiṇī  
jneyā tu śuddhā yadi sā parānvitā ।।97।।

विद्धाथ (viddhātha), √विद् (vid), imperative, 2nd person singular  
वेद्य-अग्नि-युता (vedyā-agni-yutā), वेद्य (vedya, adj. "to be known") + अग्नी (agnī, "fire") + युत (yutá, "joined, united"), bahuvrīhi compound, feminine singular nominative  
हि (hi), particle, indeclinable  
रोहिणी (rohiṇī), रोहिणी (rohiṇī, proper noun, feminine singular nominative)  

ज्ञेया (jneyā), ज्ञेय (jñeya, "to be known," gerundive adjective), feminine singular nominative  
तु (tu), particle, indeclinable  
शुद्धा (śuddhā), शुद्ध (śuddha, "pure") adjective, feminine singular nominative  
यदि (yadi), conjunction, indeclinable  
सा (sā), pronoun, feminine singular nominative  
परान्विता (parā-nvitā), पर (para, "other, beyond") + अन्विका (anvikā, "connected"), past passive participle feminine singular nominative

"Know, indeed, Rohiṇī united with the sacred fire;  
She (is) to be known, indeed, pure, if she is endowed with the surpassing (attribute)."

"Certainly know that Rohiṇī, united with the sacred fire;  
She is indeed to be known as pure, if she is endowed with the supreme qualities."
