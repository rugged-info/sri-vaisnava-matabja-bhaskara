=== मूलपाठः ===


धृतोर्ध्वपुण्ड्स्तुलसीसमुद्धवां दधच्च कण्ठे शुभमालिकां जनः ।
तज्नन्मकर्माणि हरेरुदाहरेद् गृहश्च नामानि शुभप्रदानि सः ।।१६०।।

dhṛtordhva-puṇḍstulasī-samuddhvāṁ dadhac ca kaṇṭhe śubhamālikāṁ janaḥ ।
tajnnamakarmāṇi harerudāhared gṛhaś ca nāmāni śubhapradāni saḥ ॥160॥

धृतः (dhṛtaḥ), धृ (dhṛ), participle, past passive participle, masc. nom. sing.;  
ऊर्ध्व (ūrdhva), adjective, masc. nom. sing.;  
पुण्डस्तुलसी (puṇḍa-stulasī), compound (tatpuruṣa), masc. nom. sing.;  
तुलसी (tulasī), noun, fem. nom. sing.;  
समुद्धवां (samuddhvāṁ), adjective/participle, fem. acc. sing.;  
दधः (dadhaḥ), verb, √dha, 3rd pers. sing. perf. active;  
च (ca), indeclinable conjunction;  
कण्ठे (kaṇṭhe), noun, masc. loc. sing.;  
शुभमालिकां (śubhamālikāṁ), compound (tatpuruṣa), fem. acc. sing.;  
जनः (janaḥ), noun, masc. nom. sing.;

तजन् (tajan), present participle, √tyaj, masc. nom. sing.;  
न (na), indeclinable particle (negation);  
अकर्माणि (akarmāṇi), adjective, neut. acc. pl.;  
हरेरु (hareru), compound, masc. gen. sing. (of 'Hara', Śiva or Viṣṇu);  
दाहरेत् (dāharet), verb, optative mood, 3rd person singular, √dhā;  
गृहः (gṛhaḥ), noun, masc. nom. sing.;  
च (ca), conjunction;  
नामानि (nāmāni), noun, neut. nom./acc. pl.;  
शुभप्रदानि (śubhapradāni), compound (tatpuruṣa), neut. nom./acc. pl.;  
सः (saḥ), pronoun, masc. nom. sing.;

Held upward, the bunch of tulasi plants with clusters (acc. sg. fem. samuddhvāṁ), (he) placed (dadhḥ) on the neck (kaṇṭhe), the auspicious garland (śubhamālikām) the man (janaḥ).  
Not abandoning (tajan) the akarmāṇi (inactions / non-actions) of Hara (Śiva) (gen.) the house (gṛhaḥ) also, the auspicious gifts (śubhapradāni nāmāni), he (saḥ) would burn (optative mood, dāharet).

A man placed upward on his neck an auspicious garland made of a bunch of tulasi plants.  
Not abandoning the inactive deeds of Lord Hara, he would also burn at home the auspicious names and gifts.

A man wore an auspicious garland of tulasi clusters around his neck.  
Without renouncing the inactive deeds of Lord Hara, he purified his home by chanting holy names and making auspicious offerings.
