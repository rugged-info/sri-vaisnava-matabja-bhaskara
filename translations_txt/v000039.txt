=== मूलपाठः ===


शरणेतिपदेनैवोपायस्तद्विग्रहो बुधैः  
उपायाध्यवसायस्तु प्रपद्य इति वर्ण्यते ।। ३९।।

śaraṇetipadenaivopāyastadvigraho budhaiḥ  
upāyādhyavasāyastu prapadya iti varṇyate ।। 39 ।।

"By the very footing of refuge (loc. sg.) through (instrumental pl.)  
indeed (particle) the means (nom. sg.) that (nom. sg.) conflict (nom. sg.) by the wise (instrumental pl.)  
means' purpose (nom. sg.) indeed (particle) having resorted (nom. sg. participle) thus (particle) is described (3rd sing. passive indicative)."

By the very step of refuge only, the means and its conflict, by the wise, the purpose of means having resorted to, thus it is described.

It is said that the conflict of that means by wise persons occurs only through the very step of refuge, and by resorting to the purpose of the means.
