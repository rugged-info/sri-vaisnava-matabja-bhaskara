=== मूलपाठः ===


न मां दुष्कृतिनो मूढाः प्रपद्यन्ते नराधमाः। माययापहृतज्ञाना आसुरं भावमाश्रिताः

na māṁ duṣkṛtino mūḍhāḥ prapadyante narādhamāḥ। māyayāpahṛtajñānā āsuraṁ bhāvamāśritāḥ

(na) (na) indeclinable, negation  
(mām) (mām) pronoun, 1st person singular accusative  
(duṣkṛtinaḥ) (duṣkṛtinaḥ) noun, masculine, nominative plural, compound (tatpuruṣa), "bad-doers"  
(mūḍhāḥ) (mūḍha) adjective, masculine, nominative plural, "fools"  
(prapadyante) (prapadyate) verb, present, middle/passive, 3rd person plural, "resort to"  
(narādhamāḥ) (nara-adhama) noun, masculine, nominative plural, compound (bahuvrīhi), "worst of men"  
(māyayā) (māyā) noun, feminine instrumental singular, "by illusion"  
(apahṛta) (apahṛta) adjective, past participle, nominative plural feminine, "taken away"  
(jñānā) (jñāna) noun, neuter, nominative plural, "knowledge"  
(āsuram) (āsura) adjective, accusative singular masculine, "demonic"  
(bhāvam) (bhāva) noun, masculine accusative singular, "being/state"  
(āśritāḥ) (āśrita) adjective, masculine nominative plural, past participle, "having resorted to"

"Not to me (na mām) the bad-doers (duṣkṛtinaḥ), fools (mūḍhāḥ), worst of men (narādhamāḥ) resort (prapadyante). Having had their knowledge taken away by illusion (māyayā apahṛtajñānā), they have resorted to a demonic state (āsuraṁ bhāvam āśritāḥ)."

"The bad-doers, fools, the worst of men do not seek refuge in me. Having had their knowledge stolen by illusion, they take refuge in a demonic nature."
