=== मूलपाठः ===


वृन्दावने सुन्दरनन्दसूनुकं गोविन्दमेवं त्वथ कालियहृदे ।  
गोवर्धने गोपसुवेषधारिणं तथा भवघ्रेऽपि च पदालोचनम् ।।१९३॥।

vṛndāvane sundaranandasūnukaṃ govindamevaṃ tvatha kāliyahṛde ।  
govardhane gopasuveṣadhāriṇaṃ tathā bhavaghre'pi ca padālocanam ।।193।।

vṛndāvane (vṛndāvana, locative singular neuter)  
sundaranandasūnukaṃ (sundaranandasūnuka, accusative singular masculine)  
govindamevaṃ (govinda + eva + am, govinda base noun masculine accusative singular + emphatic particle + accusative marker)  
tvatha (indeclinable — conjunction/adverb)  
kāliyahṛde (kāliya + hṛda, kāliya = proper noun masculine locative singular, hṛda = locative singular neuter; compound tatpuruṣa)  

govardhane (govardhana, locative singular masculine)  
gopasuveṣadhāriṇaṃ (gopa + suveṣa + dhāriṇaṃ, gopa = noun masculine nominative or accusative plural or singular, suveṣa = noun neuter accusative singular, dhāriṇa = present participle masculine accusative singular; compound bahuvrīhi or tatpuruṣa)  
tathā (indeclinable — adverb)  
bhavaghre'pi (bhavaghra + api, bhavaghra = proper noun neuter locative singular + particle 'api')  
ca (indeclinable — conjunction)  
padālocanam (pada + ālocanam, pada = noun neuter nominative singular, ālocanam = noun neuter nominative singular; compound tatpuruṣa)  

In Vṛndāvaṇa, (the one who is) the son of Sundara-nanda, Govinda himself,  
and also in the heart of Kāliya;  
on Govardhana, the wearer of the garb of cowherds,  
likewise on Bhavaghṛa too, (he is) the one who observes the feet.  

In Vṛndāvaṇa, Govinda, the son of Sundara-nanda,  
and also in the heart of Kāliya,  
on Govardhana, the cowherds' robe-wearer,  
and even in Bhavaghṛa, he gazes upon the feet.
