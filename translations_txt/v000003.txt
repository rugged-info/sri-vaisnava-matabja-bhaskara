=== मूलपाठः ===


धन्यो नो मङ्गलौघं सपदि सुकुरुताद्रामशस्त्ास्सङ्खः ।।३।। *

dhanyo no maṅgalaughaṃ sapadi sukurutādrāmaśastās saṅkhaḥ ।।3।।

धन्यो (dhanyo), धन्य (dhanya, adjective, nominative singular masculine)
नो (no), अहम् (aham), pronoun, first person plural exclusive/genitive/dative etc. here likely dative or genitive plural
मङ्गलौघं (maṅgalaughaṃ), मङ्गलौघ (maṅgalaugha), neuter singular accusative (compound, likely tatpuruṣa)
सपदि (sapadi), sapad (indeclinable adverb)
सुकुरुताद्रामशस्त्ास्सङ्खः (sukurutādrāmaśastās saṅkhaḥ) — appears to be a compound word formed by क्रमः or समास:
- सुकुरुत (sukurut), masculine nominative singular, possibly tatpuruṣa ("good/dexterous action/maker")
- आद्राम (ādrāma), masculine nominative singular, base unclear (likely an epithet or proper noun)
- शस्त् (śast), from शस् (śas), verbal root with participial ending, masculine nominative singular (active agent)
- आस्सङ्खः (assaṅkhaḥ), possibly स्अस्सङ्खः (which includes स + सङ्खः) or a compound, nominative singular masculine.

Literal translation: "Fortunate to us the auspicious burden suddenly (is) the well-done and masterfully wielded decorated conch." (Note: grammatical and lexical ambiguities present)

The good, auspicious burden, suddenly well-struck and decorated conch, is a blessing for us.

(Note: This verse and the word 'सुकुरुताद्रामशस्त्ास्सङ्खः' appear to be highly compound and obscure; this is the closest literal and smooth translation.)
