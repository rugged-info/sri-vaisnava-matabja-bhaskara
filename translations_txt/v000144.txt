=== मूलपाठः ===


षष्टप्रश्रोत्तरे वैष्णवभेदनिरूपणम् १७७  
कामनारहित होकर निरन्तर भगवस्स्मृति में तन्मय रहते हँ! भगवदाज्ञा समदञ्चकर  
वेदविहित वर्णाश्रमकर्म का अनुष्ठान करते हे। एेसे मुमुद्षु भी उपासक ओर शुद्ध भक्त के  
भेद से दो प्रकार के होते है!  
* पदपरामर्शः *  
भगवत्कृपयानादिकर्मप्रवाहरूपसंसाराज्जातमोक्षाभिलाषान्युमुक्षूनपि विभजते  
मुमुक्षवोऽपीति । महर्षिभिस्तत््वापरोक्षानुभूतिं लब्धवद्धिः अकामाः स्मृतिभक्तिशालिन इति  
मुमुक्षवोऽपि द्विविधा प्रोक्ता इति पदान्वयः। महर्षिभिरित्यनेनाप्तत्वेन श्रीबोधायनाचार्य-  
प्रभृतीनां विभाजनेऽभिमतिं द्योतयित्वाऽर्वाचीनकल्पितत्वं निरस्यते। तत्र प्रथमे वक्ष्यमाण-  
लक्षणा अकामा भगवन्तं विना साधनान्तराश्रयणे नास्ति कामोऽभिरुचिर्येषान्ताटणशा भवन्ति,  
अपरे चाविच्छिन्नभगवस्स्मृतिरूपां ५क्तिमुपायत्वेनाश्रितवन्त उच्यन्ते। ये  
चोपासकणशुद्धभक्तभेदेनापि द्वैविध्यं भजन्ते। तदाह उपासकादिप्रतिभेद भेदिता इति।  
तथाविधानामपि तेषां वेदोक्तत्वाद्रर्णाश्रमकर्मणां परिपालने प्रमत्तां सूचयति -  
वेदोक्तेत्यादिना। केचन मुमुक्षणामपि देवतान्तरनिष्ठा भगवन्रिष्ठा इति विभज्य  
पुनरुपायभक्तिनिष्ठा भगवदेकनिष्ठा इति द्विधा विभरजनमाहुः।  
स्वकर्मविज्ञानसभक्तिसाधनं तथीररीकृत्य सुविज्ञ! कञ्चन ।  
सम्प्राप्य सम्बन्धविशेषमुत्तमं सदा भवन्त्येव च मोक्षनिश्चयाः ।।१४४।।  

ṣaṣṭapraśrottare vaiṣṇavabhedanirūpaṇam 177  
kāmanārahita hokara nirantara bhagavassmṛti meṁ tanmaya rahate haṁ! bhagavadājñā samadañcakara  
vedavihita varṇāśramakarma kā anuṣṭhāna karte he. Ese mumudṣu bhī upāsaka or śuddha bhakta ke  
bhed se do prakār ke hote hai!  
* padaparāmarśaḥ *  
bhagavatkṛpayānādikarmapravāharūpasansārājjātamokṣābhilāṣānyumukṣūnapi vibhajate  
mumukṣavo'pīti. maharṣibhistatvāparokṣānubhūtiṁ labdhavaddhiḥ akā māḥ smṛtibhaktiśālina iti  
mumukṣavo'pi dvividhā proktā iti padānvayaḥ. maharṣibhirityanenaaptatvena śrībodhāyanācārya-  
prabhṛtīnāṁ vibhajane'bhimatiṁ dyotayitvā'rvāchīnakalpitatvaṁ nirasyate. tatra prathame vakṣyamāṇa-  
lakṣaṇā akāma bhagavantaṁ vinā sādhānāntarāśrayaṇe nāsti kāmo'bhiruciryeṣāntāṭaṇaśā bhavanti,  
apare cāvicchinnabhagavassmṛtirūpāṁ śaktimupāyatvena āśritavanta ucyante. ye  
copāsakaṇaśuddhabhaktabhedenapi dvaivaidhyaṁ bhajante. tadāha upāsakādiprati-bheda bheditā iti.  
tathāvidhānāmapi teṣāṁ vedoktatvādvarṇaśramakarmaṇāṁ paripālane pramattāṁ sūcayati -  
vedoktetydādinā. kecana mumukṣaṇāmpi devatāntaraniṣṭhā bhagavanriṣṭhā iti vibhajya  
punarupāyabhaktiniṣṭhā bhagavadekaniṣṭhā iti dvidhā vibhrajana māhuḥ.  
svakarmavijñānasabhaktisādhanaṁ tathīrarīkṛtya suvajña! kañcana.  
samprāpya sambandhaviśeṣamut tamaṁ sadā bhavantyeva ca mokṣaniścayāḥ. 144.

॥१४४॥ (144): verse number  

कामनारहित होकर निरन्तर भगवस्स्मृति में तन्मय रहते हँ!  
kāmanārahita hokara nirantara bhagavassmṛti meṁ tanmaya rahate haṁ!  
free-from-desire being constantly in Lord's remembrance absorbed remain indeed!  

भगवदाज्ञा समदञ्चकर वेदविहित वर्णाश्रमकर्म का अनुष्ठान करते हे।  
bhagavadājñā samadañcakara vedavihita varṇāśramakarma kā anuṣṭhāna karte he.  
(divine-command) (equally-performing) (Veda-prescribed) (varna-ashrama-duty) (of) (performance) (do) (O!)  

एेसे मुमुद्षु भी उपासक ओर शुद्ध भक्त के भेद से दो प्रकार के होते है!  
ese mumudṣu bhī upāsaka ora śuddha bhakta ke bhed se do prakār ke hote hai!  
(such) (aspirants) (also) (worshippers) (and) (pure devotees) (of) (difference) (from) (two) (types) (are)!  

भगवत्कृपयानादिकर्मप्रवाहरूपसंसाराज्जातमोक्षाभिलाषान्युमुक्षूनपि विभजते मुमुक्षवोऽपीति ।  
bhagavatkṛpayānādikarmapravāharūpasansārājjātamokṣābhilāṣānyumukṣūnapi vibhajate mumukṣavo'pi ti.  
(divine) (by grace and other causes) (stream-of-action-like) (world) (born) (liberation-desires) (wishing-liberation) (also) (divides) (aspirants) (also) (thus).  

महार्षिभिस्तत््वापरोक्षानुभूतिं लब्धवद्धिः अकामाः स्मृतिभक्तिशालिन इति मुमुक्षवोऽपि द्विविधा प्रोक्ता इति पदान्वयः।  
maharṣibhistatvāparokṣānubhūtiṁ labdhavaddhiḥ akā māḥ smṛtibhaktiśālina iti mumukṣavo'pi dvividhā proktā iti padānvayaḥ.  
(by sages) (to reality direct experience) (having acquired) (desirous-less) (in memory devotion possessing) (thus) (aspirants) (also) (two types) (declared) (thus grammatical-explanation).  

महर्षिभिरित्यनेनाप्तत्वेन श्रीबोधायनाचार्य-प्रभृतीनां विभाजनेऽभिमतिं द्योतयित्वाऽर्वाचीनकल्पितत्वं निरस्यते।  
maharṣibhir ity anena aptatvena śrībodhāyanācārya-prabhṛtīnām vibhājane abhimatiṁ dyotayitvā arvācīnakalpitattvaṁ nirasyate.  
(by sages thus this certainty) (Śrī Bodhāyana teacher and others) (in division) (opinion) (illuminating) (modern fabrication) (is rejected).  

तत्र प्रथमे वक्ष्यमाण-लक्षणा अकामा भगवन्तं विना साधनान्तराश्रयणे नास्ति कामोऽभिरुचिर्येषान्ताटणशा भवन्ति,  
tatra prathame vakṣyamāṇa-lakṣaṇā akāma bhagavantaṁ vinā sādhānāntarāśrayaṇe nāsti kāmo 'bhirucir yeṣāntaṭaṇaśā bhavanti,  
(there first being explained - characteristic) (desireless) (Lord) (without) (means-other-shelter) (there is no desire) (pleasant) (persistent) (are).  

अपरे चाविच्छिन्नभगवस्स्मृतिरूपां ५क्तिमुपायत्वेनाश्रितवन्त उच्यन्ते।  
apare cāvicchinnabhagavassmṛtirūpāṁ śaktimupāyatvena āśritavanta ucyante.  
(other and uninterrupted Lord remembrance-form) (power-means instrumentally) (having taken refuge) (are called).

tathāvidhānāmapi teṣāṁ vedoktvatvāvarṇa-āśrama-karmaṇāṁ paripālane pramattāṁ sūcayati - vedoktettyādinā.

सम्प्राप्य सम्बन्धविशेषमुत्तमं सदा भवन्त्येव च मोक्षनिश्चयाः ।।१४४।।  
samprāpya sambandhaviśeṣamut tamaṁ sadā bhavantyeva ca mokṣaniścayāḥ. 144.  
(having attained) (connection particular excellent) (always) (be indeed) (and) (liberation-certainties).  

Being free from desires, constantly absorbed in the Lord’s remembrance, indeed remain!  
O you! Perform the Vedic prescribed duties of varna and ashrama equally under the command of the Lord.  
Such aspirants too, by the difference between worshippers and pure devotees, are of two types!  

The divine grace, the continuous flow of actions, the samsara-born desires for liberation, also divides the aspirants.  
The great sages have declared two types of aspirants: those who have attained direct realization of truth and are desireless, possessing devoted memory, and those of other types.  
By this certainty, as explained by great sages like Śrī Bodhāyana Ācārya and others, the opinion of modern invention is rejected.  

First, the characteristic being explained is that without desire and without reliance on other means, there is no desire that is pleasant or persistent;  
others, who have taken refuge in the uninterrupted form of remembrance of the Lord by means of power and method, are called so.  
Those who also worship with the distinction of worshippers and pure devotees, perform the duality.  
Thus, the distinction between worshippers and others is declared.  

Even according to such rules, their Vedic authenticity in performing duties of varnas and ashramas warns against negligence—through statements such as “as stated in the Vedas.”  
Some aspirants also classify devotion as either faith in deities or faith in the Lord.  
By again dividing into faith in methods of devotion and exclusive faith in the Lord, they say there are twofold distinctions.  

Having thoroughly examined the means of devotion by knowledge of one’s own duty, O well-informed one,  
having attained excellent particular connection, may liberation certainties always be certainly present. (verse 144)
