=== मूलपाठः ===


मोक्षे मुमुक्षोर्न हि तारतम्यं फले प्रपन्नस्य तु सत्प्रपत्तेः ।
अस्त्येव तद्रामकृपोपलभ्ये पर्ति प्रियोऽनन्तगुणार्णवन्तम् ।।१०३।।

mokṣe mumukṣorna hi tārathamyaṃ phale prapannasya tu satprapatteḥ ।
astyeva tadrāmakṛpopalabhye parti priyo'nantaguṇārṇavantam ॥103॥

मोक्षे (mokṣe) मोक्ष (mokṣa) ङस्मिन् स ध्यानपूर्वक अधिकृतः (locative singular masculine)  
मुमुक्षोर्न् (mumukṣorn) मुमुक्षु (mumukṣu) त (ablative singular masculine) + अपि (an enclitic particle shortened here to अ in compounds)  
हि (hi) - अव्ययः (indeclinable, emphatic particle)  
तारतम्यं (tārathamyaṃ) तारतम्य (tāratamya) संज्ञा (noun, accusative singular neuter)  
फले (phale) फल (phala) संज्ञा (noun, locative singular neuter)  
प्रपन्नस्य (prapannasya) प्रपन्न (prapन्नa) विशेषण (participle or adjective, genitive singular masculine)  
तु (tu) - अव्ययः (indeclinable, adversative particle)  
सत्प्रपत्तेः (satprapatteḥ) सत्त्व (sattva) + प्रपत्तिः (prapattiḥ) संयुक्त रूप (compound tatpuruṣa), genitive singular feminine  
अस्त्येव (astyeva) अस्ति (asti) धातु (verb, 3rd person singular present indicative active) + एव (eva) प्रत्यय (particle, "indeed")  
तद्रामकृपोपलभ्ये (tadrāmakṛpopalabhye) तद् (tad) + राम (rāma) + कृप (kṛpa) + उपलभ्य (upalabhya) + इ (i) (participial / gerundive form, locative singular masculine/neuter)  
पर्ति (parti) प्रति (prati) पूर्वस्मृति (indeclinable or noun, locative singular neuter)  
प्रियः (priyaḥ) प्रिय (priya) विशेषण (adjective, nominative singular masculine)  
अन् (an) न (na) + त (tatpuruṣa compound, negative prefix; part of अनन्त (ananta))  
अन्तगुणार्णवन्तम् (antaguṇārṇavantam) अन्त (anta) + गुण (guṇa) + आर्णव (ārṇava) + अन्त (anta) + अन्त (anta) (compound bahuvrīhi or complex composition), accusative singular masculine

(In mokṣa-locative, for one desiring liberation (mumukṣu-ablative) indeed (hi) there is (asti) a gradation (tārathamyaṃ-accusative) in the fruit (phale-locative) for the surrendered one (prapannasya-genitive) but (tu) for the true surrender (satprapatteḥ-genitive); indeed (eva) for him who obtains (upalabhye-locative) the grace of Rama (tadrām-kṛpa), there is the beloved (priyo-nominative) infinite (ananta-) ocean (guṇārṇavantam-accusative).)

For one desiring liberation, indeed, there is gradation in the fruit for the surrendered, but for the true surrender; indeed, for one who obtains the grace of Rama, the beloved infinite ocean of qualities exists.

For one seeking liberation, there is indeed gradation in the fruits attained by the surrendered, but for the true surrender; indeed, the beloved infinite ocean of qualities exists for the one who attains Rama's grace.
