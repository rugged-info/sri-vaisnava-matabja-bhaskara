=== मूलपाठः ===


श्रैताद्रौ त्वथ सिंहरूपिणसमथी श्रीधर्मपुर्या तथा  
योगानन्दमशेषदेवसुनतं श्रीकाकुले तु प्रभुम् ।  
देवेर्वन्द्यमथान्ध्रनायकमिह श्रीदं तथाऽहोबिले  
तस्मिञ् श्रीगरुडादिसजञ्ज्ञ उचिते देवं हिरण्यार्दनम् ।।१९७।।

śraitādrau tva tha siṁharūpiṇasamathī śrīdharmapuryā tathā  
yogānandamaśeṣadevasunataṁ śrīkākule tu prabhum  
devervandyamathāndhranāyakam iha śrīdaṁ tathā'ḥobile  
tasmiñ śrīgaruḍādisajañjña ucite devaṁ hiraṇyārdanam ॥197॥

श्रैताद्रौ (śraitādrau) - śraitā + drau; dual locative/vocative? (compound unclear, possibly bahuvrīhi),  
त्व (tva) - tvad, pronoun, 2nd person singular nominative/vocative, indeclinable (particle/emphasis),  
अथ (atha) - atha, indeclinable, "then", particle,  
सिंहरूपिणसमथी (siṁharūpiṇasamathī) - siṁha-rūpiṇa + samathī; bahuvrīhi compound: siṁha (lion, masc), rūpiṇa (having form/shape), samathī (calm/steady one, fem), singular nominative feminine;  
श्रीधर्मपुर्या (śrīdharmapuryā) - śrī-dharma-puryā; tatpuruṣa locative singular feminine (from śrī-dharma-pura, "glorious dharma city");  
तथा (tathā) - tathā, indeclinable, "thus", "also", particle;  

योगानन्दमशेषदेवसुनतं (yogānandamaśeṣadevasunataṁ) - yoga-ananda + maśeṣa + devasunataṁ; complex bahuvrīhi compound, masculine singular accusative; yoga-ananda (joy of yoga), maśeṣa (without remainder/complete), devasunata (dev-sunata = deity praised) "fully praised deity";  
श्रीकाकुले (śrīkākule) - śrī-kā-kule; locative singular compound, "at the family of Śrīkā", (kula = family),  
तु (tu) - tu, indeclinable, "but", particle;  
प्रभुम् (prabhum) - prabhu, noun, masculine singular accusative, "lord";  

देवेर्वन्द्यमथान्ध्रनायकमिह (devervandyamathāndhranāyakam iha) - devaḥ (masc nom sg) + vandya (adhvitiya) "worthy of worship", atha (indcl), andhraprefix nāyaka (leader) masculine singular accusative, iha (indeclinable, "here");  
श्रीदं (śrīdaṁ) - śrīda (noun, masc neuter singular accusative?), unclear; possibly "auspicious gift" or "wealth";  
तथाऽहोबिले (tathā'ḥobile) - tathā + ahabile (fem singular locative/ablative/?), compound unclear, possibly "also in Ahobilam", proper noun locative;  

तस्मिञ् (tasmiñ) - tasmin, indeclinable locative singular; "in that",  
श्रीगरुडादिसजञ्ज्ञ (śrīgaruḍādisajañjña) - śrī-garuḍādi+sajañjña; bahuvrīhi compound: śrī (auspicious) + garuḍādi (starting with Garuḍa) + sajañjña (knowing, acquainted); masculine singular locative/nominative;  
उचिते (ucite) - ucita, adjective masculine/neuter singular locative, "appropriate", "proper";  
देवं (devaṁ) - deva, noun masculine singular accusative, "god";  
हिरण्यार्दनम् (hiraṇyārdanam) - hiraṇy-ārdana; bahuvrīhi compound masculine singular accusative, "the golden-thatcher" (a name of Viṣṇu/Kṛṣṇa), "the remover of gold" (epithet);  

Literal translation:  
"From the Śrāitā twins, then the lion-shaped calm one, from the glorious city of Dharma likewise,  
the completely praised deity, the joy of yoga, at the family of Śrīkā indeed the lord,  
worshipful God and the leader of Andhra here, this auspicious one likewise in Ahobilam,  
in that, the auspicious one known from Garuḍa and others, in the appropriate place, the God Hiraṇyārdana."

Smooth English translation:  
"From the Śrāita twins, then the lion-shaped tranquil one, from the glorious city of Dharma as well,  
the fully praised deity, delight of yoga, at the Śrīkā family indeed the Lord,  
here the worshipful God, the leader of Andhra, this auspicious one also at Ahobilam,  
there the auspicious Lord, known from Garuḍa and others, in the proper place, the God Hiraṇyārdana (Vishnu)."
