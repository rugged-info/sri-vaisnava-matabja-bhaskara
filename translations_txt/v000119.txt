=== मूलपाठः ===


जितेद्धियश्चात्सरतो बुध सत्सुनिश्चितं नाम हरेरनुत्तमम् ।
अपारसंसारनिवारणक्षमं समुच्यरेदरैदिकमाचरन् सदा ।।११९।।


jiteddhiyashchātsarato budha satsuniśchitaṁ nāma hareranuttamam ।
apārasaṁsāranivāraṇakṣamaṁ samucyaredaraidhikamācaran sadā ॥119॥


जितेद्धियः (jiteddh iyaḥ), जितद्धिय (jitaddhi), tatpuruṣa (कृदन्त compound)
च (ca), indeclinable (conjunction)
आत्सरतः (ātsarataḥ), आत्सरत (ātsarata), adjective (case: nom. sg. masc.)
बुधः (budhaḥ), बुद्ध (buddha), noun (nom. sg. masc.)
सत्सु (satsu), सत् (sat), noun (case: loc. pl. neut.)
निश्चितं (niśchitaṁ), निश्चित (niśchita), adjective (acc. sg. neut.)
नाम (nāma), नाम (nāma), indeclinable (particle)
हरेर् (harer), हर (hara), noun (gen. sg. masc.)
अुत्तमम् (uttamam), उत्तम (uttama), adjective (acc. sg. neut.)
अपरसंसारनिवारणक्षमं (apārasaṁsāranivāraṇakṣamaṁ), compound (bahuvrīhi):
- अपार (apāra) adj.
- संसार (saṁsāra) noun
- निवारण (nivāraṇa) noun
- क्षम (kṣama) adj.
सम् (sam), indeclinable (prefix)
उच्य (ucya), verb root (participle)
अरेद (areda), noun (acc. pl. masc.)
ऐदिकम् (aidikam), adjective (acc. sg. neut.)
आचरण् (ācaraṇ), verb root (present participle, nom. sg. neut.)
सदा (sadā), indeclinable (adverb)


Conquering intellect and being devoted, O wise one, known as the best named of Hari,
Endowed with the capacity to utterly remove samsara, always practicing (it) as instructed by the Vedas.


O wise devotee who has conquered the intellect and is steadfast, renowned as the supreme among those of Hari,
Always engaged in the supreme Vedic practice capable of completely ending the endless cycle of samsara.
