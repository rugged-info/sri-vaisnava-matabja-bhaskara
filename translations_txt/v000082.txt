=== मूलपाठः ===


तत्र सूर्यप्रकाशदर्शनादपूर्व यद् घटीद्रयं स दण्डद्रयात्मककालोऽतिवेधः, रविप्र भासस्य  
दर्शनादनन्तरमर्थे सूर्य उदिते महावेध इति बुधैः कालवेर्तृभिरीर्यते।  
योगस्तुरीयस्तु दिवाकरोदये तेऽर्वाक्सुदोषातिशयार्थबोधकाः ।  
सर्वेऽपि वेधा मुनिभिर्विनिश्चिता निर्णेतृभिस्तस्य तु तत्त्वदर्शिभिः ।।८०।।  

tatra sūryaprakāśadarśanādapūrva yad ghaṭīdhrayaṃ sa daṇḍadrayātmakakālo'tivedhaḥ, ravipra bhāsasya  
darśanādanantaramarthe sūrya udite mahāvedha iti budhaiḥ kālavertṛbhirīryate।  
yogasturīyastu divākarodaye te'rvāksudoṣātiśayārthabodhakāḥ ।  
sarve'pi vedhā munibhirviniścitā nirṇetṛbhistasya tu tattvadarśibhiḥ ।।80।।  

तत्र (tatra) तत्र indeclinable/locative adverb

There, by (tatra) the sun-light (sūryaprakāśa) sight (darśana) having not yet occurred (adapūrva), that (yad) ghati-draya (pot-collection) (ghaṭīdhrayaṃ), he (sa), a daṇḍa-draya-atmaka-kāla (stick-collection-like time) overreacher (ativedhaḥ), the sun-like light's (ravipra bhāsasya), after vision immediately (darśanādanantaramarthe) at the time (kāla) the sun (sūrya) rose (udite), a great vedha (mahāvedha) indeed (iti), by wise men (budhaiḥ) by the time-calculators (kālavertṛbhiḥ) is counted (rīryate).  
The yoga (yogaḥ) is turīya (fourth) indeed (tu), at sunrise (divākarodaye) those (te), from defects and excess (arvāksudoṣātiśayārthabodhakāḥ), all (sarve) also (api) the vedhas (vedeha) by sages (munibhiḥ) certainly (viniścitā) determined (nirṇetṛbhiḥ), of it (tasya) by truth-seeing (tattvadarśibhiḥ).  

There (tatra), before (adapūrva) the sight of sunlight, that ghati-group is (is) an excessive vedha of daṇḍa-group formed by the light of the sun. Immediately after the sight, at the rising of the sun, it is called great vedha according to the wise time-keepers. The yoga is the fourth; at sunrise those vedhas, which know the excesses and defects, are all surely identified by sages and decision-makers who are truth-seers.  

There, before the sunlight is seen, that pot-group (ghati-draya) is an excessive overreacher of the stick-group type, by the illumination of the sun. Immediately after the sight, at the sun's rising, it is called great vedha by wise time-keepers. The yoga is the fourth; at sunrise, the vedhas that are aware of excess and defects are all certainly determined by sages and those who decide, who see the true essence.
