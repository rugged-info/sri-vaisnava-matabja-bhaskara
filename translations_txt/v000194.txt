=== मूलपाठः ===


शारं तथा गोमत एव पर्वते तथा हरिद्रार ऋजुं जगत्पतिम् ।
तीर्थं प्रयागे बत माधवाभिधं तथा गयायां तु गदाधरं परम् ।।१९४।।

śāraṃ tathā gomata eva parvate tathā haridrāra ṛjuṃ jagatpatim।
tīrthaṃ prayāge bata mādhavābhidhaṃ tathā gayāyāṃ tu gadādharaṃ param।।194।।

Literal translation:  
Arrow (acc. sg. neut.) likewise Gomata (nom. sg. m.) indeed on the mountain likewise Haridrāra (nom. sg. m.) straight (acc. sg. m.) Lord of the world (acc. sg. m.)  
Holy place (acc. sg. neut.) at Prayāga (loc. sg. m.) indeed Mādhava-named (acc. sg. neut.) likewise at Gayā (loc. sg. f.) but Gada-dhara (acc. sg. m.) supreme (acc. sg. neut.)  

Smooth English translation:  
The arrow, likewise Gomata indeed on the mountain; likewise Haridrāra, the straight Lord of the world.  
The holy place at Prayāga, likewise called Mādhava; but at Gayā, the supreme Gada-dhara.
