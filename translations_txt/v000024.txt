=== मूलपाठः ===


बन्धुत्वप्रतिपत्तिश्च भासमाना विचारतः ।।२४।।

bandhutvapratipattiśca bhāsamānā vicārataḥ ।।24।।

बन्धुत्वप्रतिपत्तिश्च (bandhutvapratipattiśca):  
- bandhutva, bandhutva (noun, neuter, nominative singular, compound base meaning "brotherhood")  
- pratipatti, pratipatti (noun, feminine, nominative singular, compound base meaning "comprehension, understanding")  
- ca (indeclinable, conjunction, "and")  
compound type: tatpuruṣa (bandhutva-pratipatti) + ca (particle)

भासमाना (bhāsamānā):  
- bhāsamāna (participle, present active participle of the root bhāṣ, verb class 1, masc./neut./fem. nominative singular)  
grammatical note: here nominative singular feminine agreeing with vicārataḥ (or vocative root to be clarified)

विचारतः (vicārataḥ):  
- vicāra, √vicṛ (to think, reflect)  
- vicārataḥ (present active participle, nominative singular masculine or neuter)  
grammatical note: masculine/neuter nominative singular present active participle

Literal translation:  
"Brotherhood-comprehension-and shining thinking." (Here, "bandhutva-pratipattiḥ ca" = "and the comprehension of brotherhood," "bhāsamānāḥ vicārataḥ" = "shining while thinking")

Smooth English translation:  
"And the understanding of brotherhood shines forth in reasoning."
