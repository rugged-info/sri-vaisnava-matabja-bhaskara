=== मूलपाठः ===


प्राप्यं मिथुनमेवेति श्रीमते पदतो मतम् ।
रामचन्द्रेति पठतः स्वामित्वं प्रतिपाद्यते ।।४०।।

prāpyaṃ mithunameveti śrīmate padato matam ।
rāmacandreti paṭhataḥ svāmitvaṃ pratipādyate ।।40॥

प्राप्यं (prāpyaṃ), प्राप्त (prāpta), verbal adjective, accusative singular neuter  
मिथुनम् (mithunam), मिथुन (mithuna), noun, accusative singular neuter  
एव (eva), particle, indeclinable  
एति (eti), इति (iti), indeclinable; quotation particle  
श्रीमते (śrīmate), श्रीमān (śrīmat), adjective, dative singular masculine  
पदतः (padataḥ), पद (pada), noun, genitive singular masculine  
मतम् (matam), मत (mata), noun/participle, nominative singular neuter  
रामचन्द्रेति (rāmacandreti), रामचन्द्र (rāmacandra) + इति (iti), noun + advocative/quotation particle  
पठतः (paṭhataḥ), पठ (paṭha), present participle, genitive singular masculine  
स्वामित्वं (svāmitvaṃ), स्वामित्व (svāmitva), noun, accusative singular neuter  
प्रतिपाद्यते (pratipādyate), present passive indicative, 3rd person singular, verb  

“prāpyaṃ” (having obtained, acc. neuter sing.)  
“mithunam” (a pair/couple, acc. neuter sing.)  
“eva” (indeed)  
“eti” (thus says)  
“śrīmate” (to the illustrious, dat. sing. m.)  
“padataḥ” (of the foot-step, gen. sing. m.)  
“matam” (opinion/view, nom. sing. n.)  
“rāmacandreti” (says Rama Chandra, proper noun + quot. part.)  
“paṭhataḥ” (of the reader, gen. sing. m.)  
“svāmitvaṃ” (lordship/ownership, acc. sing. n.)  
“pratipādyate” (is established/expounded, 3rd sing. pass.)  

Having obtained the pair indeed, thus is the opinion to the illustrious from the footstep.  
Reading “Rāmacandra” (the name Rama Chandra), lordship is expounded.

Upon having indeed obtained the pair, this is the opinion attributed to the illustrious by the footstep.  
Reading the name Rama Chandra, lordship is explained.
