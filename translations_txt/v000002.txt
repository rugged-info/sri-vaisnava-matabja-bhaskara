=== मूलपाठः ===


प्रकृत पद्म में शार्दूलविक्रीडित छन्द है तथा रूपक अलंकार हे।
पशव्य यदपाङ्गसंश्रयमिदं भोग्यं दिगीशैर्जग-
चित्रं चाखिलमद्श्वतं शुभगुणा वात्सल्यसीमा च या।
विदुयुत्पुञ्चसमानकान्तिरमितक्षान्तिः सुपद्येक्षणा
दत्तात्नेऽखिलसमग्पदो जनकजा रामप्रिया साऽनिशम् ।।२।।

prakṛta padma meṃ śārdūlavikrīḍita chanda hai tathā rūpaka alaṃkāra he।
paśavya yadapāṅgasaṃśrayamidaṃ bhogyaṃ digīśairjag-
citraṃ cākhilamadśvataṃ śubhaguṇā vātsalyasīmā ca yā।

In the natural lotus (of creation) there is a meter played by a tiger and a figure of speech called rūpaka (drama).  
This enjoyable refuge without limbs (?), related to cattle, is wondrous and complete, adorned with auspicious qualities and the limit of parental affection.  
She who has a splendor like lightning’s rays, immeasurable patience, and excellent vision,  
Given as soul (essence) and completely united, born of Janaka, beloved of Rama, she is always present.

In the natural lotus, there exists a meter played by a tiger and the ornament of drama.  
This delightful refuge, associated with creatures, endowed with auspicious qualities and the boundaries of affection,  
Who shines like lightning’s rays, possessed of boundless patience and keen insight,  
Given as the entire unity, born of Janaka, beloved of Rama, she is always there.
