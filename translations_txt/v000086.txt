=== मूलपाठः ===


त्रिस्प्शा, पक्षवर्द्धिनी, जया, विजया, जयन्ती तथा पापनाशिनी। यह भेद पुराणों में वर्णित हे।

trispshā, pakṣavarddhīnī, jayā, vijayā, jayantī tathā pāpanāśinī। yaha bheda purāṇoṁ meṁ varṇita he।

त्रिस्प्शा (trispshā), त्रिस्प्श (trispsh, root word) (feminine, singular, nominative, tatpuruṣa compound);  
पक्षवर्द्धिनी (pakṣavarddhīnī), पक्ष (pakṣa, noun, masculine) + वर्द्धिनी (varddhīnī, feminine, singular, feminine agentive suffix), (feminine, singular, nominative, tatpuruṣa compound);  
जया (jayā), जया (jayā, noun, feminine, singular, nominative);  
विजया (vijayā), विजया (vijayā, noun, feminine, singular, nominative);  
जयन्ती (jayantī), जयन्ती (jayantī, noun, feminine, singular, nominative);  
तथा (tathā), (indeclinable, adverb, means "also, and");  
पापनाशिनी (pāpanāśinī), पाप (pāpa, noun, masculine) + नाशिनी (nāśinī, feminine agentive suffix), (feminine, singular, nominative, tatpuruṣa compound);  
यह (yaha), (demonstrative pronoun, masculine, singular, nominative);  
भेद (bheda), (noun, masculine, singular, nominative);  
पुराणों (purāṇoṁ), पुराण (purāṇa, noun, masculine, plural, ablative or locative plural);  
में (meṁ), (indeclinable postposition, meaning "in");  
वर्णित (varṇita), वर्णित (varṇita, past passive participle of वर्त (vart), "described");  
हे (he), indeclinable particle, vocative or emphatic

The trispshā, the pakṣavarddhīnī, the jayā, the vijayā, the jayantī and also the pāpanāśinī—this differentiation (such as these) is described in the Purāṇas.

The trispshā, the wing-increasing one, the victorious (jayā), the victorious (vijayā), the victorious (jayantī), and the destroyer of sins (pāpanāśinī)—this distinction is described in the Purāṇas.
